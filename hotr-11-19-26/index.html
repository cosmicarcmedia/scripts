3
Who tells you how much mold you have?
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
Most homeowners have never heard how a mold job is supposed to work. Laying out the consultant, protocol and clearance steps from the company's own process shows a practice that a franchise rarely explains, without naming one.
Here is a question to ask any company that finds mold in your house. Who is going to inspect it, and who is going to get paid to remove it? When you contact us, we recommend a list of local Texas state licensed mold consultants. The consultant inspects the house and writes a protocol. Then we give you a bid according to that protocol. We perform the remediation according to the protocol. And we guarantee clearance results. When clearance is complete, we give you a Certificate of Mold Damage Remediation. You can see every step of it on paper. We also hold four Texas state mold remediation contractor licenses on staff. So ask the next company the same things. Who wrote the plan? What does the bid follow? What do I get at the end, and what does it say? If somebody cannot answer, that is worth knowing before they open a wall. Mold can come from water damage and flooding, high humidity, hidden leaks in a roof or pipes, and condensation on windows and walls. Meanwhile, if you suspect mold, do not clean it yourself. Cleaning can spread the spores. Turn off your HVAC, keep the area closed off, and take photos. Photos help with your insurance too.
4
The first hour after you find water
The useful one
▾
A short checklist anybody can follow at midnight, taken straight from the company's own water damage page. It earns the follow from every homeowner in the audience and costs nothing to give away.
If you find water in your house, here is what to do in the first hour. It can come from an appliance leak, a flood, a storm, a broken pipe or a sewage backup. Even damage that looks minor can point to something much larger. Call a restoration company at the first sign of water damage. Remove what water you can with towels or a mop. Move your furniture, or put something waterproof under the legs. Do not run any appliance in the wet area. Then take photos and video before anybody cleans anything. Photos matter for your insurance claim. Insurance usually helps with sudden damage, like a burst pipe or a storm. It gets harder when the damage came from a leak that went ignored for a long time. When in doubt, call your insurance company and have them walk you through your policy. The next thing that matters is drying. Excess moisture leads to mold, so drying is the step you cannot skip. We use industrial grade blowers and dehumidifiers to pull the moisture out quickly. And we keep accurate paperwork on the whole job. That paperwork is what gets a claim accepted quickly and covered fairly. We handle the water, and we keep the paper trail clean. We take emergency calls 24 hours a day, so do not wait until morning.
