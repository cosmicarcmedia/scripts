1
My dad started this shop in the little building across the street
The origin and the face
▾
Henry Gray built this shop in two buildings on Pulliam Street and three generations now work in it. Nathan telling it in his own voice puts a family face on a shop with no video of its own.
Spot A
My dad, Henry Gray, met my mom in Los Angeles. They built a family out there, and then they came home to her hometown, San Angelo. Dad started Gray's Transmissions in a little building right across the street from this one. Then this building came available, and he added on to it. [OWNER: the year Henry opened the first building] That's how Gray's got started.
Spot B
My brother Travis is our mechanic. He remembers Dad bringing us to the shop back in junior high, showing us what he did. By high school, Travis was taking transmissions out and putting them back together. [OWNER: how Cedrick came into the shop] Dad is semi retired now, and my brothers and I run it. I'm Nathan, and I run the office and manage the shop. When you call, I'm the one who answers the phone. When you pull in, I'm out there to meet you.
Spot C
My mom, Sandra, is the vice president. She joined the Chamber of Commerce and goes to the meetings she can, and to the events at the San Angelo Museum of Fine Arts. She says that's what you do to take part in a town. We have three generations working here now. [OWNER: what your dad still does around the shop] We put one line on the website because it says it best. We are here when you need us.

3
A repair shop that wants you out of the shop
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
The shop's own Specials page says it wants to keep customers out of the shop as much as possible. Few repair businesses say that out loud, and it frames the diagnosis, the options and the guarantee fairly without naming anyone.
Spot C
You'd expect a repair shop to want you back every week. So it may surprise you that we put this on our own website. We want to keep our customers out of the shop as much as possible. And here's our advice for doing that. Do your factory recommended maintenance. Don't put off a repair that can turn into a bigger one. Check your tire pressure, and fill it up when it's low. Combine your errands so you drive fewer miles.
Spot A
When you do need us, here's how we work. A customer named George wrote that we diagnosed his van, told him the best option, and he agreed to it. That's the whole process. We find the problem, we tell you the options, and you decide. We give free next day estimates, and we stand behind our work with a guarantee. [OWNER: what the guarantee covers and for how long]
Spot B
Another customer, Nowell, wrote that we priced better than anyone else. We also take five percent off qualifying transmission service. We set reasonable rates, we manage our time well every day, and that's how we save people money. So if a shop ever tells you it wants to see you more often, ask them why. Call us at 325 655 6614, or schedule online on our website.
4
Four questions to ask any transmission shop
The useful one
▾
The shop already promotes a blog post on what to ask a transmission shop. This puts it in the owner's face and voice, costs nothing to give away, and every answer comes from how the shop says it works.
Spot A
Here are four questions to ask any transmission shop before they touch your car. Number one. What is wrong, and how did you find out? A good shop diagnoses the problem first, and it uses real diagnostic equipment to do it. Number two. What are my options? There's often more than one, and the shop should tell you which one it would pick.
Spot B
Number three. What does it cost before you start? Ask for an estimate and keep it. Ours are free, and we give them the next day. Number four. Is the work guaranteed? Ask what the guarantee covers, and for how long. [OWNER: what you tell customers about your guarantee] One more thing, because it saves people money. A transmission service means pulling the pan, draining the fluid, and replacing the filter. Done on time, it helps prevent bigger problems later.
Spot C
Today's cars are full of electronics, and electronics affect how a transmission works. That's why we go to a lot of seminars to keep learning. A well maintained transmission can improve your fuel efficiency and reduce wear on other parts of your vehicle. Small problems turn into expensive repairs when they wait. So if your car is shifting strange, call us. Tell us what you're feeling. We'll tell you what we'd check, and whether it can wait.
