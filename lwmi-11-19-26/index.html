4
The spring tune-up that saves a July breakdown
Useful customer education
▾
A yearly tune-up is the one thing every homeowner can do before the heat arrives, and Carrier's own pages back it up. The shop offers tune-ups, so the script ends on the phone number.
The best time to call a heating and air company is before you need one. Carrier says to have your air conditioner tuned up at least once a year. And the best time is spring, before the peak of summer. That's the dealer we are, so we'll say the same thing. A tune-up catches worn parts and small refrigerant leaks early. It covers things like the filters, the coils and the condensate drain. It covers the thermostat, the electrical connections and the airflow. Carrier's own checklist runs twelve points, down to oiling the motors and fans and testing the safety switches. A tune-up is where a small problem gets found early. Before the first hot week, call and put yours on the calendar. It beats a Saturday night breakdown. Skip the tune-ups, and Carrier says you often end up with higher energy bills, weaker cooling and frequent breakdowns. In West Texas, the breakdown always comes on the hottest day. Between visits, change the filter often. Carrier says every thirty to ninety days, and more often with pets or allergies. Filters are the easy part. You can do that yourself.
