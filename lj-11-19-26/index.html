2
The least likely place for a pearl
Local anchor
▾
The Concho pearl grows in the rivers and lakes around San Angelo, and the owner already tells the story on camera. It gives the shop a reason to be remembered that no chain store can copy.
Most people figure a pearl comes from the ocean. Ours come from the rivers and lakes around San Angelo. Freshwater Tampico mussels, in a corner of West Texas where water is hard to come by. Something gets inside the mussel, and the mussel coats it in a shiny layer called nacre. Layer after layer, and that becomes a pearl. They come out pink and purple and lavender. Natural colors. They aren't white. Some are round. Plenty are baroque, which means shaped however the mussel made them. To me, a Concho pearl is a gemstone found in the least likely place it should be. I'd say there aren't five places on earth that grow a pearl as good. A big, nearly round one is exceedingly rare. [OWNER: the best Concho pearl you've held, and what made it special] Texas stopped issuing new licenses to harvest the mussel shells, and I don't expect that to change soon. Protecting the mussel is how there's still a pearl for the next generation. We build jewelry around them, and we inlay mother of pearl into metal. The shop sits at 18 East Concho Avenue. Upstairs is Miss Hattie's Bordello Museum. A river pearl on the ground floor, a bordello museum upstairs. Downtown San Angelo has some stories. Come in and ask to see a Concho pearl up close.

4
Grandma's ring doesn't have to stay in the drawer
Useful customer education
▾
Heirloom redesign is a service the shop offers and most people never ask about. The script tells them what to bring, and it ends on a customer who wore her mother's bridal set again.
You've got a ring that belonged to somebody you love, and it's sitting in a drawer. Nobody wears it. It's worn out, or it isn't your style. There's a name for fixing that. It's called an heirloom redesign, and yes, it can be done. Bring everything to your appointment. The old jewelry, any paperwork that came with it, your gold, your diamonds. Somebody on our team cleans it, inspects it and measures it. Then they tell you straight about any limits. Some pieces can go a long way. Some have limits, and you should hear about them up front. Come with a rough budget in mind, and an open mind. You get an estimate on the piece. If it fits, we go. If it doesn't, we say so. If you have a design in mind, bring a picture. If you don't, our custom design team will help you find one. One customer had her mother's bridal set reset into a ring she wears every day. She wrote that it brought her to tears. Rings, necklaces, bracelets, earrings and pins. Gold or silver or platinum, whatever suits the piece. Pearl strands get restrung and broken clasps get fixed. Call us at the shop and ask.
