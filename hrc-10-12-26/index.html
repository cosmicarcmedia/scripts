2
Look up when you drive around San Angelo
The local anchor
▾
The site names four San Angelo buildings the crews have roofed, and the National Weather Service logged tennis ball hail over town in May 2020. Together they give a roofing company a reason to talk about the town itself, with no competitor in sight.
Spot B
Next time you drive around San Angelo, look up. You'll pass roofs we've put on. The San Angelo Museum of Fine Arts. The Visitors Center. The Foster Communications Coliseum. Central High School. Plus restaurants, hotels, shopping centers, schools, and a lot of homes. When the job is a school or a city building, our crew works around the building's hours, so the day goes on as usual.

West Texas is hard on a roof. On May 21, 2020, one storm sat over San Angelo for about ninety minutes. The National Weather Service logged hail from golf ball size up to tennis ball size. Those two notes are what I think about when I drive around town. We've served San Angelo and West Texas since 1928. Today we also work across San Antonio and the Texas Hill Country. 3
If we can restore your roof, we won't sell you a new one
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
Restore before replace is the sentence a roofing sales crew has the least reason to say, and it is printed on the company's own commercial page. The deductible line adds a second fair question with the Texas law behind it.
Spot C
If we can restore your roof, we will not sell you a new one. Most roofers won't say that out loud. It's on our website, and it's how we handle commercial roofs. An inspector looks at the roof and tells you whether maintenance can extend its life. Then you get a comprehensive report after every inspection. We would rather save you the money than sell you a roof you don't need yet. And if the roof is finished, we'll tell you that too.
Spot A
Second thing is preventative maintenance. A regular inspection program finds the small problem before it turns into a big one. That saves you money. We don't sell more service than a building needs. When you do need a new roof, our installers make sure the job is done right the first time. We can put the roof on new construction too.
Spot B
Third thing, for anybody filing an insurance claim after a storm. Texas law says you pay your deductible. A roofer who expects to be paid from your claim isn't allowed to let you skip it. So if somebody knocks on your door after hail and says the deductible is covered, they're on the wrong side of that law. We work closely with your insurance company, and we'll tell you the same thing up front. Ask any roofer about all three.
4
Pick your roof like you expect hail
The useful one
▾
Every material fact here comes off the company's own roofing pages, from shingle lifespan to the hail warranty on stone coated steel. It gives a homeowner something to use on any estimate, and it shows the showroom and the technician visit.
Spot A
If you're replacing a roof in West Texas, plan for hail. Here's what I'd tell you about the main choices. Composition shingles are the economical one. Fiberglass mat, asphalt, and crushed rock on top. A typical one lasts fifteen to thirty years. We carry Owens Corning, Tamko, GAF, and CertainTeed.
Spot B
Metal gives you strength at a fraction of the weight, and it reflects heat. It also comes in a wide range of colors and profiles. Tile, concrete or clay, is highly resistant to fire, hail, and moisture. It's meant to last as long as the building does, and if one tile breaks you replace that tile. Decra is stone coated steel. Every Decra installation comes with a hail stone penetration warranty. DaVinci makes synthetic tiles that look like slate or shake. They're impact resistant, and they come with a fifty year limited warranty. [OWNER: the roof type you'd put on your own house, and why]
Spot C
Not sure which one fits? Come look at our showroom. You can browse colors, roof types, and styles from the vendor books and photographs. And after a storm, don't judge your roof from the driveway. Damage from extreme weather isn't easy to spot with an untrained eye. Call us at three two five, six five three, six seven eight six, and we'll send a certified technician to evaluate it.
