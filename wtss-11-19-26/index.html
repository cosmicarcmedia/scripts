1
My dad started this place in 1983
The origin and the face
▾
A family steel yard that has been on the same road since 1983 has a story no big-box store can copy, and Steve is the face of it. The script keeps to what the About page says and leaves the family's current history in Steve's own hands.
My dad, Paul English, started this business in 1983. He'd spent several years in the steel industry first. Then he opened up to sell steel to the oilfield, oil and gas lines, construction crews, and the farm and ranch folks around San Angelo. [OWNER: what your dad did in the steel business before he opened, in your words] We've grown a lot since then. We're a large, varied steel supply company now. But it's still owned and operated by the family, and we still try to run it with the work ethic and the values Dad raised us with. [OWNER: one thing your dad taught you about dealing with customers] I'm Steve English, owner and president here. There are three generations of the family on the staff list now, and I'd say that matters when you're buying steel. Come meet our friendly, knowledgeable staff, ready to help you. We've got four people in sales, a warehouse crew, an office, and our own drivers on the delivery trucks. When you call the counter, you reach people who work with steel every day. A lot of the people who walk in here have been doing it for years. Welders, ranchers, contractors, oilfield crews. We call many of them friends, and we're glad to. If you need steel and you want to deal with somebody who knows your name, come see us at 6617 South Highway 277. We're open Monday through Friday, and Saturday morning too.
2
Find us a half mile down 277, past the Eldorado exit
The local anchor
▾
The site gives driving directions down to the frontage road and the shortcut off Brodnax Lane. Reading them out loud makes the video useful to anyone who has driven past the yard and never turned in, and it names the roads the customers use.
If you've never been out to our place, here's how to get there. We're at 6617 South Highway 277. From town, head south on Bryant, which is also US 87 and Loop 306. Take the South 277 Eldorado exit. We're a little more than a half mile down, on the right. If you're coming from the Eden and Wall side on South 87, there's a shortcut. Turn left on Brodnax Lane and follow the frontage road over to 277. Cross the median and take a left. We're about a half mile on the right. [OWNER: the landmark customers use when they give directions to your yard] You can also take the Eldorado exit that merges onto the frontage road, then Christoval Road. Cross the bridge, turn left at the stop sign, and follow the frontage road to Grand Canal Road. Short left, short right, and you're back on 277. When you pull in, the sales office is right in the front center of the building. Come on in. We're open Monday through Friday, 8 to 5, and Saturday 9 to noon. We're closed Sunday. If you get turned around, call 325 651 7322 and we'll talk you in. Our warehouse is full of steel, and there are people at the counter who'll help you find what you need. 3
A website can't cut your pipe to length
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
Cutting, consulting and delivery are all listed on the shop's own products page, and each one is something an online order form does not do. The script stays on what the shop offers and leaves the other sellers unnamed.
You can order steel online now. Plenty of folks do. But think about what happens when you need three different pieces of tubing for a gate, and you're unsure of the size. On a website, you guess. At our counter, we ask what you're building. We offer project consultation, so somebody who knows steel looks at your job with you. We also do material cutting. Tell us the length, and we'll cut it before you take it home. That saves you a trip to a saw, and it saves you a lot of waste. [OWNER: the cut-to-length job you do most often] We have our own delivery trucks, too, and we deliver all over the area, depending on the size of the order. If you want to know whether your load qualifies, call us at 325 651 7322. We've got four people in sales, a warehouse crew, and office folks who answer the phone. Monday through Friday, 8 to 5, and Saturday morning. Our website lists pipe, tubing, angle, beam, rebar and more. We stock galvanized livestock panels, culverts, expanded metal and bar grating, and R and U panel with a color chart if you're building a metal building. Call us for availability and pricing, because those change. A box on a screen won't tell you that.
4
How to ask for steel so you only buy it once
The useful one
▾
Most people walking into a steel yard do not know the vocabulary, and a few questions saves them a second trip. It draws on the shop's own product list and gives away something the sales office tells people all day.
If you've never bought steel before, here's how to do it right the first time. First, know what the job is. A gate, a trailer, a cattle pen, a shop shelf, they all call for something different. Tell us what it has to carry or hold. Second, bring measurements. How long, how wide, how many pieces. We cut material, so you can leave with it already sized. Third, ask about the pipe. You'll hear the numbers S10, S40 and S80. Those are the pipe schedules, and the bigger the number, the thicker the wall. Thicker usually costs more, so pick the one your job needs. [OWNER: the pipe size you sell the most of] Fourth, ask about the finish. We carry painted square tubing and black square tubing, and galvanized livestock panels for pens, plus poly hay rings made right here in Texas. The right one depends on whether it stays outside. Putting up a metal building? We carry R and U panel in a range of colors, plus trim, screws, insulation and walk doors. Last, tell us whether you need it delivered. We deliver around the area, depending on the size of the order. Call 325 651 7322 for availability and pricing. Bring your list, and we'll help you get all of it.
