1
I bought one lube shop and asked a lot of questions
The origin and the face
▾
Feliciano's own SBDC story is a first person arc from buying one lube center to running two and an express wash, and nobody in town has heard him tell it. It gives a face to a name on the sign.
Spot A
My name is Feliciano Hernandez. Priscilla and I own Shine Brite Lube Center and Car Wash here in San Angelo. I bought this business from the previous owner. [OWNER: the year you bought it, and what you did before] I walked in with one lube center and a lot of questions. How do I hire people. How do I manage them. How do I set prices.
Spot B
So I went to the Small Business Development Center at Angelo State. We worked through hiring, managing a team, and pricing. After that I hired more people, and the business grew. In 2022 we opened a second lube center on North Bryant. Then we took the car wash on Sherwood Way and rebuilt it as an express wash. That one was a labor of love, and it took us a long time to get open.
Spot C
In 2024 San Angelo voted us best lube center in the Community's Choice Awards. Our Sherwood Way shop has also been a CARFAX Top Rated Service Center five years in a row. That comes from customer reviews. Our technicians have a lot of years behind them, and customers keep coming back. Our goal is the fastest service and the best quality oil change and car wash in San Angelo. [OWNER: one line on what you want a customer to feel when they pull in] Oil change, wash, vacuum, done. Two lube centers and an express wash, and you never need an appointment.
2
The old KFC on North Bryant now changes oil
The local anchor
▾
The north side shop sits in a building San Angelo LIVE reported was a KFC and other businesses, empty for some time. Everybody who drives North Bryant remembers something about that corner, and it opens the video on a place the viewer already knows.
Spot B
Our north side shop is on North Bryant Boulevard, in a building that used to be a KFC. It sat empty for a while. Now it's a lube center, and you can drive in without an appointment. [OWNER: what the corner looked like when you first saw it] Our other shop is on Sherwood Way, with the express wash right next door.
Spot C
Driving in West Texas means dust, pollen, and bugs on the windshield. So we built the wash around that. We pre-bug your vehicle before it goes through. We prep it, we towel dry it, and the vacuums are free. Towels and spray bottles are right there at the vacuums.
Spot A
If you get an oil change at either shop, you get a free wash with it. At Sherwood Way that's a free silver wash. At North Bryant you get a coupon for the wash on Sherwood Way. Seniors, students, and military get ten percent off an oil change, and Wednesday is Ladies Day. Both lube centers are first come, first served, so drive in any time. Got a fleet of five or more cars? Ask us about fleet prices. Two addresses, forty two thirty two Sherwood Way, and twenty three oh two North Bryant. Come see whichever one is closer to you.
3
Your owner's manual says when to change your oil. Ask your shop if they agree.
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
The site says Shine Brite recommends following the manufacturer's interval and that a wash member can cancel at the wash any time. Those are two printed policies a chain built on volume has little reason to advertise.
Spot C
Four questions to ask any oil change shop. Question one. How often should I change my oil? Our website answers it. We recommend you follow your vehicle manufacturer's recommendation. The number that matters is in your owner's manual. That goes for the synthetic blend, the full synthetic, and the diesel oil change. Pick the oil your engine calls for.
Spot A
Second question is about car wash memberships. How do I cancel? Ours is billed monthly, and your license plate is your ticket into the wash. You can cancel at the wash any time. Silver is fourteen dollars a wash or twenty eight a month. Do two washes a month and you've covered it. Unlimited is for one vehicle. Commercial and ride share vehicles don't qualify for it, but we do have a fleet program for them.
Spot B
Third question. Do I need an appointment? Not at either shop. Drive in, and it's first come, first served. Fourth question. What's free? The vacuums at our wash are free. So are the towels and spray bottles. With an oil change you get a multi point maintenance check and a free wash. It's all on our website. Ask whoever you use now for the same list, and compare it with ours.
4
Four small things to check before your car feels off
The useful one
▾
Wiper blades, cabin filters, and air filters are the add ons customers never hear explained. Every number comes off the shop's own service page, and it costs them nothing to give away.
Spot A
Four small parts that make a car feel better, and they're easy to forget. First, your wiper blades. Over time they get streaky or stop clearing the glass. They matter in rain and they matter in dust. New blades fix that, and we replace them at either shop.
Spot B
Second, your cabin filter. That's the filter for the air inside the car. It traps dust, pollen, and bacteria. Most manufacturers say to replace it every twelve thousand to fifteen thousand miles. But with Texas pollen, you might want it done more often. We can do it as often as you like. Our technicians handle all four of these right along with the oil change.
Spot C
Third, your air filter. It keeps clean air flowing to the engine, which helps performance and fuel efficiency. Fourth, your fuel filter. It takes dirt and contaminants out of the fuel. A clogged one can cost you performance and efficiency. Check what your manufacturer says about when to replace it. None of these are big jobs. You can have them done during an oil change at either of our shops, and every oil change comes with a multi point maintenance check. Drive in and ask what's due on any of the four, at either shop.
