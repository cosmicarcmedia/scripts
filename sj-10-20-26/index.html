The scripts

A first pass. I made some assumptions about your business to write these. On a shoot we'd dial them in to your goals.

1
I started this at nineteen with three things on the menu
The origin and the face
▾
Kurt was nineteen when he launched a trailer with a three item menu, and KTAB just put him and his mom on TV. That is a face and a story a stranger can follow in under a minute, and it gives the new audience a reason to root for him.
Spot A
I started Sunset Jr. when I was nineteen years old. It was a food trailer, and the menu had three things on it. A hamburger, a Texas cheesesteak, and fries. That was January of 2022. I'm Kurt Klausner. I'm from San Angelo, and I played offensive line for the Central Bobcats.
Spot B
My parents owned Sunset Grill, and my mom Pam says our family has been in the restaurant business since the 1960s. As a teenager, I cooked burgers for my friends during football games, and I kept playing with flavors. A kitchen is a kind of dance. You need technique, timing, and teamwork. Some of what I learned on the football field carried over. [OWNER: one thing from football that you use in the kitchen every day] The Hawaiian cheesesteak started with a pizza. I love Hawaiian barbecue chicken pizza, so I put those same things on a hoagie. Grilled chicken, thick cut bacon, jalapenos, pineapple, and barbecue sauce.
Spot C
In the spring of 2023, the trailer grew into a restaurant at 502 North Van Buren. The trailer didn't go away. It still works around San Angelo, for lunches, dinners, and events. Now we do burgers, cheesesteaks, sliders, and gyros. My dad Troy and my brother in law Oscar are in the kitchen with me. [OWNER: what your mom Pam does at the restaurant] We open at eleven, Monday through Saturday, and we close at eight. Come say hi.
2
From a bar patio to the corner of Van Buren
The local anchor
▾
A trailer on a patio that became a building on Van Buren is a path locals can picture, and the trailer is still out working. This script ties the restaurant to its corner, its trailer and its weekday specials so people know when to stop in.
Spot B
Before we had a building, we had a trailer on a bar patio. In the spring of 2023, that trailer was parked at the Fiddlestrings patio. We were waiting on a restaurant to be finished at the corner of Van Buren and the Houston Harte frontage road. That's 502 North Van Buren. We planned to open it around April first, with a ribbon cutting that day. [OWNER: what you remember about opening day]
Spot A
The trailer still works, too. That trailer is where it all started, and we still use it. It does lunches, dinners, and events around San Angelo. If you've got an event coming up, catering and special events are on our website. I'm a San Angelo native, and I played for the Central Bobcats.
Spot C
Every weekday has a special, so you always have a reason to stop in. Monday is a gyro, with grilled lamb, spinach, tomato, and tzatziki on warm pita. Tuesday is Burger Tuesday, a hand made burger with fries. Wednesday, it's a chopped cheese, with a chopped beef patty, cheddar, and Sunset Sauce on a hoagie roll. Thursday is buffalo ranch chicken sliders, with sidewinder fries. Friday is a Texas cheesesteak, with shaved ribeye, grilled onions, and jalapenos, and fries on the side. Come by 502 North Van Buren, Monday through Saturday, eleven to eight. [OWNER: your own favorite day on the specials menu, and why]
3
Who is making your burger? Ask the next place.
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
The owner, his dad and his brother in law are the ones in the kitchen, and the patties, the ribeye and the sauces are made in house. That is the one thing a chain cannot say, and it comes straight from the menu and the KTAB story. No competitor is named.
Spot C
Next time you order a burger, ask one question. Who's making it? Here, it's me, my dad Troy, and my brother in law Oscar. My mom Pam is part of this too. The owner is in the kitchen.
Spot A
Every patty is hand made and pressed, from fresh ground beef, on a brioche bun. The ribeye on your cheesesteak is shaved in house. The Texas cheesesteak comes with grilled onions, jalapenos, and provolone on a toasted hoagie. The ranch, the honey mustard, the Sunset Sauce, and the tzatziki are all house made. [OWNER: whether you hand first timers sauce samples, and which sauce people pick] I care about two things. Quality ingredients, and the same food every time. The Jr Burger is the smaller one, with grilled onions, baby spinach, pickles, and tomato. It's a smaller patty, made by hand like the big ones.
Spot B
When I started, the menu had three items, on purpose. A hamburger, a Texas cheesesteak, and fries. It was simple, and I wanted to do it well. Now there are sliders, gyros, chicken, and funnel cake fries. Our customers keep telling us about those funnel cake fries. It's funnel cake batter shaped like fries, with powdered sugar on top. Our sidewinder fries are thick cut and seasoned. Come by 502 North Van Buren, and say hi to the people making your food.
4
How to read our menu when you're counting calories or protein
The useful one
▾
Calories and protein are printed on most of the menu, and few customers know to use them. Showing how costs nothing, helps anyone who is training or watching their day, and gives people a reason to follow.
Spot A
Our menu has calories and protein printed on most of the items. Here's how you can use that. The numbers are in the item descriptions when you order online. The Jr Burger is 480 calories and 29 grams of protein. The cheeseburger is 710 calories and 52 grams of protein. If you want to skip the bun, we have bunless salads over fresh baby spinach.
Spot B
The chicken cheesesteak salad is 400 calories and 47 grams of protein. The ribeye cheesesteak salad is 530 calories and 39 grams of protein. If you want a sandwich, the Hawaiian cheesesteak is 900 calories and 78 grams of protein. The BBQ bacon burger is 950 calories and 72 grams. If you like gyros, the chicken gyro is 550 calories and 65 grams of protein. The classic lamb gyro is 540 calories and 26 grams.
Spot C
Most of our sandwiches come without a side. So what you add is up to you. The sidewinder fries are 280 calories. The tater tots are 260. The kids' meals come with fries and a bottle of water. Look at the numbers before you order, then pick what fits your day. You can order online, or come by 502 North Van Buren. We'll see you there.
One more idea
The sauce taste test

Line up the ranch, buffalo, honey mustard and Sunset Sauce, and let one person taste each on camera. Open on the question of which one wins, then show the reaction. Scott P.'s review gives you the line to build around.
