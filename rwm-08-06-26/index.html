14Grape Creek, older than San Angelo2 beats
A · tailgate · shortbeat 1 of 5
Grape Creek is older than San Angelo. By almost ten years.
A · tailgate · longbeat 4 of 5
Back in 1858, the Butterfield stage line won the contract to carry mail clear from St. Louis to San Francisco. Twenty eight hundred miles. About twenty five days, twice a week, both directions. And one of the stations on that entire run sat right here at Grape Creek. A shelter, a corral, a hot meal, and fresh mules. The one reporter riding that very first stage west wrote that Grape Creek was a fine stream, near some fine timber. That is 1858. Fort Concho did not exist. San Angelo did not exist. They showed up nine years later.
15Carlsbad, not the one in New Mexico2 beats
A · tailgate · shortbeat 1 of 5
Everybody figures Carlsbad, Texas got its name from Carlsbad, New Mexico. Nope.
A · tailgate · longbeat 4 of 5
In 1907 a land company bought the old sixty thousand acre Hughes Ranch, laid out a townsite, built a hotel, and named the whole thing Hughes. Then somebody drilled a deep well and the water came up loaded with minerals. So in 1908 the town renamed itself Karlsbad, with a K, after a famous health spa over in Europe, and started selling itself as a resort. A bathhouse. Health tourists. People paid good money to come out here and drink the water. Then in 1912 the state opened the first tuberculosis hospital in Texas right down the road. Fifty seven beds. And because this is Texas, they opened a hospital with a barbecue.
16Water Valley, six names in four years2 beats
A · tailgate · shortbeat 1 of 5
Before this place was called Water Valley, it burned through five other names in about four years.
A · tailgate · longbeat 4 of 5
Then around 1889, two fellas named Phelin and Glynn dammed up the North Concho, dug an irrigation ditch, and brought water right down into the valley. Farms came. English settlers came. By 1914 there was a general store, a cotton gin, and two cotton buyers, which out here made it practically a metropolis. And the name? They looked at the water. They looked at the valley. Water Valley. Nailed it, boys. Sixth try.
