3
Before you sign with any internet provider, ask to see the broadband label
Competitor contrast
▾
VGI's own September 21 post already tells people to ask for the Broadband Consumer Label and compare price, fees, data limits and contract terms, so this script turns that post into the one thing a shopper can use on any provider, VGI included.
Spot C
Internet plans shouldn't come with surprises. So before you sign with any provider, ask to see the broadband label. It's the FCC's Broadband Facts label, and providers are supposed to show it to you at the point of sale. It puts five things in one place, including any introductory price and when it ends. The monthly price. The speeds. Any additional fees. Data limits. And contract terms.
Spot A
Start with the monthly price, then check the fees on top of it. Check the speeds, because plans vary by location. Check whether there's a data limit. On our plans, there are no data caps. Then read the contract terms, including what it costs to leave. Our terms of service are on our website, cancellation included. You can cancel in writing, and an email to our billing address works.
Spot B
In our online ordering, you'll find a View Broadband Facts link on the plans. Ask every provider you're considering for the same label, and put them side by side. The point is to make providers easy to compare. We want you to know exactly what you're paying for. If you can't find the label, ask for it. Real people in your community answer our phone. If you have questions, call our local team at 325-223-3000.
4
How to restart your internet the safe way
Useful customer education
▾
VGI's own support page already walks customers through a safe restart and what to tell support, so giving it away on camera costs nothing and puts the company in front of anyone whose internet just dropped.
Spot A
When your internet drops, start with a safe restart. Unplug the power from your router or gateway. That's the box that connects your home to the internet. Wait at least thirty seconds. Plug it back in, and give it several minutes to come back. Leave the little recessed reset button alone, unless your provider tells you to press it.
Spot B
If it's still down, look at the lights on your equipment. Notice which ones are on. Then check whether every device is offline or just one. That tells your provider whether the trouble is in your home or somewhere else on the network.
Spot C
Before you call, you can check our network status page for known outages. You'll find it, and our help desk, under Support on our website. You can run a speed test to measure your current download and upload speeds, and you can open a support ticket on our website for a documented response. When you call, have your account name, service address, or invoice number handy. Our internet tech support line is 325-223-3014, Monday through Friday, eight to five. After hours, our on-call line is 325-413-2550. That's weekdays from five to ten at night, and weekends from eight in the morning to ten at night. I'm Scott Gibbs at VGI Technology.
