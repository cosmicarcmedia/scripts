1
I started a steel building company when I was twenty-four
The origin and the face
▾
A steel building is a big check to hand a name on a website, and nothing public yet tells a stranger that Jacob grew up in Ballinger between construction sites and ranches, or that his customers already call him Jake. This puts the About page story on camera in his own voice.
Spot A
I started a steel building company when I was twenty-four. Yes, that sounds young. But by then I'd spent years on construction sites and ranches. I'm Jacob Huston, and my customers call me Jake. I grew up in Ballinger, splitting my time between construction sites with my family and working on local ranches.
Spot B
Ballinger is where I learned to work. Faith and family came with it, and so did a love of being outdoors. Building was half of what I knew, and ranch work was the other half. So at twenty-four I put them together and started Valley Creek Construction. That was more than ten years ago. Today we go by Valley Creek Steel Buildings, same company. We put up barns, shops, equipment sheds and barndominiums, and we do metal roofing. We're members of the Metal Building Contractors and Erectors Association.
Spot C
We're a small, family-run company. My wife Brittany and I have three kids, and we're active in our church. When I'm not on a job site, the family's outdoors, camping or hunting. One customer wrote that I didn't flinch when he threw changes at me halfway through his shop. I'll take that. I believe a positive job site gives you a better experience as the customer, so I treat my crew the way I'd want to be treated. If you're picturing a building, call me and tell me about it.
2
If you lived in San Angelo in May of 2020, you remember the hail
The local anchor
▾
The May 2020 hailstorm was the worst San Angelo had seen since 1995, and metal roofing and hail damage repair are on the company's service list. Opening on it tells the Concho Valley this video is about their roofs and their buildings.
Spot B
If you lived in San Angelo in May of 2020, you remember the hail. A Thursday night. Golf ball size, and some of it tennis ball size. It went on for about three hours. People called it the worst hailstorm this city had seen since 1995. It damaged home roofs and car windshields across town.
Spot A
That's the weather every roof and every building out here has to be ready for. Heat, high wind, hail and heavy rain. Every building we put up is designed to meet or exceed the local code and the wind load requirements for San Angelo. A metal roof can last fifty years or more with very little upkeep. It also reflects heat, which helps with the cooling bill in a San Angelo summer.
Spot C
Metal roofing is one of our main services. Standing seam, re-roofs, recover roofs and hail damage repair. We roof houses, barns, shops and commercial buildings. One customer had a rental house with a garage, and we put a new metal roof on both. A crew of three, two days. The owner wrote that the crew showed up on time both days and stayed on task. We work in San Angelo, across Tom Green County, and in the towns around it.
3
Before you hire a steel building company, ask who pulls the permit
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
Permits, a crew on site every day, in house sheet metal and a walked site are real practices from the company's own pages and reviews. Laid out as questions a buyer can ask any steel building company, they draw a fair line without naming anyone.
Spot C
Before you hire a steel building company, ask who pulls the permit. Sounds like a small question. But it tells you who's running your job. With us, the answer is we do. From the permit to final completion, we handle every step. On a barndominium that includes the plumbing and the electrical, which we coordinate with trusted local trades.
Spot A
Next, ask who'll be on your property every day. One customer wrote that we were there on time every day. We worked around the weather so we wouldn't track up his property. He also went over the welds himself and found zero incomplete ones. He went looking, and I'm fine with that.
Spot B
Third, who makes the trim and the flashing? We do our own sheet metal work, in house. Trim, flashing, wall and roof panels, soffits, gutters and downspouts. When a building needs a custom piece, we make it here, which means faster turnaround and a tighter fit. Last one: will somebody walk my site before I get a number? With us, somebody does. The quote is free, and we schedule a site visit. If a builder gets vague on any of those, you've learned something before you signed. Ask me the same four questions. I'll answer them standing on your site.
4
How much for a shop? It depends on a handful of choices
The useful one
▾
The company's own FAQ lists the choices that shape a steel shop's price and schedule. Putting them on camera answers how much for a shop and gives a buyer something to bring to the first call.
Spot A
How much for a shop? That's the first question I get. The number moves with a handful of choices, and they're all yours. How big you want it. How tall the walls are. How many doors and windows. Whether it's insulated. How steep the roof is. We talk through all of them on your first call.
Spot B
A recent one of ours was a forty by forty with fourteen foot walls. Two lean-tos, a ten by forty and a twenty by forty. The roof is a three in twelve pitch, with ash grey walls and a galvalume roof. Every one of those is a choice, and changing one changes the price. We can handle the site prep, the slab and the finish-out too.
Spot C
A basic shop might take a few weeks. A big turnkey barndominium can take several months. Size, customization and where we are on the schedule decide which. If you need financing, we've partnered with First Financial Bank of Texas. You can apply online and pre-qualify before you ever call me. Some calls are small, too. One customer wanted to add a chicken coop onto his carport. We gave him advice, and he bought the sheet metal from us to finish it himself. Call me, and we'll walk the site together.
