1
Nobody walks in here on a good day
The origin and the face
▾
A customer review names the owner stopping to help a stranger in a parking lot, then driving her to work while her car was fixed. That story is the face this shop is missing, and it sets up the trust a customer needs before handing over a car.
Spot C
We've been locally owned and operated in San Angelo since 1966. Nearly six decades of fixing cars for our neighbors. Nobody walks in here on a good day. Something happened to your car, and you're already worried. Our job is to make that part easy. [OWNER: who runs the shop today and how long you have been here]
Spot A
Here's how I think about it. One morning I saw a lady struggling with her car in a parking lot. I went over and helped her. I told her to bring it by the shop the next day. When she did, we made sure she got to work while we fixed it. [OWNER: confirm this story is yours to tell, and add the part she left out]
Spot B
That's how we try to work every day. Customers tell us we keep them updated. Some say weekly, some say twice a week. One of our guys, Hayden, stayed late the day before Thanksgiving so a customer could pick up his car and have it for work the next morning. We welcome walk-ins, no appointment needed, because damage doesn't happen on a schedule. Customers say the front desk is friendly and helpful, and I'd say so too. We paint with PPG. And every repair we do carries a lifetime warranty. Come by 3003 South Bryant, walk-ins are welcome.
2
Hail, deer, and the mesquite trees of San Angelo
The local anchor
▾
Hail and deer damage are the two repairs that customers name in their own reviews, and the long wait after last year's hail storm is an honest detail only this shop can own. It also names the small towns around San Angelo, which tells them this video is for them.
Spot A
In this part of Texas, a car gets hurt one of two ways. Hail, or a deer. Hail season here runs through spring and early summer. After the big storm last year, we were covered up with work. Some customers waited months to get in, and some of them told us so. We know it was a long wait, and we didn't like it either.
Spot C
Then there are the deer. One customer brought in deer damage and was told three months. Brandon got it in the next week. After the deer repair, we fixed the hail damage from the May before. The customer said it looked like a brand new car. [OWNER: how many deer jobs you see in a normal fall, and what the worst one looked like]
Spot B
San Angelo has wide streets lined with mesquite trees, and a lot of people who are proud of their trucks. We fix cars and trucks from all over this part of the state. Grape Creek, Christoval, Mertzon, Eldorado, Sonora, Ballinger, all the way out to Ozona. If you broke it out there, we can fix it here. Walk in and you'll get a free estimate, no obligation. We're at 3003 South Bryant, and we've been there a long time.
3
Your insurance company gave you a shop. You don't have to use it.
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
The right to choose any licensed shop is stated on the company's own insurance page, along with direct billing and supplemental estimates. This script turns it into the thing a stressed customer is not told while the adjuster is on the phone, and names no one.
Spot B
After a wreck, your insurance company will probably hand you a name. A shop they like to send people to. Here's what they may not mention. You don't have to use it. You can choose any licensed repair facility you want. We work with all the major insurers, no matter who they recommend.
Spot C
We also handle the paperwork. We write the estimate and send it straight to your insurance company. If we find hidden damage once the car is apart, we send them a supplement, and you don't have to chase anyone. One customer said we walked them through the insurance process and sent weekly updates on the repair. We bill them directly, so all you pay is your deductible.
Spot A
And every repair we do has a lifetime warranty. Our Enterprise rental desk is right next door, so you drop off your car and pick up a rental on the same visit. We're not always the quickest place in town. One customer said you may find someone cheaper, or get in sooner somewhere else. But he said he's used us for years and gets fair prices and good work. That's the kind of customer we want. Choose your shop, and ask all of them what their warranty covers.
4
What to do after a hailstorm hits your car
The useful one
▾
The paintless dent repair details, the claim timing and the cracked paint exception all come from the shop's own FAQ and hail page. Hail is the loudest repair in West Texas, so a plain explanation here reaches every driver with dents.
Spot C
If your car just got hit by hail, here's what to do first. Take pictures before you touch anything. Get the roof, the hood and the trunk, from a few angles. The biggest mistake people make is waiting too long to file the claim. Paint can deteriorate, and you can miss your insurance window. So start the claim, then call us.
Spot A
Here's how most hail damage gets fixed. If the paint isn't cracked, we can use paintless dent repair. We get behind the panel, and push each dent out with special rods. No sanding, no filler, no repainting. You keep your original factory paint, which helps your resale value. Most insurance carriers prefer this method for hail, because it's faster and costs less than repainting.
Spot B
Now, not every dent qualifies. If the paint is cracked or chipped, or the dent is sharp or deep, it needs regular bodywork and a new coat of paint. We'll tell you which one you've got after we look at the car. Most hail jobs take between three and seven days. That depends on how many dents there are. Bring it by for a dent count and a damage assessment. The estimate is free, and walk-ins are welcome. We'll go from there.
