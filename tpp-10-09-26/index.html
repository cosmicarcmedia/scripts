Spot B
I'm George Norwood with The Pooch Pros, here in San Angelo. Angela and I train dogs, and I tell people about Lobo on purpose. [OWNER: what finally showed you that yelling was the wrong way]. Spot C
Most of the dogs we meet aren't bad dogs. Some are scared. Some are anxious. Some have an owner who was never shown how. That was me with Lobo. So the person we spend the most time training is you. You'll likely have that dog for ten years or more. We want those ten years to be good ones, for both of you. If your dog is a handful, you can book a free phone consultation on our website. Three questions to ask any dog trainer first

Trust, respect, and communication. A dog that feels safe is eager to learn. One family getting ready for a new baby called us practical, realistic, kind and effective. 4
Is it anxiety or bad behavior? Check for these
The useful one
▾
A free checklist from the specialist. Dog owners search this exact question late at night, and a short list they can screenshot earns the follow.
Spot A
Is your dog anxious, or just badly behaved? From the couch they can look exactly alike. I'm George Norwood, and here are the signs I tell owners to watch for. Pacing. Barking or whining a lot. Trembling. Watch your own dog tonight and see how many you count.
Spot B
Hiding. Chewing things up when you're gone. Panting or drooling when the dog hasn't been running. If you see a few of these, your dog is probably worried. And a worried dog needs a different plan than a stubborn one. Harsh correction is the wrong tool for a worried dog. Gentle and consistent is the way through.
Spot C
Next, find the trigger. Is it the keys in your hand? The doorbell? You walking out? Write down what happens right before it starts. That note is the most useful thing you can bring to a trainer. [OWNER: the first thing you do with an anxious dog on a first visit]. And before a consultation, keep the house calm. Have the dog at home, and keep things quiet. If it's more than you can handle alone, you can book a free phone consultation on our website. We'd be glad to talk it through. The earlier you call, the easier it is on everybody.
