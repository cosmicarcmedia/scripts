4
Three signs your pool filter needs fresh sand
The useful one
▾
It costs nothing to give away and comes straight from your own sand change and cartridge filter pages, so the person who saves it already knows who to call.
Spot A
If your pool water stays cloudy no matter how much you clean, look at your filter. Filter sand doesn't last forever. Over time the grains wear smooth and the water starts to channel through them. Then they stop trapping debris. Most sand filters need fresh sand every five to seven years.
Spot B
Three signs it's time. One. Your water stays cloudy. Two. The pressure on your filter climbs fast. Three. You're backwashing constantly. If you see any of those, it's usually time for a change.
Spot C
When we do a sand change, we pull out the old sand. We inspect and flush the tank. We check the laterals and the standpipe for damage. Then we add fresh pool-grade sand, backwash, rinse, and pressure check the whole system. Fresh sand restores the filter to like-new performance. Some pools run a cartridge filter instead. Cartridges trap everything that makes your water cloudy. Once they pack with oils, dirt, and debris, the flow drops and your pump works overtime. A garden hose can't reach between the pleats, so a proper pressure wash goes deep. Worn or torn pleats mean it's time to replace it. We'll tell you honestly which one you're looking at. Call or text, and we'll walk you through it.
