1
We are The Attic, a fun and trendy boutique on South Main
The origin and the face
▾
The shop has no published owner story, so this one introduces the shop in its own words and numbers: a fun and trendy boutique, a small business that thanks its customers, and a customer review that says why shoppers keep stepping in. The owner slots are where the owner steps in.
Spot A
We are The Attic, a fun and trendy boutique in Winters, Texas. You will find us at 333 South Main Street. [OWNER: the year the shop opened] [OWNER: who started it, and what you did before] We carry clothing, gifts, home decor, baby things, and a little something for the guys. Find us on Facebook as theatticwinters, and on Instagram as theattic_boutique. We post new looks almost every day.
Spot B
We are a small business, and we say thank you to everyone who supports us. Our Facebook page has about sixty four hundred followers. Ninety six percent of the fifty one people who reviewed us there recommend the store. One customer wrote that The Attic is a wonderful store with helpful staff, and that they always enjoy stepping in to see what is new. [OWNER: the story behind the name The Attic]
Spot C
Here is some of what is on the shelves. Jon Hart Design bags and accessories. Katie Loxton bracelets and sunglasses. Natalie Wood Design jewelry, and The Darling Effect. For babies, Copper Pearl, Magnetic Me and MudPie Baby. SWIG drinkware, and Glory Haus for the home. Shinesty and GameGuard for the guys. Jon Hart Design has its own section on the website, and its own highlight on our Instagram. The website has a New Arrivals section, and a Christmas 2026 collection is already up. Come in and take your time. [OWNER: the first thing a new customer usually picks up]

National online boutiques are a click away. None of them has a phone that rings on South Main Street in Winters. One customer ordered a Jon Hart bag on our website and hit an error. So they called the store. They wrote that the staff were quick to answer and fixed it easily, and the bag arrived within a few days. Our number is 325 754 5555.

A gift for every budget, with real prices
The useful one
▾
Gift shoppers want to know what they can get for how much. The shop's own site has real prices from about eleven dollars up, and a review mentions a baby shower gift, so this gives a quick guide that costs nothing and sends people to the shelves.

Need a gift, and want to start with the price? Around eleven dollars, there is a quote pen set. Twelve dollars gets you a mini acrylic picture frame. Fifteen, a floral dot scarf. Eighteen, a ceramic mug. Thirty two, a mini tempered glass cutting board. Those are real prices from our website right now.

Going to a baby shower? One customer shopping for one wrote about our great selection of clothing, bags and so much more. We carry Copper Pearl, Magnetic Me, MudPie Baby, Warmies and Dreambaby. Hosting a dinner? There is a dining and hosting section, and gourmet food and drinks. Buying for him? There is a men's section, with Shinesty and GameGuard. If you cannot get to Main Street, shop online. Orders are processed in one to three business days. Standard shipping is eight fifty, three to five business days. Shipping is free over a hundred and twenty dollars. Returns are within thirty days, unworn, with the tags on. Sale items, accessories and gift cards are final sale, so check before you buy. Refunds go back to the original form of payment, and you should allow five days. [OWNER: your most popular gift under twenty dollars]
