2
What an inch of rain does to your roof
The local anchor
▾
Nobody pictures their roof shedding a thousand gallons or more in one storm. Putting the number next to San Angelo's own rainfall shows the homeowner why gutters matter, and the numbers come from NOAA and Texas A&M with no sales pitch attached.
Spot C
San Angelo averages about twenty one inches of rain a year. That doesn't sound like much until you see where it goes. For every inch of rain, a roof sheds about six tenths of a gallon per square foot. So a two thousand square foot roof sends around twelve hundred gallons through your gutters from one inch of rain. Twelve hundred gallons. Every inch.
Spot A
All of that water has to go somewhere, and where it goes is the whole job. Your downspouts are there to drain it away from your foundation. If the gutter is full, or the downspout dumps right beside the house, that water ends up at your foundation. The problems a gutter system is there to prevent are mold growth, structural damage, and poor air quality inside the house. Don't wait for the damage to become evident.
Spot B
Our weather isn't gentle on gutters either. We get heavy rain and strong wind, and we build for the San Angelo climate. We repair storm damage too. Our gutters are made right at your house, cut to your home's exact dimensions. We take the measurements, cut and join the gutters, and add the outlets and downspouts. Fewer seams means fewer places for a leak to start. We work in San Angelo and Big Spring, and the quote is free.
3
Ask who's still going to be here
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
A gutter that lasts twenty to thirty years needs a company that will last that long too. Every point below comes from what the site and the reviews already say, and it lets Chuck lay the questions out without naming anyone.
Spot B
Before you hire a gutter company, ask who owns it. Then ask whether that person is the one who ends up in your yard. Ours is the same two people since 1991, Chuck and Melissa Hardgrave. When something needs fixing, nobody has to track down whose job it is. It is ours. Ask how the gutter gets made. Ours is made on site to your home's exact dimensions, so it fits your house. Ask whether they can work on your roof. We install gutters on virtually any type of roof, after we look at the design and recommend what fits.
Spot C
Ask what the warranty covers. Ours covers materials and workmanship, and we guarantee that our work will last. Call us and we'll walk you through the specific terms. Ask whether they do commercial buildings or just homes. We do both. Ask what the quote costs. Ours is free, and there is no obligation. Ask what other people say. We're at 4.7 out of 5 on more than fifty reviews, across Google, Facebook, Angi, and HomeAdvisor.
Spot A
And ask how long they have been around. A seamless gutter can last twenty to thirty years. We've been doing this for thirty five. One customer told us they have used us for about thirty years. Pick a company that has been around long enough to stand behind that.
4
How often to clean your gutters
The useful one
▾
A plain schedule, spring and fall, with the reason behind it. It costs the shop nothing to give away and puts leaf guards and rain chains in front of people who are already thinking about their gutters.
Spot A
Clean your gutters at least twice a year. A gutter is there to carry rain off your roof and away from your foundation. Spring and fall is the usual schedule. If you have a lot of trees around the house, clean them more often than that. Leaves, twigs, and other debris end up in the gutter, and the gutter stops doing its job.
Spot B
Here's what that costs you. Water that cannot run down the gutter goes somewhere else. That can mean mold, foundation damage, and erosion around your landscaping. A clean gutter and a downspout that drains away from the house handle most of it.
Spot C
If you would rather clean them less, leaf guards are the answer. They are covers that go over the gutter and keep out leaves and twigs. They suit a house with a lot of trees, and they cut down how often you clean. And if you like how it looks, a rain chain can stand in for a downspout. They come in copper, aluminum, and stainless steel. We install all of it in San Angelo and Big Spring, gutters, guards, and rain chains. With proper maintenance, a seamless gutter can last twenty to thirty years, and this is the maintenance. Every spring and every fall. Put it on the calendar.
