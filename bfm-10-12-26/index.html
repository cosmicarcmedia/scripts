1
A Maytag store on East Concho in 1942
The origin and the face
▾
Most furniture shoppers see a big store on Sherwood Way and have no idea it began as one appliance store downtown. Marshall, P.J. and Rodney give the person on camera a family to stand in front of, and eighty-four years is a number no new store can match.
Spot A
In 1942, a man named Marshall Bratton opened an appliance store in downtown San Angelo. It was called Maytag Appliance Company. It sat at 108 East Concho, in historic downtown. That was eighty-four years ago. Three generations of Brattons have worked in this business since. I'm [OWNER: your first name], and I'm [OWNER: which generation, and how you're related to Marshall].
Spot B
Marshall's son P.J. joined the company in the 1950s. In the 1960s, P.J.'s son Rodney joined too. Rodney moved the business to a bigger spot around the corner, at 222 South Oakes. That's when TVs and stereos started going in next to the appliances. [OWNER: one story about Marshall, P.J., or Rodney that the family still tells] Three generations, one family name on the door.

Today Brattons is an old fashioned, family owned furniture store. We're a full service furniture company, with a huge selection. Marshall started with appliances, and appliances are still on our floor. Whether you want a dining room set, bedroom furniture, a television, or a laptop, we have it. We carry mattresses too. We sell high quality furniture. And we have payment options to fit every family's needs. Yes, it started with one Maytag store. Now you can furnish a house here. We're at 2626 Sherwood Way. 2
Four stores, one family, and an eight-track tape
The local anchor
▾
A local anchor with real street names gives a San Angelo shopper a reason to say I remember that. East Concho, South Oakes, the Village Shopping Center, Sound Box and Sherwood Way are all from the family's own company history, and the eight-track tapes make it fun to watch.
Spot B
Brattons has had four stores in San Angelo over the years. The first one started downtown on East Concho. Then it moved around the corner, to a bigger spot on South Oakes. The second was Appliance Mart, in the Village Shopping Center. The third was Sound Box, in 1970. And the fourth is the one we're in now, on Sherwood Way. All four were Brattons.
Spot C
Sound Box was a home and car audio store. It sold stereos, TVs, LP albums, and eight-track tapes. Yes, eight-tracks. We sold those. We'd install the stereo in your house, or in your car. If you grew up in San Angelo, you might remember it. [OWNER: a memory of Sound Box or one of the other stores]

Then TV and Appliance World opened at the place we're in now, 2626 Sherwood Way. Today it's furniture, mattresses, appliances, and electronics. Dining room sets. Bedroom furniture. Televisions. Laptops. Marshall's first store sold appliances. Ours still do. When you walk in, you're walking into a store that started on East Concho in 1942. That's three generations of one family selling to one town. We have payment options to fit every family's needs. If you remember any of them, tell us when you come in. Come see us at 2626 Sherwood Way, or call 325 942 1678.
3
Before you sign for no interest on a couch, ask these three things
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
This is the one built to get forwarded. Store financing is where furniture buyers get hurt, and the owner is the one who can say plainly how the store's own payment options work. It names no store, and every number comes from the CFPB and Bankrate.
Spot C
Before you sign for a payment plan on furniture, ask what kind of no interest it is. Some are called deferred interest. That means interest has been building since the day you bought it. If you don't pay off the full balance by the deadline, you owe all of it. Even if there's only a small balance left. The regular rate on those plans is often around twenty-five percent.
Spot A
So ask the store three things. What's the last day to pay it off? What happens if I'm late, or if I come up short? What's the interest rate if I don't make it? Then do the math. Divide the total by the number of months. Two thousand dollars over twelve months is about a hundred sixty-seven a month. That's the least you should pay, even if the statement says less. Set up autopay, so one late payment doesn't cancel the offer. Keep other charges off the card if you can. Ask for the terms in writing. Aim to pay it off a few months early. Check your balance near the end, so you can adjust.
Spot B
We have payment options to fit every family's needs. [OWNER: explain how your payment options work, in plain words, so a customer knows the total before they sign] Ask us the same three questions. We'll answer them. We're at 2626 Sherwood Way, and our number is 325 942 1678.
4
Pick a mattress by how you sleep
The useful one
▾
A mattress shopper stands in a store full of beds and has no idea what to look for. The firmness numbers here come from the Sleep Foundation, they cost nothing to give away, and they put the store in front of a buyer who is ready to lie down and decide.
Spot B
Here's how to pick a mattress by how you sleep. Firmness runs on a scale of one to ten. If you sleep on your side, you usually want about a four to a six. Back sleepers usually want a six or a seven. Stomach sleepers usually want a seven or an eight. That's a starting point.
Spot A
Your weight changes it. A mattress feels firmer if you weigh under a hundred thirty pounds. It feels softer if you weigh over two hundred thirty. So when you're in the store, lie down the way you sleep at home. Don't just sit on the edge. Try more than one, side by side. A salesperson can help you narrow it down if you're unsure what you want. If you share a bed, bring the person you share it with. Pick one that suits you both. Ask what the return terms are before you buy.
Spot C
Give it time, too. It takes about a month to adjust to a new mattress. Foam mattresses soften over that first month. So one night on one doesn't tell you much. Ask about the warranty. Anything shorter than ten years is worth a second look. Come try ours at 2626 Sherwood Way. Our number is 325 942 1678.
