Three questions to ask a roofer after hail

4
How to check your roof for hail damage
The useful one
▾
Everyone in the Concho Valley has stood in the yard after a storm wondering if the roof took a hit. Giving them a safe five minute check from the ground earns trust before they ever call.
Spot A
The storm just passed, and you're wondering if your roof took a hit. Hail comes through fast, and the damage is easy to miss from the street. You can check from the ground, and you don't need to climb up there. Walk the outside of your house. Look at the gutters, the downspouts, and the vents on the roof. Those are soft metal, so hail leaves dents you can see.
Spot B
Check the metal flashing too, and the cover on your air conditioner. Dents on those mean the shingles may have taken hits as well. Look at the ground under your downspouts. Little bits of grit from the shingles collecting there can be a sign. [OWNER: what you look for first when you get up on a damaged roof] Take photos of everything with your phone, and take them close up and from a distance. Don't climb on a wet or steep roof to check it yourself. Leave the climbing to a roofer.
Spot C
Then call your insurance company and ask how long you have to file a claim. Check your policy for that number. Photos help whoever looks at your claim later. And call a roofer you trust to take a look. If your roof has been damaged by recent hail or storms, our inspection is free, and we'll tell you what we find. [OWNER: how soon after a storm people usually call, and how soon you can get out there] I'm Blake Duncan with Premiere Re-Builders in San Angelo.
