Myda and Tiffany, a Conversation

No script and no prep. These are the questions patients ask you both every week, so answer the way you would in the room. Whoever the question is aimed at goes first, then the other jumps in, agrees, pushes back, or adds a story.

Skip anything that doesn't spark, and chase anything that does. The best part of the roundtable was the back and forth.

A few ground rules for the edit.

Leave out specific doses, lab numbers, and prices. If one slips out, we cut it.

If testosterone for women comes up, mention that it's off label.

No "this brand works better than that one" comparisons.

  1. 01What's the question you get in almost every first visit, and what's the honest answer you give?
  2. 02What's a myth you each still hear every single week?
  1. 03"I lost the weight and it all came back." Why does that happen, and is it a willpower problem?
  2. 04Somebody walks in already asking for a GLP-1 by name. What has to be true before you'd start them?
  3. 05How do you lose weight on one of these medications without losing a bunch of muscle with it?
  4. 06A guy has low testosterone and he's carrying extra weight. Which one do you fix first? Myda, do you agree?
  1. 07"My labs came back normal and I still feel terrible." What does normal even mean on a lab report?
  2. 08Why are so many tired people low on iron when their regular blood count looks fine?
  3. 09Can a man raise his testosterone without medication? When do you tell him he's tried long enough?
  4. 10Most guys think testosterone is muscle and sex drive. What else does it do that nobody talks about?
  1. 11Tiffany, you say hot flashes usually aren't the worst part. What shows up first that nobody connects to hormones?
  2. 12She's tired, her cycle's off, her hair's thinning. How do you two sort out thyroid, iron, or perimenopause starting early?
  3. 13Myda, you tell women to learn their normal cycle first. What do most women get wrong about what normal is?
  1. 14The same patient with the same fatigue complaint walks into each of your rooms. What do you each check first, and where do you disagree?
  2. 15Your brother or your best friend has been "just thinking about it" for two years. What do you tell him?
  3. 16What's one thing someone watching could do this week, no appointment needed?
