The scripts

A first pass. I made some assumptions about your business to write these. On a shoot we'd dial them in to your goals.

1
Why we renamed a salon that was already twenty years old
The origin and the face
▾
Most people in San Angelo still know this place by its old name, and the owners have never told the story of why they changed it. Telling it in their own voice puts a face on the new name.
Spot A
If you've lived in San Angelo a while, you probably remember this salon as Beautiful People. It was part of this town for more than twenty years. But in 2022, Ronnie and I changed the name. It's a fair question, why rename a salon with twenty years behind it. So here's why we did. [OWNER: the year you took over and what made you want this salon]
Spot B
Beautiful People had a good name, and a good reputation. Welcoming. Great stylists. A place people trusted with their hair. We wanted to keep every bit of that. We also wanted clients and stylists of all ages to feel like this place was theirs. So it became Firefly, and the idea that comes with the name is simple. Be the light. [OWNER: what Firefly means to you, in your own words]
Spot C
Everything we do comes back to three words. Experience. Education. Relationships. That goes for the people in the chair, and it goes for the people standing behind it. There are more than nineteen providers under this roof now, and they are what make this business. So we invest in them, with training and services that make their work easier. We're updating the salon, inside and out, too. It hasn't been updated since 2003, so it's a big project. There's some mess. We're working on it. Come by, say hello, and watch the change happen.
2
Our building hasn't been updated since 2003
The local anchor
▾
The About page already admits the building is mid remodel. Saying so on camera, with the street address, turns the mess into a reason to follow along and tells the town what else happens here.
Spot B
The last time anyone updated this salon, the year was 2003. That's twenty three years ago. So yes, we know. We're working on it. Right now the outside and the inside of our building on Freeland Avenue are both getting a makeover, and the plan is vibrant, colorful, and fun. [OWNER: the first thing that changed, or the one coming next] If you drive past and see a mess, that's why.
Spot A
Here's what's good about this address. It's easy to find, and there's plenty of parking. We're open Tuesday through Saturday, nine to six. Providers set their own hours, so text 325 657 0738 and we'll tell you who has time. Or book online and see openings as soon as today. Tanning takes walk ins. We have three beds, one stand up and two lay down.
Spot C
We do more than hair here. We host kids' birthday parties. Manicures or hair styles, a craft to take home, a goody bag, and a sash and tiara for the birthday princess. Our back to school event is called Fresh Cuts of Firefly, and it goes full nineties. Five dollars off kids' cuts, twenty dollar blowouts for teens, and a photo booth for the first day of school. So if you haven't been in since this place was Beautiful People, come see what's changing. Freeland Avenue, Tuesday through Saturday.
3
Ask who your stylist works for
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
Every provider here is independent and the Google reviews name stylists by first name. That gives the owners a fair, unnamed contrast with any chain, built only on what their own site and reviews say.
Spot C
Who does your stylist work for? Most people never ask a salon that. At Firefly, every provider is independent and self employed. They run their own business inside our building. You're their client, it's their book, and it's their name on the line. When your color comes out right, that stylist has earned the next visit. [OWNER: what happens when a client isn't happy with a service]
Spot A
Read our Google reviews and you'll see names. Sara Ashley. Cayenne. Jessica. Lela. People write about the person who did their hair. A big chain has one logo to protect. We have more than nineteen names to protect. And we don't tell them how to run their day. We give them the space, the training, and the marketing, and they take it from there.
Spot B
Then there's the price. It's on the page before you book. Online booking shows what a service costs up front, with no hidden fees and no surprises. Even tanning. Ten dollars to walk in. Sixty five for ten visits. Forty a month for unlimited. No pressure, no upselling. That's written on our website. One reviewer wrote that our staff is honest with the services offered. Birthday party packages are priced on the page too, starting at five hundred dollars. So when you're picking a salon, ask who the stylist works for, and ask what it costs before you sit down.
4
How to book a salon appointment when you don't know what to call it
The useful one
▾
It teaches the thing new clients are nervous about and walks through the site's own first visit offer, including the rule that it must be claimed before booking.
Spot A
You don't have to know what to call it to book a salon appointment. That's where a lot of people stall. You open the menu and see balayage, head spa, lash lift, and you freeze. So here's what to do. Pick the closest thing on the list. Your provider adjusts it when you sit down. That's how we do it, and it's right there on our website.
Spot B
If you'd rather talk first, text us at 325 657 0738. Tell us what you're after and we'll help you pick. Now, if it's your first visit, here's a tip worth knowing. First time guests get a free add on with any service. A hydrating treatment, a brow tint upgrade, deep conditioning, a mini scalp massage, or a lip treatment. You have to claim it before you book. Call, text, or fill out the first visit form and mention it. If you book straight through the online system, the add on doesn't apply.
Spot C
When you book, you'll get a confirmation text or email right away. You'll walk into a calm space, and your provider will recommend the add on that goes with your service. Here's the one thing I wish every new client told us up front. [OWNER: the one thing you wish every new client told you before they sit down] If your idea changes once you sit down, that's fine. Your provider can adjust during the appointment. Our first visit promise is no pressure and no upselling, and that's on our website too.
