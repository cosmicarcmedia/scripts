4
Found a scorpion in your house? Check four things before you call anybody
The useful one
▾
Scorpions come up in the reviews, and the company's own info page already lists what a homeowner can do about them. Giving that away earns the follow, and the call.
Spot A
Found a scorpion in your house? Texas has eighteen species of them. When it gets hot, they go looking for somewhere cooler, and that can be your house. Before you call anybody, check four things. First, your lights. All insects are attracted to light, and that includes a porch light or a window with no curtain. If you need lights outside, use motion lights.
Spot B
Second, the gaps. Keep your windows closed at night. Caulk the trim around your eaves. Put steel wool in your weep holes. Trim any tree or shrub that touches the house. Third, firewood. Don't bring it inside until you're ready to burn it. Those last two have a name, integrated pest management. It means controlling pests by means other than insecticide.
Spot C
Fourth, your schedule. Pests are around in winter too. Scorpions breed in the fall and spring, and the young ones are easier to kill, so treating then cuts the numbers you see in summer. The treatment also stays stable in cold weather, so it's still working when pests come out in spring. After we treat, wait a week or two before you water your lawn. It helps the service work. And tell your tech where the problem area is. They'll focus on it.
