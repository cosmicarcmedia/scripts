1
We didn't invent steak
The origin and the face
▾
A steakhouse tagline is only as good as the person standing behind it. This puts the owner's face on the line and introduces both Beefmaster locations in about a minute.
We didn't invent steak. We just mastered it. That's the line on our website, and every cook in this kitchen has to live up to it. [OWNER: your name and what you did before you opened Beefmaster] [OWNER: the year the Ballinger steakhouse opened and why you picked North Broadway] A steak has been a steak for a long time. Nobody here pretends to have a secret. You cook it right, you put it on a hot plate, and you do the same thing for the next table. That's the whole job, and it takes a lot of practice. There are two Beefmasters now. One at 1608 North Broadway in Ballinger, and one on North Main in Weatherford. Same name on the door, same kind of food coming out of the kitchen. Our tables fill up with locals, and with people passing through who needed a real meal. We cook breakfast, hamburgers made from 100 percent beef, hearty sandwiches, and seafood. Then there's the steak people ask for most, our KC steak. We run lunch specials during the week, and we cater, so the same cooking can show up at your party. The website says we serve Ballinger, Weatherford, San Angelo, Abilene, and beyond. If the road brought you our way and you're hungry, you're welcome here. We've got a salad bar, a buffet, and a menu that changes by the day. If you've driven past us a hundred times, stop in.
2
A week of dinners on North Broadway
The local anchor
▾
The menu changes by the day, and almost nobody outside the regulars knows the whole week. Laying it out day by day gives locals a reason to pick a night and gives travelers a reason to plan the drive.
Here's a week at Beefmaster, 1608 North Broadway in Ballinger. Wednesday is all you can eat tacos, enchiladas, and tamales. Thursday is the barbecue lunch buffet, and then all you can eat steak for dinner. Friday is all you can eat fish, lunch and dinner. Saturday the K.C. style sirloin comes out set up for two. That's two pounds of sirloin, grilled zucchini, your choice of potato, and endless soup or salad. Saturday morning there's a breakfast buffet too, and the lunch buffet that day is all you can eat steak. Sunday morning is the breakfast buffet. Scrambled eggs, bacon, sausage, ham, pancakes, French toast, eggs Benedict, chorizo, country potatoes, and cinnamon rolls. Sunday lunch is roast beef, turkey and dressing, fried chicken, and liver and onions. Sunday's special is an 8 ounce sirloin with grilled zucchini. Monday is chicken fried steak with cream gravy. Tuesday's special is an 8 ounce sirloin with grilled zucchini, too. Monday and Tuesday are the chef's choice on the buffet, and we post what's cooking on Facebook every week. [OWNER: a regular who eats here on the same day every week] If you're coming from San Angelo or Abilene, pick your night and plan the drive. Look at the week, decide what you're hungry for, bring the family, and come hungry. We'll see you on North Broadway.
3
Try ordering all you can eat steak at a drive-thru
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
The strip has chain restaurants and drive-thru windows, and none of them can offer a sit-down all you can eat steak night. The joke makes the contrast without naming anybody, and every item in it comes off the Beefmaster menu pages.
Try ordering all you can eat steak at a drive-thru window. Go ahead. I'll wait. At Beefmaster, Thursday night is all you can eat steak dinner. You sit down, you eat until you're done, and nobody hands you a bag. Every special on our weekly menu comes with endless soup or salad. The 8 ounce sirloin, the chicken fried steak with cream gravy, the steak trimmings. Grilled zucchini and your choice of potato go on the plate too. You pour yourself a bowl of soup, or go back to the salad bar, as many times as you like. Our burgers are 100 percent beef. Our Friday fish is all you can eat, lunch and dinner. Our Wednesday tacos, enchiladas, and tamales are all you can eat too. Saturday, two people can split two pounds of K.C. style sirloin. Monday and Tuesday the chef picks the menu, and it changes from one week to the next. Check our Facebook page and see what's cooking. Thursday lunch is an all you can eat barbecue buffet, too. That's a lot of food for one steakhouse in Ballinger. A chain restaurant has a menu designed by a committee somewhere else. Ours gets decided here, on North Broadway, by the people who cook it.
4
How to feed 50 people without cooking for 50
The useful one
▾
Catering is the part of this business that can pay back a content investment fastest. This gives anyone planning a party or a team celebration a checklist, and it ends on the phone number.
When was the last time you cooked for 50 people? It's a lot of food, a lot of dishes, and a lot of standing in the kitchen while your own party happens without you. Here's how to plan a catered event. Start with the headcount and the date. Then call us, and we'll talk through the details. If you have a party or an event coming up in Weatherford, Ballinger, or the area around them, that's exactly the call we want. Next, think about your guests. A crowd always has somebody who won't eat what everybody else will. Our menu has a lot of choices, and the catering menu can be customized, so there's something for every plate. Then count what you won't have to buy. No extra pans, no extra kitchen equipment, no three days of prep. Catering saves you time, and it saves you money. We cater all kinds of events. School football team just won? That's a party. Cowboys in the Super Bowl? That's a party too. A big family dinner counts. So does an office lunch. Ask about our specials and events while you're on the phone, because we're glad to talk through those too. Call 325-365-2424 and tell us what you're planning.
