What to ask before you tint your windows
The useful one
▾
Tint is the service most customers know least about, and the film, the warranty and the installer are questions that cost nothing to give away and send the right customer our way.
Spot A
Start with the film. Different films do different jobs, so know what you want first. We use Llumar, including ATR and IRX films, which runs from premium metal films to IR ceramic films. [OWNER: how ATR and IRX differ, in your own words] Decide whether you want it for heat, for looks, or for both. Then check the warranty. Llumar films carry a nationwide lifetime warranty from the manufacturer, so ask whether the shop stands behind its own install too.
Spot B
Next, who's putting it on. Our tint page says over thirty years of experience. Ask to see photos of finished work. Our site has a window tint gallery, and our Facebook shows a 1987 Land Rover Defender with Llumar IRX ceramic film. Look at the edges and the corners, because you don't want to be caught bubbled up. One customer wrote that ours lasts without ever peeling, with a seamless window wrap. Another said the shop has the experience and the routine to do it right every time.

Tint goes on more than cars. We do automotive, residential and commercial windows. One customer wrote that the car felt much cooler afterward, in looks and in temperature.
