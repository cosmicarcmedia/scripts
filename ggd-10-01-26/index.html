1
Why we worked a Father's Day against a customer's advice
The origin and the face
▾
A customer already wrote that you worked on Father's Day to get the job done, and this puts your face and your voice on that story, so the next homeowner meets you and your brother before they ever call.
Spot A
A customer wrote that we worked on Father's Day to get their door done. They also wrote that it was against their advice. That's how we were raised. I'm Tony Garza, and my brother and I own G and G Garage Doors here in San Angelo. We grew up around here. We learned young that hard work and honesty will get you a long way in life.
Spot B
Between the two of us, we've got more than thirty years in garage doors. We started G and G in 2018, and we want to give back to this community. [OWNER: why you and your brother went out on your own] We sell Clopay doors and LiftMaster openers, and we work on all types. Installs, repairs, maintenance. We take the big jobs and the small ones. And the estimate is free.
Spot C
One customer was visiting their daughter from out of town. The night before they had to leave, the daughter's garage door opener burned out. They called us at eight in the morning. We were there before nine. We put in a new opener, adjusted the spring tension, and lubed the door. All of it was done before eleven. They said the price was very reasonable. Another customer called about their mother's opener, which had quit a day or two earlier. We came out right away and took care of it. [OWNER: why you worked that Father's Day] So yes, we worked on Father's Day.

3
Call six garage door companies before you call me
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
This is the one worth forwarding: it hands homeowners two questions to ask any garage door company, and your own customers' reviews supply the answers.
Spot C
If your garage door breaks, don't call me first. Call six places. Ask every one of them the same two questions. What's it going to cost, and how soon can you be here? One customer did exactly that. They asked six places for a price. They wrote that ours was the best of the six. They said the installation was perfect, and called us great guys and super professional.
Spot A
Two other customers called around, too. One had backed into their own garage door and needed a new one. The places they called quoted far more, and couldn't help for weeks. The other had trouble with an opener. Everybody they called was a week or more out. We came the same day and replaced it. Another customer called at eleven on a Monday morning with a broken spring. Our tech was there by two, and the door was fixed by three.
Spot B
A garage door is part of what keeps your house safe. It shouldn't sit broken for a week. Our estimates are free, and the work comes with a warranty. Sometimes a part has to be ordered. One customer wrote that we installed it super fast once it came in. Another one said we answered right away. So call six if you want. Then call me. Ask for Tony.
4
What to ask whoever comes out to look at your garage door
The useful one
▾
It costs you nothing to give away and comes straight from your own services page and your reviews, so homeowners save it and remember who explained it.
Spot B
If your garage door squeaks, you've probably stopped hearing it. It shouldn't squeak. A squeak or a funny noise means it's time to get the door checked. We find the source of the problem and fix it. Garage doors need regular maintenance to keep running smoothly for years. A little attention now saves you money down the road.
Spot C
So when somebody comes out to look at yours, ask questions. Ask how many years your door has left. On a maintenance visit, we'll give you an estimate on the life of your door. Ask if the spring tension is right. Ask when the door was last lubed. One customer needed a new opener. In the same visit, we adjusted the spring tension and lubed the door. [OWNER: the one thing a homeowner can safely check on their own between visits]
Spot A
Your home is one of the biggest investments you'll ever make. Your garage door is part of keeping it safe and secure. So look up whoever you call on the Better Business Bureau. We're accredited, with an A plus rating. Ask them to explain what's wrong in plain words. I'll keep you as informed as I can about whatever is going on with your door. [OWNER: why nobody should try to fix a broken spring themselves] Ask about the warranty, too. Ask for the estimate first. Ours is free. Can't tell if you need a repair, a new opener, or a whole new door? I'll tell you.
