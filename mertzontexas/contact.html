Frequently Asked

Common questions

The things City Hall gets asked most. If yours is not here, send it and it will get added.

How do I get water or sewer service?
Call City Hall at (325) 835-5791 or come by 104 S. Park View. Rate and deposit information is available there, and the applications are on the services page.
Where do I pay my bill?
Online through the city payment portal, by mail to PO Box 456, or in person at City Hall.
When does the council meet?
6:30 p.m. on the first and third Monday of every month at City Hall. Meetings are open to the public. See the agendas and minutes.
How do I get an item on the agenda?
Submit the request by 12:00 p.m. the Wednesday before the meeting. Anything later rolls to the next meeting.
Can I still water my yard?
Stage 1 of the drought plan asks you to hold off voluntarily on landscape irrigation and other non-essential use from April 1 through October 31. Read the full guidelines.
Who picks up my trash?
Trash is handled by private haulers, not the city. Three of them serve Mertzon, and the numbers are on the services page.
How do I get my business in the directory?
Send a message with the business name, address, phone, and category. Listings are free for businesses in the city.
