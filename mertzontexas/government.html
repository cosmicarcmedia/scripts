City Government

Five elected officials, meeting twice a month in a room anyone can walk into. Here is who they are, when they meet, and what they have decided.

How it works

Getting on the agenda

Any resident can ask the council to take up an item. Call City Hall or send a written request.

Deadline: 12:00 p.m. the Wednesday before the meeting. Requests after that roll to the next meeting.
Submit an agenda request
When
6:30 p.m., first and third Monday of every month
Where
City Hall, 104 S. Park View, Mertzon
Open to
Everyone. Public comment is taken as posted on the agenda.

City Hall answers the phone during business hours, and the council reads what gets sent in. You do not have to wait until Monday night.
