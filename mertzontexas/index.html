About Mertzon

A railroad town that became the county seat.

The post office opened in 1908 and the railroad came through in 1911, named for M. L. Mertz, a San Angelo banker and a director of the line. Businesses followed the tracks over from Sherwood, and in 1936 voters made Mertzon the seat of Irion County.

Today it is a town of a few hundred people with an independent school district, a courthouse square, spacious parks, and enough shade to make a West Texas August bearable.
