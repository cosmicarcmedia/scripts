3
Ask every bridal shop what a gown costs before you book
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
This is the one built to get forwarded. This shop states its range plainly, on the About page and online. No shop is named, and it gives any bride a question to take to every appointment she books.
Spot A
Ask every bridal shop what a gown costs before you book an appointment. Here's our answer. Our gowns run from one thousand to thirty-five hundred dollars. About eighty percent of them land between fifteen hundred and two thousand. That's printed on our About page, so you can read it before you ever call. So a thousand dollars is the low end, and thirty-five hundred is the top. A wedding dress is a big purchase. You should know the range before you fall in love with one.
Spot B
Same with veils. Our veils are on our website, with prices. They run from sixty-eight dollars up to five hundred twenty-eight. Look at the designers too. We carry Allure Bridals, Allure Romance, Maggie Sottero, Sottero and Midgley, Abella, and Rebecca Ingram. A Maggie Sottero gown can be ordered from petite to plus size, and it's handmade with the options you choose.
Spot C
Then ask how long it takes. A gown that's ordered for you takes months to arrive, so plan ahead. Ask what the appointment looks like. Ours is one on one. If a shop can't tell you the range, ask why. We'd like you to walk in already knowing the number. Call us at 325 340 4224, or book on our website.
4
Four things to do before your first wedding dress appointment
The useful one
▾
It costs the shop nothing to give away and it is exactly what a newly engaged bride is searching for. Every item traces to the shop's own pages, so she learns something real about gowns before she ever walks in.
Spot B
Four things to do before your first wedding dress appointment. One. Book it. Appointments are recommended, so the shop can give you its full attention. Two. Decide your number. Gowns come in a wide range, and it's easier to shop when you know where you want to land. Most of ours land between fifteen hundred and two thousand dollars, if that helps. Three. Look at the details on each gown. A single style can come with different coverage, sleeve lengths, necklines, illusion details, and shapewear lining.
Spot C
Four. Give yourself time. A gown gets ordered for you, and it can take months to arrive. Some designers will make it in your size, from petite to plus size. Some are handmade with the options you pick. So if the wedding is coming up, ask about timing at your first appointment.
Spot A
Then think about everybody else. We carry veils, headpieces, and bridal jewelry. We have bridesmaid dresses from Sorella Vita. We have dresses for your mother, and your fiancé's mother. And if the wedding party has tuxes, we do those too. For an elegant evening out, our social occasion dresses are available off the rack. Book a time on our website, or call us at 325 340 4224. We're at 424 North Van Buren.
