The origin and the face
▾

From Ozona to Sonora is thirty-five miles of interstate. The other way, it's a hundred and eight miles to Fort Stockton. That is a long walk with a flat tire. 3
A tire store sells you a tire. Few will drive out and get you.
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
Names the one thing a chain store cannot do, which is come to the shoulder of the highway, and ties it to your own hours and payment options. This is the one a trucker forwards to another trucker.
Most tire stores will sell you a tire. Very few will drive out to the interstate and get you. Here's how a breakdown goes on I-10. You're on the shoulder, the nearest shop is miles away, and you need a truck first and a tire second. We do both from one phone number. We tow you, we put the tire on, and we get you rolling again. A store closes when the store closes. Ours closes at six on weekdays and noon on Saturday, and towing is available anytime. Some roadside jobs we can do right where you're stopped. Flat repair, fuel delivery, a jump, a lockout. Call, tell us where you are, and ask for an estimate. If you drive for a living, we take fleet cards. [OWNER: a call from a trucker or traveler that proves the point] We stock the brands people ask for. Michelin, BFGoodrich, Goodyear, Bridgestone, Yokohama, Hankook, and more. Passenger, commercial, industrial, and farm tires. Our website has the whole tire catalog, and you can ask for a quote before you ever come in. When you're parked on the shoulder, call 325 392 9111. 4
Five minutes a month keeps a tire off the shoulder
The useful one
▾
Gives every driver on that stretch of highway something to do tonight, and it comes straight from the tire care advice already on your own site.
Five minutes a month can keep a tire from stranding you. The tire industry recommends it, and it's easy. Start with the pressure. A tire that runs low gets hot and wears unevenly, and it brakes and handles worse. That heat is how you end up with a blowout or tread separation. Out here, that's a long way from help. Then walk around the vehicle and look. Nails, punctures, cracks, bulges. If you find one, bring it by before it picks its own spot on I-10. Check your spare while you're at it. Is it aired up, and is it ready to go? Find out in your driveway. When you buy new tires, put them on in pairs or sets. If you only need two, the new ones go on the back and the half-worn ones move to the front. One new tire mixed into the old ones can throw off how the vehicle handles. If you swap your tires for the season, store them somewhere dark and dry, away from sunlight, oil, and solvents. And if you feel a bump that gets worse the faster you go, that's usually a tire out of balance. And if something feels off, a bump that gets worse at highway speed, a pull to one side, call us. It's 325 392 9111, or come to 901 11th Street.
