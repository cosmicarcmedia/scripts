And I'm a member of the Better Business Bureau and the San Angelo Chamber of Commerce.

I've got a good crew, too. Two of my technicians have fifteen and twenty-six years in the trade. One of them is NATE certified. They're trained by the manufacturers of the equipment we install, and they keep up with hours of continuing education. When a truck from Barry Hooker Heating and Air pulls up, you get people who've done this a long time. My wife Mandy and I have two kids and two dogs. [OWNER: one thing about running this business with your family]
Spot C
Away from work, I collect antiques, and I love old muscle cars and sports cars. An old muscle car and a good air conditioner have one thing in common. Somebody has to maintain them. San Angelo weather does not wait for business hours
The local anchor
▾

Spot B
San Angelo has weather extremes, and your system has to handle both ends of them. Hot afternoons in the summer. Cold snaps in the winter. [OWNER: the busiest weekend you remember, and what the calls sounded like] That's why after-hours service is available Monday through Friday, and on Saturday and Sunday too. We're on Knickerbocker Road, and we cover San Angelo and the whole Concho Valley. Maintenance matters more in a climate like ours. I like to see a system serviced before the heating season, and again before the cooling season. Members get two diagnostic inspections a year, so the check is already on the calendar. Call us before the weather makes the decision for you.
3
Flat rate means the price is the price
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
The site says outright that the shop uses flat-rate pricing instead of hourly pricing and promises no surprises when the work is done, and it lists the diagnostic fee. Asking customers to ask any company the same question draws a sharp line without naming one.
Spot C
Ask any heating and air company one question before they start. Is this price by the hour, or is it a flat rate? We use flat-rate pricing instead of pricing by the hour. The idea is simple. When we finish, there are no surprises. That goes for residential and light commercial jobs alike. We're known as the company that takes the time to do the job right the first time.
Spot A
With hourly pricing, the clock decides the bill. With a flat rate, you know the number before the work begins. [OWNER: how you give the price, and when] Our diagnostic fee is eighty-nine dollars, and it's on our website for anyone to see. For Preferred Club members, it's fifty.
Spot B
We back the work with a one hundred percent satisfaction guarantee. We give free estimates when you're replacing equipment, and on duct cleaning too. We do free video inspections of your ductwork, so you can see what we see. After every installation, our technicians run a complete checklist for quality and proper operation. We install and service any brand, so the equipment we recommend fits your house and your budget. And we offer financing through First Financial Bank. If you're comparing prices, compare what's in the price. Ask the next company those same questions, and see what they say.
4
The filter, the old furnace, and the dryer vent
The useful one
▾
Three things in nearly every San Angelo house get ignored until something goes wrong, and all three come straight from the shop's own FAQ page. It costs nothing to give away and leads right to the free video duct inspection.
Spot A
Three things in your house that nobody checks until something goes wrong. One. Your filter. In most homes, check it every two months. If you have pets, check it once a month. [OWNER: how a homeowner can tell a filter is done in ten seconds] Dust, allergens, bacteria, and viruses all circulate through your HVAC system. Good filtration helps with that.
Spot B
Two. An older gas furnace. An older furnace raises the risk of carbon monoxide gas. You can't see it or smell it, so don't guess about it. Our technicians have equipment to check for it. Get your system serviced before the heating season, and again before the cooling season. That's twice a year, and it's what our Preferred Club is built around. And if you're thinking of replacing an old system, higher efficiency systems have higher SEER ratings, which can lower your utility bills.
Spot C
Three. Your dryer vent. Dryer vents are commonly ignored, and they can be a fire hazard if they aren't cleaned. We do dryer vent and duct cleaning with Rotobrush equipment, and a HEPA filter on the machine. We take care to protect the inside of your house while we work. And before we clean your ducts, we do a free video inspection. You see what's in there, and then you decide.
