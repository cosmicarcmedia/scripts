2
Forty days over a hundred, and where to sit
The local anchor
▾
San Angelo's heat is the one thing every customer already agrees on. Opening on a local number leads straight into shade, and shows three ways Premier handles it without a hard sell.
Spot A
San Angelo had forty days at a hundred degrees or hotter this year, as of early September. The average is thirty. So if you want to use your patio here, you need shade. We build it three ways. A pergola, an awning, or motorized shades. Pick the one that fits your house and your budget.
Spot B
Pergolas are the quick way. A prefab pergola is a quick and cost effective way to turn a back patio into a usable space. They come in multiple styles and prices, and the prices are on our website. An awning is steel or wood. Every awning has a frame, a roof covering, and a rain gutter, finished in the color you pick. We can add a porch lid, which hides the framing overhead. Add the electrical package and you get fans and lighting under it. Birds and wasps like to nest up there.
Spot C
Motorized shades block UV, cut down on wind and moisture, and keep insects out. They are built to the size of your porch, your awning, or your pergola. You run them with a remote, or you ask Alexa. There are two levels. Basic is cable guided. Premier runs on a track, and it is rated for heavy wind. Whichever one you choose, we build it custom to your specs.

Ask three things before you hire anyone for your patio

▾
Price, preview and accountability are the three things a patio buyer should demand. Premier answers each from its own pages: posted prices, a 3D and VR preview on every job, and a named owner with an accredited BBB profile.

Before you hire anyone to build your patio, ask three things. One. What does it cost? Ask that before anybody comes out. Ours is on the website. 4
Three decisions to make before an awning goes up
The useful one
▾
A plain checklist from the shop's own awning page: frame, porch lid, and electrical. It costs nothing to give away and gets the buyer thinking about the details before the first quote.
Spot C
Thinking about an awning? Make three decisions before anybody builds it. We build every awning custom, steel or wood beam, and we build it to last. Number one. Steel or wood. Either one gets a frame, a roof covering, and a rain gutter. Steel can also take an eyebrow. It cleans up the front and the sides, and it blocks some extra sun. On a wood frame, you would use a fascia for the same clean look.
Spot A
Number two. A porch lid. It covers the framing overhead, which is a common nesting place for birds and wasps. It gives the awning a clean look. And it's a must have if you want the electrical package. So if you plan on fans and lights, you want the porch lid. There are two levels. Basic is R panel or metal soffit. Premier is shiplap, painted or stained. You pick your porch lid option when you book.
Spot B
Number three. What you want to plug in. The electrical package covers fans, recessed and accent lighting, control switches, outlets, motorized shades, and solar. Decide that early, because it ties into the porch lid. The eyebrow on a steel awning is also a clean mounting spot for motorized shades. Then pick your color, and we finish it to your spec.
