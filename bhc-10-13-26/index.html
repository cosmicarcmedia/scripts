Ask any heating and air company if you get your money back on a new system
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
A money back promise on a new install is the one thing a big dealer's fine print rarely gives you, and your own site makes it in plain words, so asking the question draws the line without naming anyone.
Spot C
Here's a question to ask any heating and air company before you buy a new system. If I'm not happy with it, do I get my money back? Here, the answer is yes, for the first ninety days after a new install. [OWNER: what the ninety day guarantee covers and how a customer uses it] Every service we do carries a hundred percent satisfaction guarantee, too. We service all makes and models, whatever brand is on the unit.

Second question, who is coming to my house, and when? Our technicians show up in uniform, in a clearly marked truck, and we keep the service windows tight. Our trucks are fully stocked, and we carry a wide selection of replacement parts. We use mobile web dispatch to get to you quicker. Third, will you tell me what you found, in writing? On a tune-up, you get a written report of what we found.

We call if we're running late, we keep the noise down, and we clean up after ourselves. Ask whoever else you're calling the same questions, and then call us at 325 208 1892.
4
What a tune-up checks, and why it happens twice a year
The useful one
▾
Most homeowners hear the word tune-up and have no idea what it covers, and the checklist you already publish shows them what they are paying for and why spring and fall both count.
Spot A
Most people don't think about maintenance until the system breaks. Then they wish they'd kept up with it. Your air conditioner is a lot like your car. It runs fine for a long time, and then it breaks on the hottest day of the year. The oil change for your system is a tune-up, and you want two of them a year. One in the spring for the air conditioning, and one in the fall for the heating.
Spot B
The fall tune-up covers your heat. We check for carbon monoxide, inspect the heat exchanger, check for gas leaks at the furnace, and test the ignition system and the safety devices, and check the gas pressure, the flue pipe, the electrical connections, and the thermostat. The spring tune-up covers your cooling. We clean the outdoor coil, look for refrigerant leaks, check the pressures and temperatures, check the capacitor and the contactor, clean the drain, and lubricate the moving parts.
Spot C
Our technicians are NATE certified, and they work from a checklist, so nothing gets skipped. You get a written report of what we found. If we spot a small problem, we fix it before it turns into a big one. Book it before the peak of summer or the peak of winter, so we can work around your schedule. Call us at 325 208 1892.
