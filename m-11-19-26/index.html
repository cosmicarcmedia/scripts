1
We're just a regular Texas family that raises cattle
The origin and the face
▾
The About page says the family chose to bring its beef to customers after 2020, and the owners are cattle raisers first. This puts a face on the label before a stranger decides to put a whole beef in a freezer.
We're a regular Texas family. We've raised cattle for generations. [OWNER: who you are and how many generations, in your words] Then came 2020. And after that, we decided to bring our beef straight to you. We're MTXBeef, from Mason, Texas. Here's what we do. We're involved in every step. Genetics, feed, how the cattle are handled and cared for, and plenty of ranch land for them to roam. We call it ranch to table. We own and operate an eight thousand head feed yard, Mason County Feeders, right here in Mason. We're responsible for the health of those cattle, and the feed is nutritionally sound, and uses human consumable byproducts from the Texas Hill Country. When the cattle are ready, we haul them. We oversee the processors. Then we pick up the finished meat and get it ready for delivery to your home. [OWNER: the part of that process you do yourself that surprises people] That's the family business. Wholesale, retail, whole beef and half beef. You can buy online, at participating retail spots, or at the plant in Mason. We sell ribeyes and tomahawks, ground beef and hamburger patties, all beef hot dogs and smoked jerky. Premium beef at affordable prices. Custom whole or half beef is the most economical way to buy MTXBeef. If you want to know where your steak came from, ask us. We can tell you.

3
Ask where your beef came from, and who handled it
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
Plenty of companies sell beef boxes, and almost none of them can say who raised, fed, hauled and delivered the animal. These are five plain questions, and the family answers each one from the About page.
When you buy beef, ask five questions. One, where was it raised? Ours is raised by a family of generational cattle raisers in Mason, Texas. Two, who fed it? We own the feed yard, an eight thousand head operation called Mason County Feeders. We're involved in the feed program, and we use human consumable byproducts from the Texas Hill Country. Three, who hauled it? We did. We haul the cattle ourselves. Four, who made sure the processor did it right? We did that too. We oversee the processors. Five, who brings it to your door? We pick up the finished meat, and we prepare it for delivery to your home. [OWNER: a delivery you remember, in your words] Want to see what's available? Our virtual meat market lists individual cuts, ribeyes, ground beef and beef boxes. Whole and half beef prices are on the website. Wholesale prices come by email. Custom whole or half beef is the most economical way to buy from us. Many places can answer one or two of those. A family that raises, hauls, oversees and delivers can answer all five. If you want to talk to the person who knows the answers, call 817 681 5723. You'll get the family, and that's the point. Ranch to table means we're in every step.
4
How a whole or half beef works, and why winter is the time
The useful one
▾
A freezer fill is a big purchase most people have never made, and the steps are easy to miss. The script walks through the shop's own order process, step by step, and ties it to the winter stock up season.
If you've never bought a whole or half beef, here's how it works. You're buying the animal's hanging weight. That's the weight after processing, before the meat gets trimmed and cut. A beef averages about 800 to 1,000 pounds hanging weight. [OWNER: how much meat that turns into, in your words] Step one, email us or call us and we'll send the order sheets. Step two, fill out the cut sheet. You pick your cuts, how thick you want them cut, and which cuts get ground. Step three, send it back. You can email it or text a photo. Step four, pay a deposit to hold your order. Half beef is a $750 deposit. A whole beef is $1,500. After it's processed and weighed, you get a final invoice for the exact weight, minus your deposit. A whole beef lasts a family at least a year. You can also split a whole or half with family or friends. Pickup is at the plant in Mason, and we can also deliver. So find out how many people are in on it first. Delivery is available inside the standard delivery areas. Whole beef is $5.75 a pound hanging weight, and half beef is $6. It's winter, and that's when freezers get filled. Call 817 681 5723 and ask about the cut sheet.
