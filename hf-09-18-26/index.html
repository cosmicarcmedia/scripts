The question is whether you're resting after the work or hiding from it. To him who knows to do good and doesn't, to him it's sin. Which means it can be repented of. Root two, ignorance. Your father was passive or gone, and you've improvised for twenty years. That's a real disadvantage that still leaves you without an excuse. Your example is Christ and your manual's the book. Root three, inferiority. She's more organized, more disciplined, more mature, and you shrank back. Is headship a trophy for the most competent person in the room? It's a post you were assigned, so lean on her strengths and keep the weight. Root four, fear, and it wears a disguise: avoiding conflict feels like keeping the peace. That feeling is fear that learned to quote a Bible verse. You're the thermostat in your home rather than the thermometer. A thermometer reads the room, a thermostat sets it, and when her mood runs the house, nobody set it.
