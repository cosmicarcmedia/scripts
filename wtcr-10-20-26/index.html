2
A porch cover built for West Texas weather
The local anchor
▾
The site's patio cover page says Texas weather damages outdoor furniture and that its covers are built for heat, wind and storms. On camera, that speaks to homeowners from Grape Creek to Menard.
Spot A
West Texas weather is hard on a patio. The heat, the wind, and the storms that blow through now and then. It damages outdoor furniture and keeps you inside. But a good cover changes that, and it can help cool your house too. Shade on your windows and walls cuts the solar heat coming in, which may lower your cooling costs.
Spot B
We build solid roof covers, pergolas, and screened in porches. Wood or aluminum, and we match your roofline, your finishes, and your colors. We also do porch extensions, and insulated covers if you want better temperature control and less noise. If the cover needs structural work, it usually needs a permit. We handle the permit process for you. Most patio cover projects wrap up in one to two weeks, and custom features can stretch that out. You get a schedule and regular updates along the way.
Spot C
We work well outside of town, too. We go out to Grape Creek, Christoval, Wall, Knickerbocker, and Carlsbad. We go to Ballinger, Brady, Eden, Robert Lee, Sterling City, Mertzon, and Menard. Every one of those places gets the same sun and the same wind. A cover that fits your house will hold up to it. If you want to talk it through, book a walkthrough on our site or text us.

4
How long a remodel takes, and what happens before the hammer
The useful one
▾
Every homeowner planning a kitchen or bath wants the timeline and the order of steps, and the shop already publishes both. Giving it away on camera costs nothing and shows a crew with a plan.
Spot B
Planning a kitchen or a bathroom remodel? A bathroom usually takes two to four weeks. A kitchen usually takes four to six. A home addition runs six to twelve. Those are typical numbers, and structural, plumbing, or custom work can stretch them. Most of the work happens before the first hammer swings.
Spot A
First, you get a starting price range online. Then we walk through your house with you and talk goals and budget. Then we build a design with you until you approve it. Only then do you get construction pricing. You'll pick your materials along the way, like quartz, granite, or custom wood for countertops, and tile, vinyl, or hardwood for floors. If you plan to stay in the house for years, ask about grab bars, curbless showers, slip resistant surfaces, and wider doorways.
Spot C
Then comes the contract, the permits, and the materials order. Ask early about the permit, because it shapes the schedule. When construction starts, we protect your home first. After that we work from site prep through trim out. The last step is the punch list. That's the list of small details that make it done. We handle the permits when the work touches plumbing, electrical, or structure. If you have a project in mind, text us at the number on our page.
