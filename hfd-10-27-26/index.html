I grew up in Aztec, New Mexico. My wife, Karina, grew up in Sanderson, Texas. Two small towns, a long way apart. When we finished school, we wanted to take care of people who didn't have many places to go. Karina went to work at a community health center here in San Angelo. I moved to San Angelo right after graduation to do the same kind of work. We both spent our early years caring for underserved patients. That commitment still drives us today. [OWNER: why San Angelo and no other town]
Spot C
Now we run Harrison Family Dental together, on Sunset Drive. We're both certified in oral conscious sedation, and in nitrous oxide sedation. Karina is fluent in Spanish. 4
When should your child first see a dentist?
The useful one
▾
New parents search this question, and the page answers it with a specific age. It costs nothing to give away and sets up an easy first appointment for a family.
Spot A
New parents, here's a question I hear a lot. When should my baby first see a dentist? The recommendation is no later than the first birthday. That's usually right around when the first tooth shows up. That's what the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends. We see children from birth all the way through their teen years.
Spot B
Why so early? Early childhood cavities are the most common chronic disease in American children. They're more common than asthma. That's worth knowing. At a first visit, the dentist follows how the teeth and gums are developing. We can also talk with you about diet and brushing habits. It's a good time to ask every question you've been saving up.
Spot C
Care for a child ranges from cleanings and sealants to treating problems when they come up. Regular checkups continue through childhood, and we're here for all of them. A visit for a small child is about making it comfortable. That's our goal for patients of any age. [OWNER: one thing you do to make a toddler comfortable in the chair] If your child is close to a year old, call us. If your child is past a year old and hasn't been yet, call us too. Our number is 325 949 1004. We will walk you through booking that first appointment.
