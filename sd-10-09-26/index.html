▾

I only had three years of college, and I was still finishing my bachelor's degree. They gave me a scholarship, and I graduated from Ohio State.

Over there, I served as the Regional Chief of the TMJ Department, for a population of more than thirty thousand people. 4
What to do in the first minutes when a tooth gets knocked out
The useful one
▾
A knocked out tooth is a moment every parent of an athlete fears, and the first few minutes matter. Giving away the steps costs Dr. B nothing, ties directly to the mouthguard offer, and gives the viewer a reason to save the video.
Spot A
If a tooth gets knocked out, don't panic. Stay calm. Here's what to do in the first few minutes. First, pick up the tooth by the crown, which is the white part. Never touch the root. If it's dirty, rinse it gently. Don't scrub it, and don't pull off anything that's still attached to it.
Spot B
Second, try to put it gently back into the socket. If that doesn't work, put the tooth in a cup of milk. Then get to a dentist as fast as you can, and bring the tooth with you. Do all of that on the way to the office. That cup of milk matters.
Spot C
Third, call us. If the tooth is out, the clock is running, so make the call early. During office hours, we'll try to see you the same day. Our number is 325 387 2659. This can happen on football fields and basketball courts. That's why we make custom mouthguards for local student athletes, free of charge. JV and varsity players in Sonora and nearby districts qualify. Keep our number in your phone, and share this with a coach or a parent. You never know when somebody in your family might need it.
