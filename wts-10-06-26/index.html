1
People call me on Sundays
The origin and the face
▾
The shop is new and the owner's name appears nowhere on its site, so the face has to come from what customers already say about him: the Sunday blowout, the night call, the big rig stuck at a stoplight. This puts his name and voice on those stories.
Spot A
A customer wrote that I went above and beyond on a Sunday. They had a blowout, they called, and I came out. They wrote that the work and the price were both great. I'm Marco Saucedo, and this is WTX Tire Shop here in San Angelo. We're a family-owned shop. [OWNER: why you opened a tire shop, and what you did before it] Most businesses are closed on Sunday. Our Facebook page says roadside assistance, twenty-four seven.
Spot B
Another customer wrote that a tire went flat right when they got home. They were outside the city limits, nowhere near a tire shop, with one vehicle. I came out to their house that night and patched the tire. I took an electronic payment right there. Somebody else wrote that I pulled their big rig out of the middle of a stoplight. A stranded driver wrote that I came and fixed the tire with no hesitation. [OWNER: your own story from one of those calls]
Spot C
One customer bought nine new trailer tires. I greased the trailer myself, checked the oil in the hubs, and mounted the tires. They called it a heck of a deal. We sell new and used tires, and we do brake maintenance. I explain what your vehicle needs, show you the options, and let you decide. We're at 731 East 19th Street, Monday through Saturday, eight to six. Roadside assistance is day and night. The number is 325 301 0067.
2
Six towns, one tire shop
The local anchor
▾
The shop's own site names six towns, and the customer stories prove it shows up in them. A viewer in Bronte or Mertzon learns the phone number of a tire shop that comes to them, which a chain with one bay in town never says.
Spot B
Out past the city limits, a flat tire happens nowhere near a tire shop. One customer wrote almost exactly that, and I came to their house that night. San Angelo, Mertzon, Ballinger, Robert Lee, Bronte and Christoval. Those are the six towns on our website. Roadside assistance and mobile tire repair come to you. We come to the house, the jobsite or the roadside. You don't have to get the tire to us first.
Spot A
Ever had a flat when you were nowhere near town? A family's niece came down from Florida with a damaged tire. We helped her very early in the morning and got it fixed. A traveler wrote that they stopped by on their travels, and we helped them stay on the road. Another customer wrote that we picked their vehicle up from their house. We fixed it in a day. [OWNER: the farthest you've driven for one tire, and what the call was]
Spot C
Daily drivers, ranch trucks, trailers and highway travelers all end up calling us. One customer bought fifteen tires from us in six months. They wrote that it's nice to do business with people who care about their community. We sell new tires and used tires. If you're stuck in one of those six towns, call 325 301 0067. Tell me what road you're on and what you're driving. Then we'll figure out what I can do.
3
What a weekend flat costs everywhere else
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
One Google review says every other shop quoted three to five hundred dollars for a weekend flat and WTX fixed it for one fifty. That is the weekend and after hours pricing story a customer will forward, and since a customer wrote it, Marco never has to brag about it himself.
Spot C
A customer had a flat on an RV they were trying to sell. It was the weekend. They wrote that everywhere else they called quoted three hundred to five hundred dollars, because it was the weekend. I came out and had the flat fixed in under an hour, travel included. That one cost a hundred and fifty dollars. They sold the RV and hauled it off that night. They called me nice, fast and professional.
Spot A
Ask any tire shop this. What does a flat cost on a Saturday? Then ask what it costs on a Tuesday. At our shop you'll know the price before any work begins. I'll show you your options at different prices and let you decide. No pressure, and nobody selling you things you don't need.
Spot B
Another customer wrote that they had a blowout on a Sunday. They said we did great work and great pricing. Another one wrote honest pricing and excellent service. We take cards too, so you won't be hunting for cash on a roadside. Mobile repair can save you the cost of a tow. A flat tire is stressful enough. The bill shouldn't be the part that surprises you. Call me at 325 301 0067 and ask what your flat will cost.
4
Can your flat tire be fixed? Check where the hole is
The useful one
▾
Every driver with a nail in a tire wonders whether it needs replacing, and the tire industry has a clear rule that few people know. The shop teaches the rule for free and becomes the number to call.
Spot A
You've got a nail in your tire. Can it be fixed, or do you need a new one? Look at where the nail went in. A tire can only be repaired if the damage is in the tread. That's the part that touches the road. And the hole can't be bigger than a quarter of an inch.
Spot B
If it's in the shoulder or the sidewall, the tire has to be replaced. A real repair takes the tire off the wheel. It gets inspected inside and out, including the inner liner. Then a plug fills the hole, and a patch seals the inside of the tire. A plug by itself isn't a repair. That's the tire industry's own rule.
Spot C
If it's a tread puncture under a quarter inch, it's usually a repair. A tire that's been repaired several times already needs replacing too. If you drove on it flat, it's usually done. The tire loses its structure, and a patch can't fix that. On the roadside, a plug can get you home. Then get the real repair done. We do flat tire repair at the shop, and we come to you for mobile repair. Not sure which one you've got? Call 325 301 0067 and tell me where the hole is.
