1
My daughter told San Angelo LIVE I came from a family who had literally nothing
The origin and the face
▾
The most human thing ever written about the company sits in a 2016 article few people have read, where Sonny's daughter says he worked his way up from nothing to his dream. This puts that story on camera in his own voice.
Spot A
My daughter Brooke told San Angelo LIVE that I came from a family who had literally nothing. She said it at my grand opening in 2016, with the mayor and the Chamber of Commerce there. I'm Sonny Dooley, and I was born and raised in San Angelo. By that day I had sixteen years in the heating and air trade.
Spot B
That day I told the paper what would make us stand out. The quality of the work we do. We started as family and friends. My son, my wife Melissa and my older sister were in it from the beginning. These days our three kids help out too. Family is very important to us. We're here to make sure your family is safe and happy.
Spot C
That was ten years ago. When your AC quits in July, who shows up at your house? One customer wrote that she's used us since we started, and that we always come with a smile. Another called us fair prices, great attitudes, hard workers, and locally owned. A third wrote that we'll make sure your AC is kicking for the hot summers. We're state licensed, and we work on homes and businesses. No project is too large or too small. The phone number at the top of our website has my name on it.
2
Wall, Christoval, Bronte, Sterling City: we come to you too
The local anchor
▾
The site promises service anywhere within a hundred miles, and the reviews show how fast that plays out in a West Texas July. Calling out Wall, Christoval and Bronte by name tells those towns this video is for them.
Spot B
If you live in Wall, Christoval, Water Valley or Sterling City, we come to you. Same for Mertzon, Miles, Ballinger, Robert Lee and Bronte. Our service area is San Angelo and anywhere within a hundred miles. We're based out of Loch Lomond Road in San Angelo. That's a lot of West Texas to cover in July. If your air conditioner quits out in Bronte, who comes?
Spot A
One family had just bought a house and came home to eighty-seven degrees inside, with a three-year-old. They called several companies. They wrote that some never answered or responded at all. They called us, and somebody was out that day with a plan. The new AC was installed and working the next day. They wrote that we maintain it for them every year now.
Spot C
Another customer had gone four days without air while she dealt with her home warranty company. She called me at 8:15 on a July morning. We were at her house by 9:30, and the unit was fixed by 10:30. A mom wrote that she'd gone about a week without AC, and we fixed hers the same day, at the end of a Friday. And a parent wrote to thank us for helping with a daughter's Olfen Mustangs cheer season. Wherever you are inside that hundred miles, call us.
3
If somebody says you need a new compressor, get a second opinion first
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
Customers write that other companies quoted thousands more or called a good compressor bad. A second opinion offer, told through their own reviews, draws the fair line without naming anyone.
Spot C
If somebody says you need a new compressor, get a second opinion before you pay. I know that's an odd thing for an AC company to say. But it's saved customers thousands. One customer wrote that another company told her she needed a compressor, almost four thousand dollars. She called us for a second opinion.
Spot A
I came out the next day, for free, and checked everything. I explained why compressors for her unit cost what they do, and I was upfront about my own pricing. There are several reasons a compressor can shut off, and it may not be the compressor at all. Hers was in perfect shape. All it needed was a PVC cap, a two-second fix. I didn't charge her. Her review says I wanted to be sure the compressor was the problem before I pushed a sale.
Spot B
It goes the other way too. Another customer wrote that three other HVAC companies kept telling her the cooling was fine. In her words, I heard her out and explained everything, and it turned out to be the compressor. So sometimes it's the compressor and sometimes it isn't. Ask the tech one question: how do you know it's the compressor? Estimates are free. One customer who bought a full system wrote that other companies quoted thousands more.
4
Twelve, two and four hundred: three numbers every San Angelo homeowner should know about their AC
The useful one
▾
The company's own FAQ already holds three numbers every homeowner can use: filters, service visits and square feet per ton. Giving them away earns the follow.
Spot A
Twelve, two and four hundred. Those are the three numbers I'd give every San Angelo homeowner about their air conditioner. Twelve is how many times a year to change your filter. Monthly, on most systems. When did you last change yours? If you'd rather not do it yourself, filter changes are on our service list.
Spot B
Two is how many times a year to have it serviced. Your air conditioner every spring, and your heater every fall. We offer preventive maintenance plans. They're there to head off costly repairs and keep the system efficient. One customer wrote that I walked her through what to watch for and what can trigger a shutdown.
Spot C
Four hundred is square feet per ton. Most homes call for about that. Divide your square footage by four hundred and you have a rough starting point. If your system is a lot smaller than that, it's worth a conversation. If you're buying a new system, ask about the warranty. On Bryant residential equipment, ours is ten years on parts and ten years on the compressor. We don't finance it ourselves, but financing is available through First Financial. One customer wrote that we'll work with you on payments if you need. Call me and we'll go through your numbers.
