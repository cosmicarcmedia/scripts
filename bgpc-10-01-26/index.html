1
A pest control guy is about to walk through your house
The origin and the face
▾
Customers already describe the two of you in specific ways, Vern locking up behind himself and Don showing up when he says he will, so this puts your faces and voices on those stories and the next customer feels like they have already met you.
Spot A
A pest control guy is about to walk through your house. Most people don't think about that until the truck is in the driveway. We think about it every day. We're Vern Gant and Don Kennedy. We started Bug Guys Pest Control here in San Angelo in October of 2020. Between the two of us, we've got over forty years in pest control. It's a family owned and run business. We're licensed, bonded and insured, and BBB accredited.
Spot B
[OWNER: why the two of you started Bug Guys] Some of our customers go back further than that. One family wrote that they've used Vern since before there was a Bug Guys. They trust him to spray inside the house when nobody's home, and lock up when he's done. That's a lot to hand somebody. Pest control can feel invasive at times, and we know it. So we treat every customer like the only customer we have.
Spot C
One review says Don showed up when he said he would and did what he said he'd do. Another says we had them scheduled within twenty-four hours, and Vern even cleaned off the spider webs he found. We move fast on purpose. Every extra day the bugs or rodents are in your house, the risk of spreading disease goes up. If you've got a problem anywhere in San Angelo or the Concho Valley, call Vern or Don.
2
Where you live in San Angelo decides which bugs you get
The local anchor
▾
Your own site ties Lake Nasworthy, Santa Rita Park and Southland to different pests, and a customer out in the country wrote about the scorpions, so people in each part of town hear their own bugs named.
Spot B
Where you live in San Angelo decides which bugs you get. Out at Lake Nasworthy, you're next to the water. That means mosquitoes and rodents. Mosquito season here runs from March through October. Over in Santa Rita Park, the homes are older and the trees are big. Older homes can be more open to termites and rodents. Down in Southland, with all the parks and green space, it's fleas and ticks.
Spot A
Out in the country, it's spiders and scorpions. One family out there wrote that before we started spraying, the spiders and scorpions were horrible. They live in the country, so they expected it. Now they have us out every three months, and they barely see anything in the house. If they do, it's already dead. Scorpions hunt at night, and they're good at staying out of sight. That's why most Texans shake out their shoes before putting them on.
Spot C
West Texas is warm, so something is looking to get inside all year round. When it turns cold, rats and mice go looking for warmth and food. We cover San Angelo and the rest of the Concho Valley. [OWNER: the neighborhood or road where you get the most calls] Tell us what part of town you're in, and we'll tell you what to watch for. Call Vern or Don.
3
Ask any pest control company what it costs
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
Your prices, your startup fee and what the price leaves out are all on your website, so this hands viewers two questions to ask any pest company and lets you answer both on camera.
Spot C
Ask any pest control company what it costs. Then ask what it doesn't cover. Ours are both on our website, so here you go. A one time service is a hundred and twenty-five dollars. Or you can have us out monthly, every other month, or every three months. Monthly is sixty. Every other month is seventy-five. Those plans start with a one time startup fee of a hundred and twenty-five.
Spot A
Big houses and big lots can cost more, and the website says that too. The price covers ants, roaches, spiders, scorpions, mice, rats, crickets, fleas and ticks. Bed bugs and German roaches are their own job, so they're left out of that price. If you see bugs between visits, we come back free. Before every regular visit, we call you, and if nobody's home, we leave a message. You can pay your bill online, too.
Spot B
Most insurance policies don't cover termite damage. So for termites, we offer a one million dollar subterranean termite damage warranty. [OWNER: what the warranty covers and what keeps it in force] Ask the next company what its termite warranty is worth in dollars. Yes, our website asks if high prices are bugging you. Yes, that's a dad joke. The numbers under it are real. Call Vern or Don for a free quote.
4
Why you see more bugs the day after pest control
The useful one
▾
It answers the question a new customer asks the day after the first visit, using what your own FAQ already says, so they hear it from you before they start doubting the treatment.
Spot A
You had your house treated yesterday. Today there's a roach on the kitchen floor. That doesn't mean it failed. The first visit is built to flush pests out of their hiding places. Ants and roaches don't know the treatment is there, so they keep crossing it. Sprays can't work if they don't touch the bug. So the bugs come out into the open. The newly emerged ones will most likely die off in the next few days.
Spot B
Ants are a little different. Baits work on the bugs you can see and the ones you can't. An ant carries the bait back to the nest and spreads it through the colony. Fleas, ticks and bed bugs take more than one treatment. They have life cycles, and at certain stages nothing we put down will kill them. So we come back and catch them at the next stage.
Spot C
Within thirty days of the first visit, we start your regular treatments. That breaks the egg cycle. Think of it like maintenance on a car. Skip it long enough and eventually something breaks down. While you wait, check the small cracks around your plumbing and along the foundation. Those are the way in. If you see more bugs between visits, call us. We'll come back free.
