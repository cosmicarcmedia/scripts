4
How to beat an escape room
The useful one
▾
This is the thing every first timer wants to know before they book. Giving the playbook away builds trust and makes the viewer feel ready to walk in the door.
Want to beat an escape room? Here's how. First, bring four to six players. That's enough hands and brains without anyone stepping on each other. Mix it up. You want a logical thinker, a visual observer, and someone who keeps everybody talking. Second, show up early and listen to the briefing. Your game master explains the story and the rules, and small details in the intro often solve a puzzle later. Third, call out everything you find. Every number, every code, every odd object. Silent teams lose, because half the clues never leave the finder's head. Pick one table as your clue hub, and put the solved things in a separate pile so nobody rechecks them. Fourth, keep it simple. Escape rooms are built on logic, so try the obvious answer first. Don't sit on one lock for ten minutes. If you've been stuck for five minutes, ask for a hint, because hints are part of the game. When tempers rise, let another teammate try. One more tip. Wear comfortable clothes and closed toe shoes, because you'll be bending and stretching for an hour. Last, keep it fun. The best teams laugh, and relaxed teams often escape faster. Book a room and try it.
