1
Two people, one map, and a drive from Ohio
The origin and the face
▾
The About page tells how Fred and Su Webber left Galion, Ohio and picked San Angelo off a map, and almost nobody in town knows it. It is the warmest story the shop has and a ready face for a store with no video.
Spot A
Fred and Su Webber ran a used appliance business in Galion, Ohio. One day they decided they needed a change. So they sold everything they had, shut the business down, and packed up. They started driving, and they didn't know where they'd end up. Fred was an avid hunter, and Su was an avid fisherwoman, so they headed for Texas. They still didn't know which city. [OWNER: your name, and how you're connected to Fred and Su]
Spot B
They stopped in Brownwood, and Fred opened up a map. He saw a river and three lakes around San Angelo. And that's all she wrote. They started Ace Appliances in 1979. [OWNER: what the first shop looked like] Then in 1986 they bought out Henry's Appliances, a business that had been operating in San Angelo since 1966. That's why the name has two halves.
Spot C
Today it's Ace and Henry's Appliances. We buy, sell, and service appliances. We call ourselves the oldest appliance sales and service store in West Texas. We offer free delivery in town, and a ninety day guarantee. If you're an individual who needs a repair, or an investor putting appliances in a rent house, we have everything you need. Whether you need a repair or a replacement, we'd like to help with all your appliance needs. We're at 416 East 19th, here in San Angelo. Come see why we've been in business since 1966.
2
416 East 19th, and who to call
The local anchor
▾
A shop that has been on one street since 1966 and keeps separate sales and service lines needs to tell people which number to dial. Saying so on camera makes it easy for a landlord or a neighbor to act.
Spot B
We're at 416 East 19th, in San Angelo. We're open Monday through Friday, nine to five thirty, and Saturday, ten to three. There are two numbers to know. For sales, call 325 653 6000. For service, call 325 710 8203. Service is for repairs, and sales is for buying. [OWNER: what the blocks around the shop looked like when you first came here] We buy appliances too. We buy, sell, and service them, and we've been doing it here since 1979.
Spot A
We've served the Concho Valley for over forty five years. Henry's was already running in San Angelo in 1966. That's a lot of years of washers, stoves, and refrigerators. We deliver free in town. So if you live in San Angelo, you don't have to figure out how to get it home. Here's a tip if you're coming in to shop. Write down the width of the space before you come. An appliance that doesn't fit makes for a long day.
Spot C
If you're an individual who needs a repair, call service. If you're an investor putting appliances in a rent house, call sales. We have everything you need. We sell used appliances for a fraction of the cost, and we service all the major appliances. Our technicians have over forty years of combined experience. Come by 416 East 19th during our hours, and tell us what you're looking for.
3
Ask who fixes it when it breaks
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
The Service and Sales page says the shop services what it sells and backs it with a 90 day guarantee. That draws a fair line against any seller that is not asked the same question, without naming one.
Spot C
If you've ever bought something and then had no idea who to call when it broke, you know the feeling. So when you buy an appliance, ask who fixes it when it breaks. Here, the answer is us. We service all major appliances, and we service what we sell. That's on our website, in plain words. A lot of people never think to ask a seller that.
Spot A
Our technicians have over forty years of combined experience. Every appliance we sell comes with a full ninety day guarantee. We're usually able to respond to warranty calls within twenty four hours. A warranty call is when something goes wrong inside those ninety days. [OWNER: what you do when a part has to be ordered, and how you keep the customer updated] If something is wrong, call the service line, 325 710 8203, and tell us what the appliance is doing.
Spot B
Delivery in town is free. And you can get a used appliance for a fraction of the cost. So here are the three questions to ask any store. Who fixes it after I buy it? How long is the guarantee? How fast do you answer a warranty call? Ask us the same three. We'll give you the same answers you just heard. Sales is 325 653 6000, and we'll go through them with you.
4
How to put appliances in a rent house without overspending
The useful one
▾
Landlords and investors are a named customer group on the shop's own site. A short how-to costs nothing to give away and puts the shop in front of a repeat buyer.
Spot A
If you own a rent house in San Angelo, here's how to put appliances in it without overspending. Start with a list. A stove. A refrigerator. A washer and dryer, if the house has the hookups. Walk through the house with a pen before you walk into any store. Check whether the stove hookup is gas or electric, and write it down. Measure the space for the refrigerator.
Spot B
Then look at used. We sell used appliances for a fraction of the cost. Ask any seller three things. What's the guarantee? Ours is ninety days. Who services it? We service what we sell. And what does delivery cost? In town, ours is free. [OWNER: the pair of appliances landlords buy most often, and what you check on them]
Spot C
Buy everything in one trip, so you get one delivery. Then keep the service number where your tenant can find it, 325 710 8203. That way a stove that stops working gets one phone call. Investors and landlords are customers we are glad to see. We have everything you need. Our sales number is 325 653 6000. And if you're pulling out old appliances that still work, ask us about them. We buy, sell, and service. Call us, tell us what the house needs, and we'll help you work out the rest.
