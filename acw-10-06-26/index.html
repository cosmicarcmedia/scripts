2
San Angelo hit 100 degrees on 100 days in 2011
Local anchor
▾
Every San Angelo homeowner remembers the summer of 2011, and the National Weather Service numbers are exact. Tying that heat to window frames, then to a real customer in Miles, makes this the one video a local window company could make and a national one could not.
Spot B
In 2011, San Angelo hit a hundred degrees or more on a hundred days. The National Weather Service says the old record was sixty days, set in 1969. That summer we had a run of twenty eight days in a row at a hundred or higher. Every window in this town took that heat.
Spot A
Vinyl frames are built with hollow chambers. Vinyl is an excellent insulator, and it doesn't conduct heat the way aluminum does. In direct sun the surface of a frame can pass a hundred and sixty five degrees, and the inside walls of the frame see limited heat buildup. New windows also take pressure off your air conditioner, which lowers the bill every month.
Spot C
We work all over the Concho Valley, from San Angelo to Grape Creek, Veribest and Miles. One customer had us come out to Miles to replace every window in her mom's house. Our office is at 1802 Pulliam Street, and we work in Abilene and Brownwood too. Another customer wrote that rain is rare here, and when it comes, it does a lot of damage to the yard, so we quoted seamless gutters for the front of the house. Seamless gutters run the length of the house in one piece, so there are fewer places to leak. When a gutter leaks, the water finds the openings in your paint and caulking, and over time it rots the wood.
3
The window salesman who talks you out of the upgrade
Competitor contrast
▾
The strongest contrast a local window company has is the salesman who talks a customer out of an upgrade. A real review says ours did, and the same site publishes the fine print most companies skip: the warranty wording and the credit card fee.
Spot C
Ask any window company whether the person selling to you will tell you what you don't need. A customer wrote that our salesman never pressured him into needless upgrades, and even talked him out of triple pane windows. He shopped around at a few local window companies, and he wrote that we had the best price. Get references from anybody you're considering, and ask to see their past work.
Spot A
How do they pick their products? Ours are hand picked for quality and value. What does the estimate cost? Ours is a free in-home consultation with a written estimate. What does the warranty say? Our window frames carry a written lifetime limited warranty, the Presidential series is a transferable lifetime warranty, and there's a glass breakage warranty too. And read the fine print. If you pay by credit card, there's a convenience fee added to the invoice.
Spot B
One customer wrote that our office walked them through the whole process of replacing their windows. Who calls you after the job? One customer wrote that Whitney called after the project to make sure he was happy. Another wrote that the crew cleaned the windows, took off the tags, and did some small repairs they didn't have to do. And find out who owns the company. I'm Charles Jolly, and we're locally owned and operated.
4
How to clean vinyl windows, and what to keep away from them
Useful customer education
▾
Every window buyer ends up asking how to take care of the windows, and the answers cost nothing to give. This one comes straight from the company's own FAQ and earns the follow from anyone who has ever stood at a streaky window with the wrong spray bottle.
Spot A
Vinyl windows are virtually maintenance free. They don't need painting or sealing, and they don't rot, rust, pit or corrode. When they get dirty, wiping them with a damp cloth is usually enough. For stubborn dirt, a few drops of mild liquid cleaner in a bucket of water does it.
Spot B
If you need something stronger, vinegar and water works, and so do Murphy's Oil Soap and Formula 409. Keep the Clorox, the Pine Power and the nail polish remover away from them. Thinking about painting your vinyl windows? There are paints made specifically for vinyl. Ask your paint supplier, and follow the manufacturer's instructions on the surface prep.
Spot C
And if a window leaks air, look at the sill. A crack or a gap there can make a bigger difference in your gas and electric bills than you'd think. Older windows that don't seal also keep a modern air conditioner from doing its job. Insulation is one of the most practical, cost effective ways to make a house more energy efficient. A 1990 University of Colorado study found homes insulated with cellulose or fiberglass used 26 percent less energy. If you want someone to look at your windows or your insulation, we do a free in-home consultation with a written estimate.
