The scripts

A first pass. I made some assumptions about your business to write these. On a shoot we'd dial them in to your goals.

1
Send me the photo and I'll match it
The origin and the face
▾
Cynthia's customers say she matches every inspiration photo and handles everything, but nobody scrolling past knows who she is or that she has been in San Angelo for over thirty years. This puts the face, the 2020 start and the full list of what Besos does into one story in her voice.
Spot A
Send me a photo of the party you're dreaming about. A customer wrote that we matched every picture they sent. I'm Cynthia Diego, and I started Besos Events here in San Angelo in 2020. I've been serving this city with my family for over thirty years. Life is made to be celebrated. That's the line on my website.
Spot B
Besos Events is a full-service event planner. We have a venue that seats up to a hundred guests. We rent decor, throne chairs, centerpieces, tablecloths, chair covers and more. And we sell event jewelry. Every piece is one of a kind. I search high and low for the most beautiful pieces for my clients. I started all of it because [OWNER: why you started Besos Events in 2020]. Before that, [OWNER: what you did before you started Besos Events]. Today you can come to one place for the room, the decor and the jewelry.
Spot C
We plan weddings, quinceañeras, graduation parties, baby showers and birthdays. One couple asked us for something at the last minute on their wedding, and we made it work. A parent told us we handled everything, so all they had to do was show up. My team checks in with you during your event, so you get to enjoy it. Send me the photo. I'd like to help you celebrate.
2
A venue on West Avenue N for San Angelo's big days
The local anchor
▾
Cynthia has lived and worked in San Angelo for over thirty years and runs a venue near South Randolph Street, so the biggest days of local families already happen in her room. This puts the address, the Chamber membership and the bilingual site on camera in her own words.
Spot B
We're at 319 West Avenue N, here in San Angelo, near South Randolph Street. Our venue seats up to a hundred guests. Baby showers, graduation parties, bridal showers, weddings and birthday parties all happen in this room. Before it was Besos Events, this building was [OWNER: what the building was before Besos Events]. We're a member of the San Angelo Chamber of Commerce. I've lived and worked in this town for over thirty years.
Spot A
So our website works in English and in Spanish. That way your whole family can read it, from the grandparents to the cousins. You can browse the decor, build a quote and send us your date right on the website. You can book an appointment with me there too. There's a gallery of real events we've done, so you can see our work before you book.
Spot C
A parent comes in with a daughter's graduation party. A couple comes in with a wedding and a short timeline. A family comes in with a baby shower and a picture of the table they want. Start with a date, a guest count and a photo. That's enough for me to start building your day. Tell me the date, and I'll tell you if the room is open. Come by 319 West Avenue N and see the room.
3
Ask any venue what it costs before you fall in love with it
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
Posted prices, payment plans and a plain refund line are all on Besos's own site, and most people never think to ask a venue for them before booking a tour. It draws the line in Cynthia's own words without naming anyone.
Spot C
Before you fall in love with a venue, ask what it costs, and ask what that price covers. I'll tell you mine right now. The venue by itself is seven hundred dollars. That includes access to the room for up to a hundred guests. The venue with our decor package is fifteen hundred. The decor package includes our premium setup and styling. Catering is an add on, and the price depends on your menu. Every package can be customized to fit your budget.
Spot A
Then ask how you can pay. We accept Klarna, Affirm, credit cards and all major payments. Ask any venue what payment options it takes. You can browse our rentals on the website and add what you like to a quote request. Ask about the quote, too. On my website you can pick your event, your guest count and your date, and it builds a package for you. On the booking page, you choose venue only or venue and decor, then add your event type, your date and your guest count. Final pricing comes after we talk.
Spot B
Last, ask about refunds. Mine is on my website in plain words. All sales are final. No refunds. I want you to hear that from me before you book. [OWNER: how you handle deposits and date changes for venue bookings]
4
How many tables, chair covers and centerpieces your party needs
The useful one
▾
The math on the shop's own quote builder is the same math any host can use, and it costs Cynthia nothing to give away. It also sends people to the quote builder on her site.
Spot A
Planning the decor for a party? Here's the math. Round tables seat eight. Take your guest count, divide by eight, and round up. That's how many tables you need. Our tablecloths come in round and rectangle sizes, so tell us which tables you're using. Say you have fifty guests. Fifty divided by eight is six and a quarter, so you need seven tables.
Spot B
Every table needs a tablecloth, a centerpiece and a table runner. Every chair needs a chair cover and a sash. So fifty guests means seven tablecloths, seven centerpieces and seven runners, and fifty chair covers and fifty sashes. Tablecloths come in satin, polyester and sequin. Chair covers come fitted or tie back. Sashes come in satin, organza and sequin. Now you know what to ask for when you call.
Spot C
Some events get extras. A quinceañera gets a throne chair for the guest of honor. A wedding or a bridal shower gets two. A wedding or a quinceañera can also have a custom flower arch. If you want us to build the list for you, use the quote builder on my website. Pick your event, your guest count and your date. Final pricing comes after we talk, because every event is different. We'll take it from there.
