1
Why we only build fences
The origin and the face
▾
The site says the company only installs fences and does no roofing, plumbing, tree trimming or landscaping, and has done so since 1998. Hearing the reason from the person who made that choice is the best proof for a customer comparing contractors.
Spot A
I'm [OWNER: your name], and I own Rockin' J Fence here in San Angelo. We've been building fences in this town since 1998. We're locally owned and operated, twenty eight years now. [OWNER: how you got into fence building] There's a line on our website that I stand behind. We're a master of one. We build fences.
Spot B
We install residential and commercial fences, and that's the whole company. We don't build portable buildings. We don't roof houses, trim trees, mow yards, or do landscaping. We don't repair or install plumbing. Our job is to take the fence you have now and turn it into the one you wanted. [OWNER: why you decided to do only fences] When you do one thing for twenty eight years, you learn it. You learn what holds up in the sun and the weather around here. [OWNER: one thing about West Texas that changed how you build]
Spot C
We do wood, ornamental iron, chain link, and metal privacy fences. We stain fences, and we put in automatic gates. All of our work comes with a one year limited labor warranty, and some products carry a manufacturer's warranty on top of that. We'll take your old, dilapidated fence and build the one you want, with no effort on your part. We use cedar in all the legal heights, including clear cedar with no knots. Our ornamental iron is all steel and American made, with a twenty year limited warranty from the manufacturer. Call us for a quote. I'm [OWNER: your name], with Rockin' J Fence.

3
Look at the rails on a metal privacy fence
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
The site says what sets its metal fences apart: square tubing rails in place of the traditional c-perlin, hidden screws and gates that swing a full 180 degrees. Anyone shopping for a metal fence can check each one themselves, which makes it a fair test that needs no competitor named.
Spot C
If you're buying a metal privacy fence, look at the rails. The traditional rail is shaped like a C, and it's called a c-perlin. Water and debris collect in the bottom of the C, and over time that can rust the metal. On a brand new fence, everything looks fine. The screws that hold the sheet metal on can show from the back side of the fence too.
Spot A
We build ours on square tubing. Water can't get inside it. The screws are hidden, so the back of your fence looks as clean as the front. Our frame is all galvanized material, which cuts maintenance down for a very long time. We offer many color combinations and all the legal heights. So ask what the frame is made of. Ask what the rails are. Ask how the gate swings.
Spot B
Then look at the gate, because you use it every day. A lot of metal privacy fences leave big gaps around the gate. Ours are built to swing a full one hundred eighty degrees, without those gaps. [OWNER: how a customer reacts when they see the gate for the first time] Ask whoever you hire to show you the rail and the gate on a finished fence before you sign. I'm [OWNER: your name], and you're welcome to ask us the same.
4
When to stain your cedar fence
The useful one
▾
Every San Angelo homeowner with a cedar fence has wondered when to stain it. The site explains the stain the company makes and how often to reapply it, so the useful part is already written and the call to action is a quote.
Spot A
A cedar fence looks great the first year. Then the sun and the weather go to work on it. [OWNER: what the sun and wind do to a fence in San Angelo, in your experience] Raw wood loses its natural sap and oils, and that's when you start to see cupping, warping, splitting, and shrinking. A good stain puts those oils back. Ours has a resin that follows the cell structure of the wood.
Spot B
Try a simple test. Splash some water on the boards. If it beads up, the wood still has some protection. If it soaks right in, it's time to stain. Our stain is meant to be reapplied every three to five years. Check your sprinklers too. Those ugly water arcs on a fence come from sprinkler heads hitting the boards. Aim them away from the wood.
Spot C
We make our own stain, called ACA Premium Fence Stain. It's a penetrating, oil based stain with Trans Oxide pigments, which last longer. It has fungicides, mildewcides, and ultraviolet inhibitors in it. One application will essentially double the life of your fence. We cut it with a food grade oil that's also used in suntan lotion. [OWNER: how long a stain job on an average backyard fence takes] On a new fence, we pre-dip the boards. On an older fence, we come out and stain it in the field. We restore somewhat older fences too. Call us for a quote.
