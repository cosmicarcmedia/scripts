Who is sewing your wedding dress?
The thing the big competitor will not say

4
What to bring to your wedding dress fitting
The useful one
▾
Brides often arrive without their shoes or undergarments, and many start late. Giving away the usual fitting timeline costs nothing, helps any bride in town, and puts her name on the answer.
Spot A
If you've got a wedding dress that needs altering, here's what to bring to the fitting. Start by bringing your wedding shoes. The hem length depends on how high your shoes are. Then bring the undergarments you're going to wear on the day, including shapewear. If you're wearing a veil or other accessories, bring those too. If you've got ideas for changes, bring photos.
Spot B
Here's the usual timeline. You start eight to twelve weeks before the wedding. At the first fitting, the hem, the bust, and the side seams get checked and pinned. At the second fitting, about four to six weeks out, the major alterations are done, and you check the work. That's when you can pin in a bustle, if you're getting one. [OWNER: how many weeks ahead you want brides to call you, and how many fittings you usually do]
Spot C
The last fitting is one to two weeks out. It's for the final polish: last adjustments, steaming, and pressing. Bring the person who'll help you on the wedding day. [OWNER: whether you do bustles, and whether you teach the person helping the bride] Some brides book one more fitting the day before or the day of the wedding. It helps with last minute steaming and adjustments, and with any change in weight during the last week. If your dress is arriving late, or your timeline is tight, call me anyway. I've fit in brides on short notice before. My number is 325 450 9829, and I'm at 210 North Harrison Street.
