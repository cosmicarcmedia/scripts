1
I run Mina's Nice & Clean, and it's a family business
Origin
▾
A cleaning company is a company you let into your house, so the person behind it matters more than the product. The script gives William the name and the proof, and leaves two marked slots for what only he knows: the year he started and who Mina is.
Spot A
I'm William Harris, and I run Mina's Nice & Clean here in San Angelo. We started in [OWNER: the year you started]. [OWNER: who Mina is, and why your cleaning company carries her name] We're a family business, and we're locally owned, which gives us a personal stake in our reputation in this town.
Spot B
Our crew speaks English and Spanish, so you and the person in your kitchen can always talk. Nobody on that crew is a subcontractor. They work for us. We're fully licensed and insured. Every job starts with a free estimate, and the plan is tailored to your house. We guarantee our work and aim to get it right the first time, and you can usually have us at your door within two to three business days.
Spot C
Houses, offices, windows, gutters and post-construction cleanup are all on our list. We're open Monday through Friday, eight to five, and Saturdays by appointment. Our neighbors on Nextdoor named us a Neighborhood Favorite in 2023, 2024 and 2025. We've got 208 reviews on Google, and they average 4.7 stars. And we're hiring experienced housekeepers right now, because we want to keep this one of the strongest cleaning staffs in Tom Green County.

3
Ask who is walking through your house, and who they work for
Competitor contrast
▾
Anyone can promise a spotless house. The questions here, who works for the company, what the cancellation fee is, who brings the supplies, make a viewer test the fine print of any cleaning company in town, and the answers on this company's side are all on its own site.

When you hire a cleaning company, ask who is going to be walking through your house. And ask who they work for. At Mina's, every person on the crew is trained by us and part of our company. We never use subcontractors, so every house gets the same standard, and you've got one place to call if something's wrong. Ask to see their insurance certificate, too. We're fully licensed and insured.

We show up with professional grade supplies and equipment, and if you've got a product you like or one you can't use, we work around it. Ask what the estimate costs. Ours is free.
Spot B
And ask what happens if you're unhappy with the job, or if somebody has to come back to fix it. We guarantee our work, and we aim to get it right the first time. One customer wrote that the crew arrived early and did a very detailed cleaning. Another wrote that they scrubbed the shower floor, cleaned behind the refrigerator and cleaned inside the microwave. 4
Standard clean or deep clean, what you're paying for
Useful customer education
▾
The most common cleaning complaint is a mismatch between the clean someone expected and the clean they booked. Explaining the difference costs nothing, and everything in it comes straight from the company's own service pages and FAQ.
Spot A
A standard clean and a deep clean are two different jobs, and you're paying for one of them. Which one did you book? A standard clean covers dusting, vacuuming the carpets, mopping the floors, cleaning the bathrooms, wiping down the kitchen and taking out the trash. We also go over the things everybody touches, like doorknobs, light switches and countertops. A deep clean goes further: baseboards, vents, detailed work in the kitchen and the bathrooms, and the hard to reach spaces where buildup hides.
Spot B
We also clean ovens, refrigerators and cabinets. If you want those, say so when you book, because we customize every plan. How often should you have it done? Many homeowners pick weekly or every other week. Businesses usually need it daily, or several times a week. You don't need to buy anything. We bring the supplies and the equipment.
Spot C
The outside of the house gets overlooked, too. When gutters clog, water can't flow freely, and it can damage your roof, your siding and your foundation. We inspect them, clear out the debris, and flush the downspouts. For windows that are hard to reach we use a water-fed pole, and we clean the screens and the frames while we're at it.
