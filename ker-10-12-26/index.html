3
Ask your roofer if he'll still be here when the warranty matters
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
Longevity, certifications, insurance claim help and a written inspection report are all on your own pages, and laid out together they let a homeowner see the difference between a company and a storm season visitor without naming anyone.
Spot C
Before you sign with any roofer, ask one question. Where will you be when I need that warranty? Some roofers follow the hail. They show up after a storm, and they leave when the work does. A warranty is a promise from a company, and the company has to still be around. We've been putting roofs on San Angelo since 1966. If you call about a roof we put on, you get the same name. Ask about the warranty options for your roof before work starts.
Spot A
Then ask about the paperwork. We're bonded and insured, and we're GAF certified. We give you a clear repair estimate before any work begins. If you have a storm damage claim, we help with the insurance side. One of our customers wrote that Rudy Garza stayed in touch through the whole process and helped when they had problems with the insurance company.
Spot B
Ask about the end of the job, too. Is somebody cleaning up your yard? Three of the four reviews on our About page mention the cleanup. And ask what the financing looks like. We offer twelve month, zero percent financing through First Financial Bank, with approved credit. I'd rather you ask all of it up front.
4
What a real roof inspection should include
The useful one
▾
This is the inspection you already do, laid out step by step, and giving it away shows homeowners exactly what to ask for from anyone who comes up their ladder.
Spot A
If a roofer tells you that you need a new roof, ask to see the photos. A real inspection ends with a written report, and the report has pictures in it. Get one after hail, high wind, or heavy rain. Here's what ours looks like, start to finish. It starts with a conversation. We ask what's bothering you, and we ask what you know about the roof's history.
Spot B
Then we go up and check the surface. Cracked, loose, or missing shingles. Damaged flashing. Soft spots. Any place the roof sags. We also look for leaks around the vents. Then we come inside, when it's needed. We check the attic and the ceilings for water stains, moisture, mold, ventilation problems, and insulation. A leak can be working on your attic long before you see it from the street.
Spot C
Last, you get the report. Photographs of what we found, and what we recommend. If it's a small repair, we'll say so. A good inspection catches problems before they turn into expensive repairs. And we can help with your insurance claim questions. Whoever you hire, ask them for those four things. A conversation, the roof, the inside, and the report. Our inspections are free, and you get the report.
