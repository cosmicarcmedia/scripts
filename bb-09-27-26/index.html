A swimmer, a saleswoman, and a moving truck to San Angelo
The origin and the face
▾
Tells the real story of an SMU swimmer turned software salesman and the woman who left her own Dallas career to build this with him, since almost none of that history is visible anywhere on the current site or socials.
Spot A
Before there was a Budget Blinds on Austin Street, there was a software salesman who swam for SMU. Ten years in high tech sales, and Brian wanted something different. Something where the work was still standing in front of a real person's living room.
Spot B
Suzan felt the same pull. She left her own career in Dallas to make the move with him. [OWNER: what Suzan's career in Dallas was before the two of you started this] Two thousand three. That's when the doors opened here in San Angelo. Twenty three years later, we're still the same two people who signed that first lease. Not a corporate buyout. Not new owners every few years. Same couple, same shop, same street. That decision turned into something real. This location has ranked a top ten Budget Blinds franchise in the country more than once, and design press has recognized it with a Best of Houzz award.
Spot C
Brian still says it the same way he did back then. A style and a smile for any budget. Nobody in marketing wrote that slogan for us. That's just how Brian talks. Twenty three years in, Brian and Suzan still walk into the same kinds of houses they walked into in 2003, just with a different generation answering the door.
2
Twenty three years on Austin Street
The local anchor
▾
Grounds 23 years of business inside one address and this town's own institutions, Goodfellow, Habitat for San Angelo, the Standard-Times, the kind of roots a franchise location rarely gets to have.
Spot B
We've been at the same address on Austin Street since 2003. Twenty three years, same shop. Goodfellow brings new families into this town every few months, and a lot of them call us in their first week here. New house, bare windows, and a base schedule that doesn't wait around. We've been accredited with the Better Business Bureau since 2008, and got named a finalist for their Business of the Year the year after that.
Spot C
We've also spent real time with Habitat for San Angelo, and we show up around events out at the state park. Habitat build days, park cleanup events, the school fundraiser table. This is a small enough town that the same names keep showing up in the same places. Ours has been on Austin Street the whole time. When a customer calls about a broken cord or a stuck motor, a neighbor picks up the phone, never a call center in another state.
Spot A
The San Angelo Standard Times voted us best in window coverings three years running, 2013, 2014 and 2015. That's a vote from the people who read the local paper. That's three separate years the readers came back and picked us again. Twenty three years in the same town will do that.
3
Why we still measure your windows ourselves
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
Contrasts the free in person measure and install against ordering blinds online and hoping the measurement holds, without naming a specific competitor. This is the one most likely to get forwarded by anyone who has been burned by a bad online order.
Spot C
You can order blinds online in about ninety seconds. Type in a width, type in a height, and hope you measured right. Get it wrong by a quarter inch and that shade either won't close or won't fit at all. Now you're shipping it back and starting over.
Spot A
We still send somebody out to measure every window ourselves, free, before you order anything. In home or virtual, whichever works for your schedule. Either way, a real person looks at your actual windows before anything gets ordered. Same person who measures it is usually the one who installs it. If something's off, we're the ones who fix it, in person.
Spot B
We're locally owned, and we're also backed by a national warranty, five years on most products, longer on some lines. Most of what we install carries that five year warranty with no questions asked. Our Enlightened Style line carries a lifetime warranty on top of that. A big box store can sell you the blinds. It cannot come back out to your house when the cord breaks two years in. A width and a height typed into a website cannot tell you any of that. A person standing in your living room can.
4
The three questions that pick your window covering
The useful one
▾
Gives away the actual decision framework customers need before they ever set foot in a showroom, at zero cost to the business, which is exactly the kind of content that earns trust before the sales conversation starts.
Spot B
Most people walk in asking for blinds when what they need is a shade, or the other way around. Here are the three questions that settle it fast. First, is the sun coming in low and straight, or high and bright? If it's harsh and direct, a solar shade or a roller shade cuts the glare without blacking out the whole room.
Spot A
Second, are there kids or pets in that room who are going to pull on a cord? If the answer's yes, you want cordless or motorized. We carry both across nearly every style we sell. That's the safer option every time, no upselling involved. Third, is this a rental, a forever home, or somewhere in between? For a rental, vinyl or faux wood blinds do the job without a big investment. For a forever home, that's when shutters or a motorized shade start to make sense.
Spot C
[OWNER: a specific example of a job where getting this wrong cost a customer money, if you have one you can share] Answer those three honestly and you'll skip the thing that sends most people back to remeasure and reorder. We'll walk you through all three for free, whether you buy from us or not.
