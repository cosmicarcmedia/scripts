1
Why I named my practice Atlas
The origin and the face
▾
A name like Atlas makes people wonder who is behind it. This puts Dr. Hollingsworth's face and twenty years of experience in front of someone who is deciding whether to call, and it says what the practice stands for before any sales pitch.
Spot A
I'm Dr. Lori Hollingsworth. I'm a licensed professional counselor supervisor here in San Angelo, and I've been doing this work for more than twenty years. I named my practice Atlas on purpose. In the old Greek stories, Atlas is the one holding up the sky. To a lot of people, he's a symbol of endurance, resilience, and steadiness. [OWNER: why that image mattered to you when you opened the practice] That idea runs through everything we do here.
Spot B
People come to us carrying something heavy. Trauma. Grief and loss. A hard stretch after having a baby. Those are the things I focus on in my own work. I also supervise other counselors, which is what the S in my license stands for. Our mission is to stand with you during life's most difficult moments. That's the sentence we hang the whole practice on.
Spot C
We see children, teens, adults, and families. My work is helping people find their own strength and build resilience. Everyone at Atlas works from that idea, with a personalized plan for each person, and a trauma-informed approach. If you're wondering whether this is the right place, you can start with a phone call. Our number is 325 656 5260. Or you can email me, lori.hollingsworth at atlascounselingtrauma dot com. Either one works.
2
The next counselors in San Angelo are training right here
The local anchor
▾
San Angelo has a university that trains counselors, and Atlas gives those graduate students supervised clinical hours. For a local audience this is a story about the town's own future mental health workforce, and it explains the thirty five dollar option without sounding like a discount ad.
Spot B
There's a university in this town that trains counselors. Angelo State. Two of the clinicians on our team are graduate students there. One is finishing a master's degree in counseling, and one is working toward a doctorate in counseling psychology. One plans to focus on trauma counseling, including domestic violence and assault. The other has experience with anxiety, OCD, and autism.
Spot C
They're here learning the work in front of real people, with real supervision. Every session with a counseling intern is supervised by a licensed clinician. I'm one of them, and supervision is something I care about a lot. That's how the next generation of counselors in this community gets ready. And one of our counselors retired after twenty-seven years in the public schools. She now works with children, teens, and adults.
Spot A
It also means we can offer low-cost counseling. Sessions with a master's level intern are thirty five dollars. They're full-length sessions, and no insurance is required. [OWNER: confirm the thirty five dollar rate is current] If cost has been the thing keeping you from calling someone, ask about it when you call. call us and ask about it. We also offer support for first responders. Our number is 325 656 5260. We list every clinician by name on our website.
3
Three things to ask before you pick a therapist
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
Teletherapy apps are the alternative most people see advertised, and a local practice can answer the three questions people forget to ask. This is the sharpest of the four because every line is a published fact about Atlas, and it makes the viewer compare without a name ever being said.
Spot C
If you're looking for a therapist, I'd ask three things before you book anything. First, who exactly will I be seeing? Second, what will it cost me, per session? Third, will I know that price before my first appointment?
Spot A
I'll answer those for us. At Atlas, you can read about every clinician on our website. Their names, their training, and what they focus on. Our fees are posted, session by session. An intake with a licensed clinician is one fifty-five, and a follow-up is one thirty-five. [OWNER: confirm the current fee schedule before this airs]
Spot B
And before your first appointment, you get what's called a good faith estimate. It's a form that lays out what you can expect to pay. We go over it with you in the phone consultation and again in the first session. We also accept most major insurance providers. Ask me anything you want about how it works. Call the office and ask about your coverage. I want you to know what you're agreeing to before you walk in the door. That's just good practice. You can find us at the number 325 656 5260. You deserve to know who's on the other side of that room, and what it will cost, before you ever sit down.
4
What happens when you call a counseling office for the first time
The useful one
▾
The biggest barrier to a first call is not knowing what will happen. This walks the viewer through the real Atlas process step by step with no clinical claims, which keeps it compliant and gives the audience a reason to save it.
Spot A
If you've never called a counseling office, here's what happens. You call, or you send an email. Our intake coordinator sets up a thirty-minute consultation. That conversation is about understanding what you need.
Spot B
After that, we match you with a therapist. Your therapist reaches out to schedule your first session. In that first session, you talk about what brought you in. You explore your concerns, you set some goals, and you make a plan together. Sessions vary based on your goals and your needs. How long therapy lasts depends on you. [OWNER: what a first session usually feels like, in your own words]
Spot C
Before you ever come in, you'll get the good faith estimate form. That tells you what to expect on cost. You can ask for your records later if you need them, and Texas law backs you on that. If you or someone you love is in crisis right now, please call 988. That's the national Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, and it's there at any hour. Everyone else, the first step is a phone call. Take your time with the decision. Ours is 325 656 5260. You don't have to have it all figured out when you call. The consultation is where we start figuring it out together. Ask whatever you like on that call.
