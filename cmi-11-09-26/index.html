1
In 1982 a CPA in Denver started an auto parts company
The origin and the face
▾
A CPA and Army veteran who started a parts importer in Denver and switched to making dash covers is the founder story on the company's own history page, and on camera it gives the plant a person and a reason to trust the part.
In 1982, a CPA in Denver started an auto parts company. Why would an accountant do that? Our founder, Paul Cornwell, came at it the long way around. He was an Army infantry officer first. A Vietnam veteran, a major by the time he finished. Then he went back to college, studied accounting at the University of Colorado, and became a CPA. During those years he worked for Goodyear and for Cadillac. He knew the auto industry, and he saw an opening. He and a partner started a company called Import Auto Products. It brought in auto parts for foreign cars. Then they noticed what people kept needing. Dash covers. Foreign cars, American cars, it didn't matter. Dashboards crack and bubble, and the need was huge. So the importers became manufacturers, and they started making dash covers themselves. [OWNER: what the first Coverlay dash cover looked like, or the story you were told about it] The Coverlay name caught on across the auto industry. In 1994, the company took it as its own name. Door panels came next, then kick panels, armrests and door inserts. These days the line has instrument panel covers and dash vent covers too. Our mission is to design, make and provide high quality auto parts that are affordable and easy to install. [OWNER: your name, your role, and what brought you to Coverlay] We've been at it since 1982.
2
Snow is the reason a Denver plant ended up in San Angelo
The local anchor
▾
Snow is a surprising reason for a plant to land in San Angelo, and it lets every customer from Ohio to British Columbia picture where the part in their truck was made, on Highway 67 North.
Snow is the reason Coverlay is in San Angelo. After about ten years in Colorado, our founder decided to move the plant. Snow delays and other bad weather meant downtime. San Angelo is known for a warm, dry climate, so that's where he went. The plant moved into a newly built facility on Highway 67 North. We're still here. Every one of our products is made in San Angelo and shipped from San Angelo. We start with a tough ABS plastic and vacuum mold it on our own molds. Then it ships wherever your truck is. You can order from us online or by phone, or through a stocking dealer. Our customer service reps work right here at the factory. In the past few years, customers have written to us from Ohio, Pennsylvania, California, Oregon, Georgia, and right here in Texas. Two of them wrote from British Columbia. One customer from Nanaimo, British Columbia, picked his up at our facility. He wrote that he was glad he got to see our beautiful city. Another, from Penticton, wrote that his door panels fit perfectly and let him finish his truck. [OWNER: what a visitor sees when they walk in, and whether people can stop by] A cracked dash in Ohio gets fixed with a part made on Highway 67 North. That's where your order ships from.
3
Ask whoever sells you a dash cover what happens when it cracks
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
Reading the warranty exclusions out loud, color and shipping included, turns a page of the company's own FAQ into a straight answer, and it leaves every other seller to answer the same question.
Ask whoever sells you a dash cover one question. What happens if it cracks? Our limited lifetime warranty says that when a Coverlay dash cover is installed properly, it won't warp or crack for the life of your vehicle. If one does fail, we replace it in the color you ordered. Color isn't covered by the warranty. We have fifteen colors to choose from, and we can't guarantee an exact match to your dash. Shipping on a replacement isn't covered either. If a part shows up defective, tell us within thirty days. After that, we can't cover a part that was never installed. We may ask you for photos. A Coverlay dash cover lays over your original dash and covers the damage, and the old dash stays underneath. If your color is a little off, there's a fix. Take your vehicle's color code to an automotive paint store. Ask for a spray can of automotive vinyl spray mixed to that code. Then paint the cover. One customer in Florida wrote that the carmaker wanted over two thousand dollars to put a new dash in her convertible. She wrote that she could put ours in for under two hundred. Read the fine print on anything you buy for that dash. Start with ours.
4
You don't have to pull a cracked dash out to fix it
The useful one
▾
Every line comes from the company's own FAQ and install notes, so it costs nothing to give away, and it answers the first thing a pickup owner with a cracked dash wonders: whether the whole dash has to come out.
Your truck's dash is cracked, so you figure the whole dash has to come out. It doesn't. A dash cover goes right over the dash you already have. It's a new skin for your original dash. You clean the surface, then lay the cover over the damage. Most owners finish in thirty minutes or less. Chevy and GMC pickups, Toyotas, Dodge Rams, Fords, we have covers for a lot of trucks. The glue is a clear RTV silicone, and it goes on the underside of the cover. We supply the adhesive and a set of instructions. A man in Ohio sent us before and after photos of his 1989 Toyota pickup. Three hundred thousand miles on it. The old dash is still under there, with a new skin on top. Door panels work differently. Those are full replacements, and they ship as a set, never one at a time. The part that trips people up is cleaning the cover afterward. Wipe it with an ammonia based cleaner like Windex and a soft rag, or use mild soap and water. Skip the Armor All type shine products. They can soften the plastic and cause warping. We have install videos on our website for dozens of vehicles. If your dash looks like a dry lake bed, pick your year, make and model on our site and see what fits.
