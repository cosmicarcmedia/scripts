Questions.

How is the deposit refundable?

Email hello@powerflag.example from the address you reserved with and we refund the full deposit to the card you paid with. You can do that any time before the final production price and ship date are confirmed. After that, you can still cancel for a full refund if the price or timeline changes materially. The reservation terms have the details.

What flags fit?

The design target is a 2 by 3 foot flag. US, state, team or business flags all fit as long as the size is right. The housing carries no branding, so the flag you fly is the only thing anyone sees.

When will it ship?

The target is ahead of Memorial Day 2027. That date depends on tooling and production, so it is a target. We will email you as it moves, and if it slips materially you can cancel for a full refund.

How does it handle wind and weather?

We have not published a wind or weather rating, and we will not until testing on production units supports one. The housing is sealed and the flag stows inside it. We will publish what the tests show before units ship.
