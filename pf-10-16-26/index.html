How it works.

At dawn. The doors open and stay open. The arm pivots at the foot of the column and swings out from the wall with the flag still rolled on the pole. Once the arm is horizontal, the doors close above it and the flag unrolls. Then the arm lifts and the flag flies at an angle.

At dusk. The arm drops back to horizontal and the doors open. The flag rolls up as the arm rises back into the column, and it keeps rolling the whole way. Then the doors close.

The pole. It is slender. The flag furls around it, so the pole and the rolled flag both fit inside the sealed column.

By hand. A 315 MHz remote lets you raise or lower the flag whenever you want.

Solar, no wiring.

A solar panel sits flush in the angled cap and charges a sealed battery. The design target is about 27 days of operation with no sun at all. Nothing plugs in.

What flags fit?

The design target is a 2 by 3 foot flag. US, state, team or business flags all fit as long as the size is right. The housing carries no branding, so the flag you fly is the only thing anyone sees.

How does it handle wind and weather?

We have not published a wind or weather rating, and we will not until testing on production units supports one. The housing is sealed and the flag stows inside it. We will publish what the tests show before units ship.

It mounts to a wall, porch column or post, with no wiring and no electrician. That is the design target. The full installation guide and hardware list come before shipping, and reservation holders see them first.
