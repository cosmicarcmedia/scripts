Privacy

Last updated October 2026.

What we collect

If you join the launch list, we collect your email address. If you send a multi-unit inquiry, we collect your business name, the number of locations and your email address. If you reserve a unit, Stripe collects your payment details and name and gives us the order information we need to fulfill it. We never see your full card number.

How we use it

To email you when reservations open, to send build updates, to handle your reservation and any refund, and to reply to your inquiry. We do not sell your information.

Who handles it

Payments run through Stripe. Form submissions and the site itself are handled by our hosting provider. If we turn on analytics or advertising measurement, such as Google Analytics or the Meta Pixel, those services receive information about how you use the site, and we will list them here.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email hello@powerflag.example.
