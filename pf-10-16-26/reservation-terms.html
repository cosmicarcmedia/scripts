Reservation terms

Last updated October 2026. These terms apply to the PowerFlag Founders Reservation.

What you pay today

A deposit of $49. No full payment is taken now. Payment is processed by Stripe.

Refunds

The deposit is fully refundable at any time before the final production price and ship date are confirmed. It is also fully refundable after that if the price or timeline changes materially from what was shown when you reserved.

To request a refund, email hello@powerflag.example from the address you reserved with. We refund the full deposit to the payment method you used.

Your deposit applies to the purchase

When your unit is ready to order, your deposit is credited toward the purchase price.

Founder price cap

Each founder tier has a price cap. No founder pays more than the price of the tier they reserved in. If production costs force the price above that cap, you may cancel for a full refund. Tier prices and sizes are shown on the reservation offer and are provisional until we confirm final costs.

Status and targets

PowerFlag is in engineering. The images on this site are product renders, and the final unit may vary slightly. Dates are targets: deposits are targeted to open on Nov 11, 2026, and first units are targeted to ship ahead of Memorial Day 2027. Specifications are design targets, and final specifications are confirmed before shipping.

Updates

We will email reservation holders progress updates on the build, and first access to installation instructions and setup information.

Who we are

PowerFlag is a product of AlCor Industries LLC, San Angelo, TX. Patent pending. Questions go to hello@powerflag.example.
