The Johnson family

Johnson's Pest Control has been family owned and operated since 1981. Here's how it started.

1974 Bandera

In March, Robert Johnson goes to work as a technician for Texas State Pest Control in Bandera.

1977 Junction

Texas State buys Hodges Pest Control of Menard. Robert, a certified applicator by then, agrees to move out to the Junction area, and he and Janice settle there with their two sons, Kevin and Joshua. The route covers Junction, Sonora and Menard, and soon Rocksprings.

1981 The route becomes ours

The owner of Texas State falls ill and offers each technician the chance to buy his route. On August 1, 1981, Robert buys his, and Johnson's Pest Control is born.

Janice earns her own Certified Applicator license, works the field beside Robert and runs the office. Kevin and Joshua ride along in the summers and on Saturdays.

1991 The boys come back

Both sons earn technician licenses after high school, try other lines of work, and come back whenever their parents need them. In 1991 Kevin's wife, Sharlotte, joins the office.

2006 1922 North Main

The office moves out of the Johnson family home and into its own place at 1922 North Main Street in Junction.

2012 Handing it down

In the fall, Robert and Janice start bringing their sons further into the business, and ease into semi-retirement.

2013 The chain is complete

In July, Josh's wife, Samantha, joins the office, and the family business chain is complete.

Today Same family, bigger map

Josh and Sharlotte Johnson run the company as co-owners. Samantha keeps the office moving, and five licensed technicians cover twelve counties of the Hill Country.
