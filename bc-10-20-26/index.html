The scripts

1
It started in Robert Lee in 1947
The origin and the face
▾
Nobody dropping off a shirt at a San Angelo counter knows the family behind it goes back to one cleaner in Robert Lee, or that it once pressed jeans by the thousand. That history is the reason to trust the people holding your clothes.
Spot A
My family has been cleaning clothes in West Texas since 1947. I'm Danny Bahlman, and my wife Kande and I own the Bahlman Cleaners stores in San Angelo, Sonora, and Ozona. [OWNER: how you are related to Melvin and Leon Bahlman] It started with Melvin Bahlman, who bought his first cleaners in Robert Lee, Texas. Sweetwater came in 1958, and Winters after that.
Spot B
In 1966, we opened our first store in San Angelo. In 1978, Melvin's brother Leon bought the San Angelo stores, after many years as the general manager. And in 1980, we signed a contract with Levi Strauss to pre wash and press up to ten thousand pairs of jeans a day. Ten thousand pairs. Every day. [OWNER: a story from that jeans contract that the family still tells]
Spot C
The family kept growing, to Odessa, Sonora, and Ozona. We do contract cleaning out to Eldorado, Mertzon, and Sterling City. Today we're a full service dry cleaner and laundry, and we do alterations too. Same day service. Drop it off by nine, pick it up by five. We care for formal wear too, including wedding and quinceanera dresses. We belong to the San Angelo, Sonora, and Ozona Chambers of Commerce. Our motto is that Bahlman Cleaners cares, and it shows in all your clothes. It's been seventy nine years, and the family is still the one running it.
2
Starting at the five way
The local anchor
▾
A street corner and a list of neighborhoods will make a San Angelo viewer say that's my route. It ties seventy years of history to the stores and the free pickup people can use this week.
Spot B
In 1966, we opened our first San Angelo store at the five way intersection of Van Buren, Ward, and Beauregard. If you've driven through that five way, you've passed where we started. Three years later, in 1969, we opened a second store on 19th Street. Today our north store is at 101 West 19th. Now there are stores on Knickerbocker, Southland, and Sunset too.
Spot C
Every San Angelo store does same day service. Drop it off by nine in the morning, pick it up by five, Monday through Friday. And the San Angelo stores are open Saturday mornings. Out in the county, our Sonora and Ozona stores take your clothes in by nine and have them ready the next day. We also do contract cleaning for Eldorado, Mertzon, and Sterling City.
Spot A
Live in Bentwood, Butler Farms, Country Club Lake Estates, Pecan Valley, or Santa Rita? Then we come to you, and it is free. We pick up on Monday and bring it back on Thursday. You sign up on our website, and we confirm it with you. You get a garment bag for the dirty clothes and a hanging system for the clean ones. Everything comes back in a protective bag. If your neighborhood isn't on that list, call 325 653 6112 and ask.
3
Dry cleaning is one of three ways we clean your clothes
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
Most customers think one process handles everything. Naming the three methods, what goes where, and the things that can go wrong shows the shop explaining itself, which is hard to find at a counter.
Spot C
Ask your cleaner one question. How are you going to clean this? Here, there are three answers. Dry cleaning uses a petroleum based solvent, with no water at all. That's for the delicate things. Rayon, silk, and wool. Every cleaning here includes pressing.
Spot A
Wet cleaning uses water with specialized detergents at controlled temperatures. That's for polo shirts, khakis, polyester, jackets, and hand wash items. Laundry uses water with computer formulated chemical blends, then a hot head press. That's for cotton, cotton blends, linen, and denim. So your polo and your silk blouse get different treatment. We have over sixty five years of experience, and we keep training with DLI.
Spot B
The FTC requires a care label to list at least one safe way to clean the garment. If a label says spot clean only, we will recommend a different method and ask for your OK first. Spot cleaning isn't something we recognize, because it can leave rings or shift the color. Pressing in laundry can weaken buttons, and pearl snaps are the most vulnerable. Some trim can't survive dry cleaning, so we take it off and replace it. We don't do wash and fold, and we don't do undergarments. We'd rather tell you that up front. If you ask for a method different from the label, we may have you sign a release form first. Your email and cell number are for ready notices and care questions. We don't share them or sell them.
4
Please don't treat that stain yourself
The useful one
▾
A plain tip that comes straight from the shop's own FAQ: stop pre-treating, and tell the counter. It saves customers a ruined shirt and shows how the people behind the counter think.
Spot A
Got a stain on a shirt? Don't pre-treat it. Tell us about it instead. Home treatments can set a stain, and home detergents can fade the color or break down the fabric. Spot cleaning at home can leave rings or shift the color. Tell our customer service team what it is, and what happened. We've been doing this since 1947, and we can't treat a stain we don't know about. Tell us about any allergies or skin sensitivities too. Every load gets its own mix of solutions, and reactions are rare.
Spot B
Some stains you can't see at all. We call those invisible stains. Oils, sugary spills that caramelize, and alcohol can all dry clear. Then they show up after cleaning. If that happens, we will treat it and re-clean it when we can. So if you spilled a drink, tell us, even if you can't see anything.
Spot C
Here is how to store your clean clothes at home. Keep them in a dark, cool closet. Take the plastic bags off when you hang them. Fur goes in a breathable bag. Wool needs mothproof bags, or a cedar closet if your storage isn't treated for pests. Sweaters come back fold and hang, so there are no dimples or stretching. And if you forget a garment, we keep it up to a year and send you reminders by email and text.
