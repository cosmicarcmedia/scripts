The scripts

A first pass. I made some assumptions about your business to write these. On a shoot we'd dial them in to your goals.

1
From waiting tables at fourteen to Cafe Annie, and home again
The origin and the face
▾
Most people in San Angelo see a supper club on Knickerbocker Road and have no idea the chef behind it worked in Houston kitchens, at Cafe Annie, and for Chip and Joanna Gaines. Her own About page tells that story, and this puts it on camera in her voice.
Spot A
I started waiting tables at fourteen. Yes, you could work that young back then. By sixteen I was managing the front of the house. Then I got curious about what happened behind the kitchen door, so I went to culinary school in Houston in 2000. I cooked for caterers there. Private homes, museum galas, University of Houston sports events. Then I landed a spot as assistant pastry chef at Cafe Annie.
Spot B
From 2016 to 2020 I worked for Chip and Joanna Gaines. I had recipes developed for two of Jo's cookbooks. I consulted on start up projects like Silos Baking Co, Magnolia Table, and Press Coffee Co. Menu design, kitchen layouts, equipment. I've run small kitchens and big ones as a kitchen manager. I've worked as a food buyer for retail goods, too. [OWNER: your favorite memory from those years] I've also food styled for Texas Monthly and the Magnolia Journal.
Spot C
But my family has been in the San Angelo area for seven generations. [OWNER: when you came back to San Angelo and why] So now I run The Plated Dish on Knickerbocker Road. Monthly supper clubs, wine dinners, weddings, private events, and catering. I've run a private catering business for years, all over the state of Texas. I've been in hospitality for over thirty years, and my favorite part is still the same. Serving people, and making memories around a table. Everyone is welcome here, novice or pro. Come try something new.
2
The wine shop next door to the supper club
The local anchor
▾
A real neighbor, a real discount and a real cowboy dinner from her own events page make this the most San Angelo piece of the four. It tells locals how to book and what to bring, and it puts her on Knickerbocker Road from the first scene.
Spot B
My supper club is BYOB, and the wine shop is right next door. I don't hold a liquor license, so you bring the bottle. The shop is In Vino Veritas. Buy a bottle there the day of your reservation, tell them you're a Supper Club Guest, and you get ten percent off. Walk it over. I've got the glasses and the ice.
Spot A
We're at 2005 Knickerbocker Road. We typically seat up to thirty five. The menu is set, and it follows the season, so the only thing you have to decide is what to drink. I use as many local ingredients as I can get. In July of 2023, Wild West Fest came to town, so we threw a cowboy supper club. Dinner and dancing, if you felt like boot scootin'.
Spot C
The food travels too. I've catered a wedding at a private home in Christoval. A whiskey tasting at the San Angelo Fine Arts Museum. Want a private event? You can book it in my space on Knickerbocker, or in your own home. Weddings, private dinners, parties, whatever you're celebrating. [OWNER: the farthest you have ever driven to cater an event] I also serve the greater West Texas area. Home base is Knickerbocker Road. But if the party is at your place, I'll bring the table to you.
3
What dinner costs, and what happens if you cancel
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
Chain dining nights and grocery catering counters bury prices, sourcing and cancellation terms. Her FAQ states all three plainly, down to the Maine oysters and the 72 hour rule, and saying them out loud makes the contrast without naming anyone.
Spot C
Dinner at my supper club runs eighty five to a hundred and fifteen dollars a person. I'd rather you know the number before you ever call. Four to eight courses. The price moves because the menu moves. Some months I splurge on oysters from Maine. Or filet mignon from Belew Farms Beef. Venison from New Zealand. Halibut from Seattle.
Spot A
Seats are open to the public, first come, first served. I confirm by text a week before. There's no club fee, and you can set up a standing reservation if you want one. There is a cancellation policy, it's posted on my website, and here's why. Ingredients like that cost real money to source from those purveyors. So if you cancel with less than seventy two hours notice, you pay in full, unless I can fill your seat from the waitlist. Or send a friend in your place. That's always allowed.
Spot B
Plenty of dinners out need your table back. Mine runs about three hours, and I don't like to rush anybody. You arrive around six. The first course comes close to six thirty, once everyone is seated and has a drink. We wrap up near nine. [OWNER: the course guests still talk about] These dinners are meant to help you slow down and savor it.
4
Your first supper club, start to finish
The useful one
▾
A first timer's biggest question is how it works, and her FAQ answers it step by step. Giving that away on camera costs nothing and fills seats.
Spot A
First time at a supper club? Reserving a seat takes one message. Dates come out about once a quarter. I post them on Instagram and Facebook and on the website, and I email them to my list. Save the dates go out ahead of the menus. To grab seats, reply to the supper club email or send me a message. Give me your name, how many are coming, and a phone number. They're open to the public, first come, first served.
Spot B
That's it. There's no membership fee. If you love it, you can sign up for a standing reservation for every dinner and cancel the ones you can't make. Now, what to bring. A bottle or two, wine, beer, or liquor. I've got the glasses and the ice. If you're making cocktails, bring your own mixers. I don't charge a setup fee. If something comes up, you can send a friend in your seat.
Spot C
Plan to arrive around six. Dinner usually runs about three hours. We don't rush, because the whole point is to slow down. We wrap up near nine. [OWNER: the one thing first timers always ask you at the door] Come hungry. Come with someone you want to talk to. The room seats up to thirty five, so you'll probably meet a few new people too. Everyone's welcome. I can't wait to serve you.
