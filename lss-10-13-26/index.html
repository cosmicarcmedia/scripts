1
We were a little bit further downtown, and now we're on retail row
The origin and the face
▾
Her own words from the move make the opening line, so the first thing a viewer hears is the owner talking about the shop she grew. It also names the address, the neighbor and the hours, which is what a local shopper needs before she walks in the door.
Spot A
We were a little bit further downtown, but now we're on retail row. Ten East Concho Avenue, same block as Cowboy-Up Chocolates. We moved in the summer of 2025, and the new space is a little bigger than the old one. We've been in business for several years now. I'm Victoria Darnell, and this is Luxe Seventy Six. [OWNER: the year you opened and where the very first Luxe was]
Spot B
Here's what we carry. Women's clothing and jewelry, mostly. We try to carry different things, including plus sizes, so more women have options. Home and gift items are starting to show up now too. [OWNER: what you were doing before you opened the boutique] Some days that means a fancy schmancy dress. Some days it's a graphic tee, or a sweater when it cools off. Every piece on the rack is picked on purpose. We call it carefully curated. We believe fashion should help you feel confident, comfortable, and like yourself.
Spot C
So that's the shop. Outfits, jewelry, hats, door signs, car charms, and freshies. Freshies are the scented charms you hang in your car, and new shapes show up all the time. We're open Tuesday through Friday, ten to five thirty, and Saturday, ten to five. Closed Sunday and Monday. Can't get downtown? We ship, and orders take five to seven business days. We do gift cards too, if you don't know her size. Come on in and say hi.
2
What retail row looks like from our front door
The local anchor
▾
The block, the neighbor, the hours and the out-of-town customer all come from the shop's own pages and posts, so a San Angelo shopper sees her own downtown in it. It stays off the town's history and sticks to a Saturday on East Concho.
Spot C
Here's what retail row looks like from our front door. Ten East Concho Avenue, front and center on one of the busiest blocks downtown. Cowboy-Up Chocolates is on the same block, so you can shop, and then treat yourself. We open at ten, Tuesday through Saturday. On Saturdays we close at five, and the rest of the week it's five thirty. We're on Instagram and Facebook all the time, so you can see what's new before you come.
Spot A
Not everybody who shops with us lives here. One customer wrote that she was out of town and asked if we had a small shirt. We did. [OWNER: what sells the week a big tournament is in town] Come summer, we put out pieces for tournament weekends. New arrivals hit the floor all the time, spring dresses, fall freshie shapes, hats. There's a sale section too, in the store and online. And when something like the Stevie Distressed Mule Loafer is almost gone, we tell you.
Spot B
In May, we say thank you to teachers and nurses. Ten percent off with a valid ID, for a few days. We also team up with the other local boutiques for a Boutique Crawl. Every shop does its own thing, and you spend the day going door to door. That's what downtown shopping is supposed to feel like. [OWNER: your favorite thing that has happened in the store since the move]
3
Why we send you to the other boutiques on purpose
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
A boutique that points you to the shops next door says something a big chain rarely does. The Boutique Crawl is her own post, with the real giveaways, and it shows a shop that wants a full downtown.
Spot B
Here's something you won't see a big chain do. We send you to the shops next door. It's called a Boutique Crawl. We team up with some of the other local boutiques, and you shop your way down the street. At our door last time, the first seven customers to spend seventy five dollars got a free travel bag. The first six to spend thirty five got a goodie bag. And we had discount racks out, so there was even more reason to browse.
Spot C
Why would we point you to the competition? [OWNER: why you started doing the Boutique Crawl] Because the shops next door are what make retail row what it is. Those shops sell things a lot like ours, and that's fine with us. A full street is good for every store on it. And when you spend the day with us, we get to meet you.
Spot A
Same idea with sizes. We try to carry plus sizes, so more women have options. When somebody messages and asks if we have their size, we answer. We shop small and we sell small. Come shop your favorite looks, snag some deals, and spend the day with your favorite Luxe ladies. Follow our page and you'll see the next crawl when we post it. We can't wait to see you.
4
What's tax free on tax free weekend, and what isn't
The useful one
▾
Back to school shoppers get tripped up every August on what counts, and the Texas Comptroller's own list answers it. This one gives the answer for free and ends on a slot for the shop's own tax free weekend deal, which is where a viewer decides to walk in.
Spot A
Every August, Texas gives you one weekend with no sales tax on a lot of back to school shopping. Before you spend it, here's what counts. It runs Friday through Sunday, and the Texas Comptroller sets the rules. Certain items priced under a hundred dollars are exempt from state and local sales tax. That means clothing, shoes, school supplies, and student backpacks. Dresses, jeans, tops, sneakers. Under a hundred a piece, and the sales tax is gone.
Spot B
Here's what trips people up. Jewelry isn't on the list. Neither are handbags, purses, wallets, or watches. The Comptroller calls those accessories, and accessories stay taxable. Textbooks, computers and software don't count either. So if your outfit is a dress and a pair of earrings, the dress is tax free and the earrings are not.
Spot C
So the weekend is the time to buy the dress, the jeans, and the shoes. The Comptroller posts the exact dates each year, so check before you go. Their website has the full list of what counts. Ordering online? You have to pay for it during the weekend, but it can arrive after. [OWNER: what Luxe Seventy Six is doing for the next tax free weekend] And if you're building a whole outfit, ask what the shop can do on the rest. Come see us on East Concho, and bring your list.
