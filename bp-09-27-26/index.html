1
Six years of ministry before I ever touched a wrench
The origin and the face
▾
Puts a real person and a real reason behind the name. Right now the brand is a truck and a logo. Nobody watching knows why Alex built it this way in the first place.
Spot A
I spent six years in full time college ministry before I ever touched a pipe wrench. Discipleship, counseling, walking through problems with people. Then I sat down and made a list of what I wanted out of a career. Work with my hands. Learn on the job. Provide for my family. Have a way to mentor younger guys coming up. Plumbing hit every single one of those.
Spot B
So my wife and I started Brothers Plumbing in 2022, right here in San Angelo. The name isn't random. We believe we're all sisters and brothers, and that should shape how you run a business, not just how you treat your own family. That means the way we talk to you about a busted water heater is the same respect we'd want walking into somebody else's house.
Spot C
Every plumber on our crew is licensed, insured, background checked, and drug tested before they ever knock on your door. We give you the price before any tool comes out of the truck. Somewhere around five hundred Google reviews in, still sitting at five stars. San Angelo voted us one of the top three plumbing companies in town two years running, through the Community Choice Awards. That's not an accident. That's six years of ministry showing up in how we run a plumbing company. That's still one of the goals now. Bring guys in, teach them a trade, watch them build a life with it.
2
What West Texas water is doing to your pipes right now
The local anchor
▾
Turns a real, sourced West Texas water problem into a reason to call before something breaks, backed by this town's actual hardness numbers.
Spot B
If you live in San Angelo, your water heater is already working against you. Our water runs eight and a half to as much as fifteen grains per gallon of hardness. That's limestone country. The water picks up calcium and magnesium on its way to your house. Total dissolved solids out here run around seven hundred eighty five parts per million. The national average is about half that.
Spot C
That hardness builds up inside your pipes, your fixtures, and especially your water heater. A water heater that should last twelve to fifteen years in soft water country usually taps out around six to eight years here in San Angelo. It's the mineral content in our water working on that tank, year after year. I see it inside water heaters all over this town every week.
Spot A
So when we come out to your house, we're not just fixing today's leak. We're looking at what our specific water is doing to the rest of your system. That might mean a water softener. It might just mean checking your water heater more often than the manual says. Either way, it's a West Texas problem, and it needs a West Texas answer, not a one size fits all fix from a plumber who's never worked with water this hard.
3
The number on your invoice, decided before the toolbox opens
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
The sharpest of the four. A franchise plumber can't promise an owner operator's accountability or a flat two year warranty without calling corporate first.
Spot C
Call one of the big name plumbing franchises you see advertised on television, and the guy who shows up doesn't own the company. He's not from here, half the time. He's got a number to hit for the day, and the price he quotes you reflects that. A lot of outfits like that are subcontracting the work out to whoever's available that day. You don't know who's walking into your house.
Spot A
We're not a franchise. My wife and I own this company. Every plumber who comes to your house gives you the price before any tool comes out of the truck, so you're not signing off on a number and hoping it doesn't grow by the time he's done. Every job we do carries a two year warranty. Not ninety days. Not "ask about our extended plan." Two years, on the invoice, no asterisk. Every one of our plumbers is background checked and drug tested before we ever hand them a truck. We'll text you a photo of who's coming before they knock.
Spot B
And if something's wrong with your line, we walk you through your real options and the tradeoffs on each one, not just the option that costs the most. You decide. We're not the ones living with the choice. You are. We put on shoe covers before we walk into your house, and we clean up after ourselves when we leave. Small thing. People notice it anyway.
4
The two things to know before the first freeze hits
The useful one
▾
Costs nothing to give away and answers the single most common emergency call of the winter. Whoever remembers this in January remembers Brothers Plumbing too.
Spot A
You don't need a plumbing license to prevent the most common call we get every January. Every homeowner in West Texas should know two things before the first freeze ever hits. Where your main water shutoff is, and how to disconnect a hose from an outside spigot. That's it. Those two things prevent most of the frozen pipe disasters we get called out for every winter.
Spot B
Your main shutoff is usually near the street, in a concrete box, or inside near your water heater. Find it now, before it's thirty degrees and you're standing outside in the dark trying to figure it out for the first time. Turn it, just to be sure it isn't seized up. If it is, that's a cheap fix now instead of a five hundred dollar emergency call later.
Spot C
Disconnect every hose from every spigot before a hard freeze. Every single time. A hose left on traps water in the pipe behind it, and that water has nowhere to go when it expands. That's how a twenty dollar hose turns into a burst pipe inside your wall. We had a crew out ahead of a bad freeze last winter finishing a sewer line before the ground locked up. Same idea. Get ahead of the weather instead of reacting to it. It costs nothing to check. It costs a lot to skip it.
