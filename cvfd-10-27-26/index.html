1
From the Air Force Dental Corps to Pecos Street
The origin and the face
▾
A Lieutenant Colonel who ran dental services in Baghdad now sees patients on Pecos Street. Few patients know it, and it tells them who is behind the chair.
Spot A
When I was a kid, I built models. I helped my dad fix the family car. I was good with my hands, and I loved the medical field. Dentistry let me do both. I started at Northeast Mississippi Junior College, then finished my bachelor's degree at Ole Miss in Oxford. I earned my dental degree at the University of Mississippi in 1991.
Spot B
Right after dental school, I joined the Air Force Dental Corps. They sent me to Guam, to Germany and to Romania. I helped with relief work after Super Typhoon Paka. I deployed to Baghdad as Chief of Dental Services. [OWNER: one Air Force story you're comfortable telling on camera] I retired as a Lieutenant Colonel, with a dozen awards and decorations.
Spot C
Now I practice general dentistry here in San Angelo, at Concho Valley Family Dental. I focus on surgical extractions, and I'm certified in IV sedation. That's over thirty years of dentistry. I've been a member of the American Dental Association since 1991. My wife, Frances, and I have five kids. I played baseball at Northeast Mississippi and at Ole Miss, and I still love the game. I'm an avid golfer, and I like to fish and barbecue with my family. If you need a tooth out, or you're nervous about the chair, come and see me.

3
Why my office is out of network with every insurance company
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
The Our Office page says the practice accepts all plans but is out of network with every insurance company. Explained plainly, it tells patients they can bring any plan, with no plan or chain named.
Spot C
Our website says we accept all insurance plans. But we are out of network with every insurance company. Out of network means we aren't on an insurance company's list of contracted dentists. The Our Office page on our website says it plainly.
Spot A
When a dentist signs a contract with an insurance company, that company sets the fees the dentist agrees to accept. We don't sign those contracts. So you can bring any plan to this office. You don't have to find us on a list first. You can book with us whatever plan you carry. [OWNER: what your front desk does with a patient's insurance card, for example filing the claim]
Spot B
So what do you get from that? I'm a general dentist with a focus on surgical extractions, and I'm certified in IV sedation. Our practice is built on honesty, integrity, empathy and hard work. We treat our patients like family. [OWNER: why you chose to stay out of network] You get a plan built around your mouth and your history, and we talk it through at your exam. I was taught that the treatment plan starts with the patient. My team stays current on dental technology to make that plan the right one. If you want to know how your plan works with us, call the office at 325 387 8543. We will be glad to walk you through it.
4
Your first visit to a new dentist, step by step
The useful one
▾
New patients get nervous because they don't know what happens first. The page spells out the steps, the forms and what to bring.
Spot A
Nervous about a first visit with a new dentist? Most people are, and that's normal. Let me walk you through ours. The first visit is mostly conversation. We talk about your dental concerns, and we go over your health history. Then we do a clinical exam to set a baseline for your care. That tells us where you are today.
Spot B
We take any diagnostic images we need. Then we talk about whether you need treatment, and we explain your options. You leave knowing where you stand. The first visit is also your chance to ask us anything. Bring your insurance card and a photo ID. You can also save time by filling out your forms at home. Take your time with them. If you have a question about any form, call us.
Spot C
Download the New Patient Package from our website. It has a dental and insurance information form, a patient history form, a financial policy form, a health information release form, and a HIPAA form. There's also a COVID-19 dental treatment consent form. Hand everything to the receptionist when you check in. Our hours are Monday to Thursday, 8 to 5, and Friday by appointment. Parking is right in our lot. We're at 2102 Pecos Street, Suite 1. Book online, or call 325 387 8543.
