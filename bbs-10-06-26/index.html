2
Two of our reviews are about a deer
The local anchor
▾
Two of the shop's own testimonials come from deer strikes, and TxDOT says the rut runs October through December, which is when this video goes out. It teaches a driver what to do on the road and puts the shop in their head for the phone call after.
Spot B
Two of the reviews on our website are from people who hit a deer. That's October in San Angelo. TxDOT says Texas has more than five million whitetail deer. From October through December they're on the move, looking for mates. That's when they end up in front of your bumper. Deer seldom run alone, so if you see one, others could be nearby. Slow down when you see one near the edge of the road. At night, use your high beams when nobody's coming. They light up a deer's eyes.
Spot A
If one steps out, brake firmly and stay in your lane. Many serious crashes happen when drivers swerve to miss a deer. If you do hit one, don't touch it. A frightened, wounded deer can hurt you. Move your car off the road, call 911, and stay in the vehicle. That's all from TxDOT.
Spot C
After that, call us. One parent dropped their daughter's car off on a Monday after a deer strike. By Thursday afternoon we called to say it was finished. They'd been worried about the paint color, and they wrote that we did a phenomenal job. Another customer wrote that we made their car look brand new again after a deer. [OWNER: the worst deer wreck you've repaired, and what it took] Bring it by 617 Glenna Street. Estimates are free, and we work with every insurance company.
3
Your insurance company can't pick your body shop
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
Texas law says the driver chooses the shop, and plenty of drivers never hear it. Saying it first, then showing a named person for every carrier, makes Busker's the shop that tells you the rule and shows you who handles your claim.
Spot C
After a wreck, your insurance company might hand you a list of shops. They can hand you the list. They can't make you use it. The Texas Insurance Code says an insurer can't limit which repair shop you choose. The Texas Department of Insurance says an insurer can't tell you to use a preferred shop either. You pick where your car gets fixed.
Spot A
I'd like it to be us, and we work with every insurance company. Our website puts a name next to each one. If you're with Liberty Mutual, Safeco, State Farm or USAA, that's me. Allstate, Farm Bureau and Farmers go to Cory Flippin. Geico and Progressive go to Michael Vaughan. Everybody else goes to Zach Taylor. Their email addresses are right there too.
Spot B
So get an estimate from us, and get one from anybody else you're considering. Ours is free. Then ask each shop three things. Who do I call when something looks off after I drive away? What does the warranty cover, and for how long? Who owns the shop? Ours is a lifetime warranty, and we've been locally owned since 1970. We're an I-CAR Gold Class shop, and we work on all makes and models. Put the answers side by side.
4
That dent might not need paint
The useful one
▾
Paintless dent repair is on the shop's service list and few drivers know when it applies. A viewer who learns to check the paint before booking a repair calls the shop with the right job in mind.
Spot A
You've got a dent in your door, and you're bracing for a paint bill. What if you don't need one? Paintless dent repair fixes a dent without sanding, painting or replacing the panel. We do it here. Three of our Google reviews mention hail damage repair.
Spot C
It works when the paint is still intact. Hail, rocks and gravel, door dings from the parking lot. Large flat panels are the best candidates: hoods, trunks, doors. It handles multiple minor dings too, like a hood full of hail damage. Somewhere between eighty and ninety percent of typical dents can be fixed this way. With no paint and no new panel, the bill usually runs lower. It usually takes less time too.
Spot B
It won't work on everything. If the metal is stretched too thin, or the panel has a crease, it needs the full repair. Same if the paint is cracked in or around the dent. So look at the paint before you book anything. If it's smooth, ask for a paintless estimate. If it's cracked, you'll know why the shop wants to paint it. Our estimates are free. One customer wrote that they called the day before and we fit them in the next morning. They were in and out in under an hour.
