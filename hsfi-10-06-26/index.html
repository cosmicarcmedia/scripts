1
Three years after high school, my own agency
Origin
▾
Few readers in San Angelo would guess the agent across the desk opened her own agency three years after graduating from Eldorado High School. It is a short, specific story, and it gives a template-heavy Farmers page a young local face.
Spot A
I opened my own insurance agency three years after I graduated high school. I'm Herlinda Sanchez, and I grew up in Eldorado, Texas. Eldorado has a little over fifteen hundred people. I graduated from Eldorado High School in 2020. Two years later, I graduated from Angelo State University with a degree in international business. In June of 2023, I opened my own Farmers Insurance agency here in San Angelo.
Spot B
I chose insurance because [OWNER: the reason you went into insurance, in your own words]. Farmers has been around for nearly a hundred years, and my agency started in 2023. The company brings the history. I get to be the person you talk to when you have a question.
Spot C
My job is to help you find the coverage that fits your life, whether that's auto, home, renters, or business insurance. I want the process to be straightforward and personal. And I'd like you to walk out of my office knowing more about insurance than when you walked in. My office is at 211 Irving Street in San Angelo. I also take care of people in Eldorado, Christoval and Menard. I speak English and Spanish, so you can choose whichever one is easier. Text me, call me, or come by, and bring your questions.
2
Twenty miles to Christoval, forty five to Eldorado
Local anchor
▾
Hyperlocal beats broad in a market this size. Highway 277 runs through the exact towns she serves, and every fact here comes from the state historical handbook, so it tells small-town viewers the video was made for them and makes her the local who knows the road.
Spot B
Take Highway 277 south out of San Angelo. Twenty miles down, you're in Christoval. Forty five miles down, you're in Eldorado, where I grew up.
Spot A
Christoval is supposedly named for a man called Christopher Columbus Doty. In the 1920s, ten thousand people a year came there for a Baptist encampment on the South Concho. Eldorado sits where Highways 190 and 277 cross, and it's the county seat of Schleicher County. The county is named for Gustav Schleicher, an early surveyor, engineer and politician. It started in 1895, when a man named W. B. Silliman gave away town lots to get people to move there. By the 1940s, my hometown was the region's main wool processing center.
Spot C
Over on Highway 83, Menard sits on the San Saba River. It started out as Menardville in 1858. It's about a mile from the ruins of a Spanish presidio that was abandoned in 1758. When the first train pulled in, in 1911, the whole town boomed. I serve Christoval, Eldorado and Menard, and Miles, Ballinger and Ozona too. My office is at 211 Irving Street in San Angelo. But you can start with a call or a text, and we'll work out the rest from there. If you live out that way, I'd like to hear from you.
3
Two phone numbers on my Farmers page
Competitor contrast
▾
Every shopper eventually wants to know who they will reach. This puts the company's 800 number next to her own 325 number, which anyone can see on her Farmers page, and says what a company that old cannot do, without naming a competitor or touching a price.
Spot C
There are two phone numbers on my Farmers page. The one at the top is an 800 number for the company. The one with my name next to it starts with 325, and it's mine. You can call it, text it, or make an appointment to come in. You can even get a quote online, and a lot of people start there. There's an email button on my page too, if you'd like something in writing.
Spot A
The logo on my page says Farmers Insurance, and my name is right under it. Farmers has been around for nearly a hundred years, and my agency opened in 2023. A company that old can send you a quote and a bill. It can't sit across the desk from you and go through your policy line by line. I can, and I can do it in English or Spanish, whichever you like.
Spot B
When the quote comes up, you may have questions about what's in it. That's where I come in. Ask me what a deductible is, or what a coverage name means on your own policy. I'd like you to leave knowing more about your insurance than when we started. My office is at 211 Irving Street, and my number is on my page. Call it, text it, or stop by.
4
Which part of your car insurance handles a deer
Useful customer education
▾
Everybody who drives Highway 277 at dusk wonders what happens if a deer jumps out. This gives them the plain English version of collision, comprehensive and deductible, taken from Farmers' own pages, and sends them to their own policy for the answer, so it costs her nothing to give away.
Spot A
A deer on Highway 277 can teach you a lot about your car insurance in about one second. Collision and comprehensive are the two words people mix up. Collision is for a crash with another vehicle, whether yours was moving or parked. It's also for a crash with something like a fence, a sign or a building, and for a rollover.
Spot B
Comprehensive, or comp for short, is for damage from something other than a crash. Farmers lists fire, theft, vandalism, weather, and yes, animals. Hail goes in that group too, and out here, hail and deer both show up. Both collision and comprehensive come with a deductible. That's the amount you pay when you have a covered loss. Think of it like a copay at the doctor.
Spot C
Whether your policy has comprehensive, and what your deductible is, depends on how it was set up. Farmers says it right on its own site: details may vary by policy. So I can't tell you what yours says from here. Pull it out and look for three words: collision, comprehensive, deductible. If you can't find them, or you can't tell what they mean, text me. I'll go through it with you in English or Spanish. I'm at 211 Irving Street in San Angelo, and my number is on my page.
