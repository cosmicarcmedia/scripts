The scripts

A first pass. I made some assumptions about your business to write these. On a shoot we'd dial them in to your goals.

1
A family company in Brady, and customers who became friends
The origin and the face
▾
Hunters buy a blind from a catalog and rarely see who builds it. The About page already says it is a family owned company in Brady with customers across North America who became friends, and this puts Greg and Cheryl on camera saying so.
Spot A
We are a family owned company in Brady, Texas. We design and build custom deer blinds, deer feeders, feed bins and feed jets, right here in town. The full line is Monster Deer Blinds and stands, feeders, feed jets and fire pits. [OWNER: the year you started, and what the first blind looked like] Cheryl and I love the outdoors, and we know wildlife management. That is why we build for hunters and landowners.
Spot B
Our customers are all over North America. Many of them have become friends. [OWNER: a customer who became a friend, and how it started] As the business grew, we partnered with a key management team. They love the outdoors like we do, and they bring manufacturing and business management skills. Their job is to keep improving the product and keep us on the cutting edge.
Spot C
Our reputation means the world to us. So we start with top grade materials. We use best practices in engineering, manufacturing and assembly, and we keep working on the product and on customer service. A Feed Jet fills a feeder from the ground, through a hose and a wand, and it is built to last. The blinds, the feeders and the feed jets are made in the U.S.A., in Brady. If you have a question about any of it, call us. The sales line is on the website, 325 597 4700, and I would like to hear what you are building.
2
Brady calls itself the Heart of Texas
The local anchor
▾
Brady sits closest to the geographic center of the state, and the home page says everything is bigger in Texas. Saying so from a shop at 511 North High Street puts a small town on the map and shows how far one shop reaches through its dealers.
Spot B
Brady calls itself the Heart of Texas. The geographic center of the state is about fifteen miles northeast of town. Our website says everything is bigger in Texas, and our Monster Deer Blinds are no exception. So we build them near the middle of it. About five thousand people live in Brady, the county seat of McCulloch County. [OWNER: what building in Brady means to the business]
Spot A
From our shop on North High Street, the blinds and feeders go out to dealers. In Texas alone there are about seventy of them, from Abilene to Llano, Lampasas, Uvalde and Del Rio. One of them is right here in Brady. Some are marked as stocking dealers on our website. There are dealers in Oklahoma, Kansas, Louisiana, Mississippi, Alabama, Georgia and Florida. If you want to see a blind in person, the dealer page on our website lists where.
Spot C
And if you want it to come straight from Brady, we make it and deliver it ourselves. Blinds and feeders, anywhere in the U.S. For delivery and setup in the U.S. and Canada, call the shop. [OWNER: the farthest place you have delivered a blind] Call the sales line with your questions, 325 597 4700. If you drive in, we are at 511 North High Street. Come see us, or call, whichever is closer.
3
Who levels it and anchors it down?
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
Shipping a blind is easy. Delivery, leveling and anchoring is what a long-distance seller skips, and one customer wrote that several suppliers would not do it for two blinds in New York. His own website says turnkey setup is included.
Spot C
Ask any deer blind seller one question. Who levels it and anchors it down? Ours comes turnkey, delivery and setup, leveled off and anchored down with steel anchors. One customer wrote that we have the delivery, mounting and anchoring down pat. Another owns property in upstate New York and wanted two blinds delivered. He shopped several suppliers. None were interested in transporting and setting up a small order over that distance. He wrote that I did not hesitate.
Spot A
Then ask what the frame is made of. Ours is an all iron blind, with no wood or fiberglass to rot. The walls, the floor and the ceiling have an inch and a half of insulation. The subfloor is heavy angle iron, and the subfloor and the staircase are fully welded. One hunter wrote that his three blinds have stood through wind gusts up to seventy five miles an hour over four years. He said the steel is heavier and the foam is thicker than any others he has seen.
Spot B
They are built here in Brady, and made in the U.S.A. You can get a free quote on the website, or call. [OWNER: how long a quote takes now] If you are buying two blinds or more, or five feeders or more, ask about the package deal. The discount gets bigger as the order does.
4
How big a deer blind do you need?
The useful one
▾
Size is the first question every buyer has, and the product pages already answer it by headcount. Giving the sizes, tower height and platform choices away helps a hunter or landowner decide before the first call, and costs nothing.
Spot A
Picking a deer blind starts with how many people are going up in it. A five by five holds one hunter. A five by seven holds two. A five by nine holds three. A seven by ten holds six to eight people, a family or a whole camp. And a ten by ten gives you up to a hundred square feet of floor.
Spot B
Then decide how high. Our towers go up to twenty feet. The legs are three inch angle iron, with two inch cross members and diagonal braces. You can get a bow hunting model, a handicap accessible model, and double child safety rails. The windows are flip glass, the door has a keyed lock and a deadbolt, and the floor and the corner shelves are carpeted. The walls are seven feet tall. There is a porch the length of the blind, with handrails, and a double railed staircase with nonslip steps.
Spot C
Last, decide what it sits on. Ground models, a trailer chassis, or a four foot tower trailer. Call us with your answers. How many people, how high, and ground, tower or trailer. We will quote the blind that matches. Turnkey delivery covers leveling it off and anchoring it down. [OWNER: the size you sell the most of]
