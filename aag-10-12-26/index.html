The scripts

A first pass. I made some assumptions about your business to write these. On a shoot we'd dial them in to your goals.

1
It started as one small glass shop on a corner
The origin and the face
▾
Most people driving past know the name and nothing about the man who started it. A 1952 shop on the corner of Washington and Irving that grew into three businesses gives the person on camera a real story to stand next to, and it tells a stranger this shop has been here longer than they have.
Spot A
There's a corner in San Angelo where a glass shop got started in 1952. Washington and Irving. A man named Skinny Lupton opened a small shop there. He had a plan to take care of the glass needs of the Concho Valley. [OWNER: one line about Skinny, who he was or how he got the name] That small shop is where this company comes from.
Spot B
It didn't stay small. It grew into the place people went for plate glass and auto glass. Then in the middle of the 1970s, a building specialties shop got added. Today three businesses share that same site. Glass and mirror. Building specialties. And auto glass, which is us. We're a branch of Angelo Glass and Mirror Company, locally owned and operated. I'm [OWNER: your name and how long you've worked here].
Spot C
Our technicians are safety certified, and they average somewhere between twelve and thirty years doing this work. We replace windshields, repair rock chips, and fix car windows. The Better Business Bureau has had us accredited since 1987. Every installation we do is guaranteed. Cars have changed a lot since 1952. Now there are cameras and sensors sitting behind the windshield, and we calibrate those too. But the shop is still on Irving Street, still doing glass, more than seventy years later. If your windshield cracks, you know where we are.
2
Everything you can get done on one lot on Irving Street
The local anchor
▾
The address is the anchor here. Naming the three businesses on the corner and what each one handles tells a San Angelo driver who to call, and the free pick-up and military discount lines give a practical reason to pick up the phone today.
Spot B
A glass shop has stood on the corner of Washington and Irving since 1952. Today it's three businesses on one lot. Angelo Glass and Mirror. Angelo Building Specialties. And us, Angelo Auto Glass. The glass and mirror side handles storefronts, shower doors, and mirrors. We handle the vehicles. It's the same corner Skinny Lupton started on.
Spot A
We work on vehicles of all makes and models. Windshield replacement and repair. Rock chips. Car windows, including the ones that stick or won't roll up and down smoothly. A power window repair should only take a few hours. Heavy equipment windshields too. We put in glass from PPG, LOF, Carlite, and Mopar. We calibrate the cameras and sensors on newer cars. And we help you file the insurance claim, including the ones that come through the national networks.
Spot C
We do same day service. If you can't get the vehicle to us, pick-up and delivery is free. If you're military, ask about the discount. We're at 918 South Irving, Monday through Friday, eight to five. A neighbor on Nextdoor put it this way. Angelo Auto Glass does good work. Call them for an estimate. So that's what we'd say too. Call 325 653 4211 and tell us what you're looking at.
3
Three questions to ask before anyone touches your windshield
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
This is the one built to get forwarded. It hands a stranger three questions to take to any glass shop, and the third one, whether the quote covers camera calibration, is a question most drivers have never heard. No shop is named, and every answer comes straight from the shop's own pages.
Spot C
Three questions to ask before anybody touches your windshield. Ask us. Ask anyone. One. How long has the person doing the install been doing it? Ours are safety certified, and they average between twelve and thirty years. Two. What glass is going in? Ask for the brand by name. We carry PPG, LOF, Carlite, and Mopar, and we keep one of the largest inventories of factory windshields in the whole state.
Spot A
Three. Does the price include calibrating the camera? Newer cars have lane assist, automatic braking, cameras that look through the windshield. When the glass changes, those systems can drift. Manufacturers call calibration mandatory after a windshield replacement. Not every shop in town can do it. We do. If your car has those cameras and the quote doesn't mention calibration, ask why.
Spot B
Then ask about the claim. We work with many insurance companies, and we help you file the claim. National network claims too. Often that comes out to little or no cost to you. Every installation is guaranteed. We do same day service, and pick-up and delivery is free. And we're A plus with the Better Business Bureau. Ask your three questions anywhere you like. If any answer is a shrug, keep looking. Then come see us at 918 South Irving.
4
The credit card test for a cracked windshield
The useful one
▾
This gives a driver something to do with their hands in the first ten seconds, and every number in it comes from the shop's own FAQ and repair pages. It costs nothing to give away and puts the shop in front of the person the day the rock hits.
Spot B
Pull out a credit card. That's your first test for a cracked windshield. If the crack is smaller than the card, you might be able to get away with a repair. Two more things have to be true. It can't be in the driver's line of vision. And it can't go all the way through the glass. Pass all three and a repair might do it.
Spot C
Chips are the ones people put off. Small chip, no big deal. Then the temperature swings, or you hit a bumpy road, and it spreads. Temperature changes, vibration, and pressure can all turn a chip into a spiderweb or a long crack. Anything bigger than a quarter, take care of it right away. Fixing a chip early is a quick job. Waiting is how a small chip turns into a full replacement.
Spot A
Your windshield is part of what holds the car's structure together. That matters in an accident, so a bad crack can't sit forever. Check your insurance. Many windshield dings are covered by insurance, and we can help you file the claim. Can't tell if it's a repair or a replacement? Bring it by or call us at 325 653 4211, and we'll tell you which. We're at 918 South Irving, Monday through Friday, eight to five.
One more idea
In and out, with a clock on it

Put a timer on screen when the car pulls in and film each step of a windshield repair until the keys go back. Say the final time out loud. Dakota H.'s review, in and out to fix a cracked windshield, is the line to build around.
