From the stage to a gym for adults over forty
The origin and the face
▾

I won my weight class at my first bodybuilding contest. A friend named Ken Lain got me into the sport. I've been competing since the mid eighties. Later on I won AAU Mr. Texas. I'm Alan, co-owner of Gym 28 here in San Angelo. I've owned a gym, promoted powerlifting meets, and judged contests.
Spot B
Ken went on to set eleven world records in powerlifting. I watched what that kind of focus does for a person. [OWNER: what made you shift from competitors to everyday adults]. Today the people I coach are mostly adults over forty. Some have bad backs. Some have sore knees. One of them started at three hundred and fifteen pounds. Every one of them can get stronger.
Spot C
I believe a body is built to be active. That matters more as you get older. Full health takes an active lifestyle and good nutrition. My co-owner Ginger is a registered nurse of twenty nine years, and she coaches nutrition. Between the two of us, that's a lot of training and a lot of health. 3
Ask any gym these three things
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
The one meant to be shared. Alan's own homepage opens with it, and it gives a member who has quit before a reason to look at coaching instead of another card on the keychain.
Spot C
Most people don't need another gym membership. You probably have one now, or you had one. What you needed was a plan, a coach, and someone to hold you to it. So ask any gym three things. Who writes my plan? Who watches my form? How will I know it's working?
Spot A
Here's how we answer. Small group training is our main thing. A smaller group means the coach has time for you, to watch your form and keep you safe. Strength, progression, and accountability are built into every session. Every few weeks you can step on our InBody scanner. It prints how much muscle you carry, your body fat, and your visceral fat. You can see the trend on paper. That's the plan, the eyes on your form, and the proof.
Spot B
If you only want a place to train, we have a basic membership. Less crowded, with fewer distractions. Either way, try us before you pay. The three day pass has no obligation. A scale can't tell muscle from fat. Our scan can, and every test comes with a free consultation. So ask us the three questions. Then ask the gym down the road. We'll leave the choosing to you.
4
How to get a body scan you can trust
The useful one
▾
Free, specific help straight off Gym 28's own InBody page. A person who learns how to prepare for a scan is a person who books one.
Spot A
If you're checking your body fat, here's how to avoid fooling yourself. Don't scan right after a workout. Don't scan right after a meal. I'm Alan with Gym 28, and we run InBody scans here. These are the rules we give. First, go in on an empty stomach, or wait two or three hours after you eat.
Spot B
Second, use the bathroom first. Take off the heavy stuff, belts, wallets, jewelry. Wear light clothing. Third, keep your normal water intake. If you feel dehydrated, drink about two cups of water, forty five minutes before. A scan only takes under forty five seconds, so there's no reason to rush into it. It's a short test, and it's worth doing right.
Spot C
Fourth, repeat it the same way every time. Same time of day, same conditions. Otherwise the numbers move for reasons that have nothing to do with your body. Scan every two to four weeks and watch the trend. One exception. If you have a pacemaker or a defibrillator, skip the scan and ask your doctor. Every test here comes with a free consultation, and the scan prints a sheet you can take home. Call the gym and we'll set one up. The number is on our website.
