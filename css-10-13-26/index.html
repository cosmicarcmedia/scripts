3
Ask any bakery for its last health inspection score
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
The city of San Angelo posts every food business's health inspection score, and almost nobody checks it. This hands a customer a question that costs them nothing, answers it with the shop's own public record, and never names a competitor.
Spot A
Before you order a cake from anybody in San Angelo, ask for their last health inspection score. The city posts them for everyone, so you can look it up yourself. Ours is a hundred. The inspection was November twentieth, 2025. That's the one on the city's list from June, for 28 North Chadbourne Street. If a bakery can't tell you its score, you can still look it up. [OWNER: one thing the inspector checks that you're proud of]

Second question. Was it baked fresh, or was it frozen? Ours is Baked Fresh Never Frozen, and we put that on everything. It's in our Instagram bio, so you don't even have to ask. Third question. Can I change it? Here you can pick it apart. The Cake Bar lets you choose your slices, your filling, your frosting, and your toppings. And custom is what we specialize in. We've made a Dumb and Dumber birthday cake, and a heart cake covered in fresh flowers.
Spot C
Fourth question. Can you make it for my diet? Our page says vegan, gluten free, sugar free, and egg free. [OWNER: what you can do on a custom cake, and how much notice you need] The city lists the score, the street, and the inspection date for every food business in town. Ask those four questions anywhere you like. They take two minutes, and the first answer is a public record.
4
What to tell your baker before you order a custom cake
The useful one
▾
A customer who walks in with a date, a headcount and a picture gets a better cake, and a bakery that tells them so wins the order. It costs nothing to give away, and every example is a real cake the shop has posted.
Spot B
If you're ordering a custom cake, here's what to bring with you. Start with the date, and whether you're picking it up. Custom work takes planning, so [OWNER: how many days or weeks ahead you need an order]. Then tell us how many people you're feeding. A cake for twelve and a cake for sixty are two different cakes.
Spot C
Bring a picture, even a rough one. A Peter Rabbit cake started with a picture. So did a strawberry first birthday cake, a Dumb and Dumber birthday cake, and a vintage scroll cake in the Lambeth style. If you want fresh flowers on it, like our heart cake, say so. Tell us the colors, the theme, and the flavors you want. If it's a gift, tell us whose birthday it is and what they love. If you want cookies with names on them, like our personalized Easter cookies, spell the names out for us, one by one.
Spot A
And tell us about anyone at the party who can't eat the regular cake. Our Instagram says vegan, gluten free, sugar free, and egg free. Say so up front, and we'll tell you what's possible. Feeding a whole office or a big group? Ask about lunchboxes and grazing tables, and give us a head count there too. Everything comes out Baked Fresh Never Frozen, so the date matters most.
