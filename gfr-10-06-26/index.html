1
Two local families bought a feed store
The origin and the face
▾
The About page says two local families bought Grogan's in November 2023 and put money into the staff, but nobody on camera says who they are. This puts the owners' faces on that sentence and on the customer who wrote that the visit was nothing like the first one.
Spot A
Who buys a feed store? Two local families did, in November of 2023. [OWNER: your name, and which family you're with] Grogan's began as a family business, and it's been serving ranchers around here for years. Both families love agriculture and rural life. [OWNER: why you bought a feed store, and what you did before it]
Spot B
When we bought it, we put our money into the team that was already here. We wanted the people behind the counter to have the tools and the knowledge to help you. One customer wrote that their visit under new ownership was nothing like their first one. They said the staff was friendly and very helpful. They'll keep coming as long as their daughter has show goats. Seventeen of our Google reviews mention the friendly staff. Somebody wrote that they love the place, especially the dogs. Another customer wrote that Grogan's reminds them of how stores used to be before the big box stores moved in. That review is ten years old, and it's still what we're going for.
Spot C
Our tagline is giving you the service you deserve. We carry Purina, Cargill, Lindner and Jacoby. We're at 3744 Summer Crest Drive, with a second store in Bronte. Monday through Friday we're open seven thirty to six. Saturday we're open eight to two. [OWNER: a story from your first week as owner] Come in and tell us what you're feeding.

The local anchor
▾
Grogan's added a second store in Bronte and wrote a welcome that no one has heard in a human voice. This puts the owner's voice on it, gives Bronte its phone number, and covers the delivery area in between.

We opened a second Grogan's in Bronte. It's at 114 South State Street. We were honored to become part of such a strong and welcoming community. San Angelo is the home store, and Bronte is the new one. We have deep roots in serving rural customers. That means reliable products, friendly service, and a personal approach. Not everybody lives close to either store. Need feed delivered? We do local feed delivery. We also do bulk feed delivery for deer protein, corn and cattle cubes. If you're getting ready for hunting season, that's one less trip. If you're keeping a wildlife feeding program going, same thing. 3
We fill your feeders and close your gate
The thing the big competitor will not say
▾
The Services page says Grogan's will drive to the ranch, fill the corn and protein feeders at a hundred dollars an hour, and close any gate it opens. That price and that gate are the most specific, most local lines on the site, and they give a rancher something concrete to repeat.
Spot C
Who fills your corn feeder when you can't get out there? Ask any feed store if they'll come out to your ranch and fill your feeders. We will. It's a hundred dollars an hour. The clock starts when our driver leaves the store. It stops when they get back. We do corn feeders and protein feeders.
Spot A
Managing a ranch takes time, hard work, and plenty of trips back and forth. We handle the trip to your ranch, so that's one less thing to worry about. And we know the ranch rules. If the gate's closed when we get there, we close it behind us. We want to take the little things off your list. [OWNER: the strangest feeder or gate story from a delivery]
Spot B
We also do local feed delivery. Livestock, wildlife, or any order that needs to get from our store to your property. One customer wrote that Grogan's called them twice about a product that gave them trouble. It was almost a year after they bought it. They'd forgotten about it, and we hadn't. They wrote that our desire for integrity is top notch. Call the store at 325 227 6870 and ask us what a feeder run would cost you. We'll tell you before we leave the lot.
4
Read the feed tag before you buy the bag
The useful one
▾
Every rancher and 4-H parent buys feed, and a feed tag is the one thing on the bag that compares brands. A store that teaches how to read it, and carries four feed brands, gets the question and then the sale.
Spot A
Every bag of commercial feed has a tag, and the law requires it. When's the last time you read yours? Read it before the bag goes in the truck. Show feed, sheep and goat feed, horse feed, they all carry one. Start with the purpose statement. It tells you which animal and which class the feed is for. It's critical to check that it was made for your class of animal. [OWNER: the feed mistake you see most at the counter]
Spot C
Next comes the guaranteed analysis. It lists the nutrients the government regulates. Some of those numbers are minimums, some are maximums, and some are both. More of a nutrient isn't always better. Some have a maximum your animal can handle. If the feed has a drug in it, the tag says medicated.
Spot B
The ingredient list runs from the most to the least. So the first thing on that list is most of what's in the bag. Read the feeding directions too. The feed is made to be fed at a certain range of rates. Read the warnings last. The tag can't tell you everything, so watch how your animals look and perform on it. Bring the tag in, and we'll go through it with you. We carry Purina, Cargill, Lindner and Jacoby.
